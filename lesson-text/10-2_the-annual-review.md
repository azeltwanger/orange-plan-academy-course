# The annual review: all six areas

One thorough pass, once a year (put it in the fall, so the tax area still has time to act before year-end). Every area asks the same question: **does this decision still fit, and what would tell me if it didn't?** Bring the updated plan, last year's notes, and the checklist.

## 1 · Spending & reserve

Read the guardrail verdict (hold / raise / cut), re-center toward 80% confidence, cap moves at ±10%, adjust for inflation. Refill the reserve: fully in a held year, ~12 of 18 months in a cut year. The 18-month target never moves. Bitcoin's major drawdowns have historically recovered in about 18 months, and the reserve is sized to that stretch. Bad sign: half-target two years running.

## 2 · Allocation

Drift happens on its own. Rebalance by directing **new dollars** at what's light. Re-ask the stress question at the *new balance*: $500k → $800k means the same 75% drawdown went from $375k to $600k of paper loss. The percentage didn't move, the number nearly doubled.

## 3 · Debt

Ratios in range? Every debt still doing its job? Bitcoin-backed loans: is the cushion honest at today's price? Would a 50% drawdown force a sale?

## 4 · Tax (before Dec 31)

Harvest room · conversion room · basis still clean (no records = basis zero = tax on the whole sale) · RMD horizon. A deliberate pass is a real outcome.

## 5 · Custody

**Prove one recovery this year**: an actual restore on a spare device. Rescan for new only-ones; refresh account security.

## 6 · Estate

Beneficiaries current (the form overrides the will)? Letter still accurate? Executor still fits and willing? Switch armed with the right recipients?

## The finish line

A tick-as-you-go checklist plus a note for next year. One card from every module, once a year, each asking whether a decision still fits.

**Custody, one Bitcoin-specific addition:** re-ask the custody level question at *today's* balance. If the price ran up, your setup is guarding more money than it was designed for and you never made that decision. Closing that gap is a real candidate for one of your three actions. Also a good moment to consolidate small UTXOs on a low-fee day.

## Your decision

**Which month you run it, and what "done" looks like.**

1. **Run it before year-end.** The tax card is the only area with a hard deadline.
2. **Give each area the same short attention.** Six screens, one question each: does the decision still fit?
3. **Cap the output at three actions.** Longer lists are why people quit doing reviews.
4. **Define the finish line up front**, so it ends instead of drifting into a rebuild.

## Homework

1. Pick your annual review month, recurring on the calendar.
2. Hold the three-action cap. More than three and none of them get finished.
3. Write what "done" means: report saved, backup exported.
