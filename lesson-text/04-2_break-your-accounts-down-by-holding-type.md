# Give every account a holding mix and a time-horizon job

## Wrapper versus holding

Account names describe tax and access rules. Holdings create the actual asset exposure.

Demo investable mix:

- Bitcoin: $175,000
- Stocks: $75,000
- Bonds: $15,000
- Cash: $30,000

## Time-horizon jobs

- **Reserve:** near-term, liquid, low-volatility protection
- **Bridge:** accessible funding for goals and gaps inside roughly 4–10 years
- **Healthcare Bridge:** HSA money assigned to qualified healthcare costs before and early in retirement
- **Legacy:** long-term growth capital generally not needed for 10+ years

## Demo assignments

- Cash account: Reserve
- Taxable brokerage: Bridge
- 529: total-family college goal
- HSA: Healthcare Bridge for qualified expenses, not unrestricted spending
- Workplace and Roth accounts: Legacy
- Hardware-wallet Bitcoin: Legacy
- Exchange Bitcoin: operating balance

The account job and holding risk must agree. A volatile HSA holding does not become stable simply because the account is assigned to near-term healthcare.

## Start with dollar needs

Reserve comes from spending basis and months. Bridge comes from known costs and year-by-year retirement gaps. Healthcare Bridge comes from expected qualified medical costs. Legacy is the long-term capital remaining.

## Where the numbers come from

- **Holding mix:** every holding across every account
- **Timeframe funding:** assigned balances compared with Reserve, Bridge, healthcare, and Legacy needs

## Done when

Every account has verified holdings and a primary job that matches when and how the household expects to use it.
