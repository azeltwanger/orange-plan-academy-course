# Walkthrough: give every debt a job in Orange Plan

Everything from the debt lesson, entered. Six steps, about seven minutes.

## What you'll do

1. **Enter every debt** at **Strategy → Debt → Add debt**: balance, rate, payment, term. All four, every debt.
2. **Read the vitals strip.** Both ratios live there, current, no arithmetic needed.
3. **Read the verdict line and the stress clause** under Financial position.
4. **Give every debt a job** on the ledger, with a reason you can say out loud.
5. **Route the extra dollars** at Cash Flow → Routing, step 2.
6. **Run Review Debt Strategy** once every debt has a job.

## Two things people get wrong

**A blank rate breaks the whole lesson.** The tier is set by the rate, so no rate means no tier, which means no job. Log in and check the real number rather than the one you remember; a card that was 18% two years ago is not 18% now.

**Extra debt payments are managed on the Debt page and shown on Cash Flow.** One number, two screens. It looks like two settings and it isn't.

## What good looks like

- Every debt entered with a real, current rate
- Both ratios read, and the verdict word said out loud
- The stress clause named as the number you actually manage against
- A ceiling stated out loud. There is no field for it, which is the point
- Every row has a job, and you can give the reason for each
- No row reads **no payoff path** unless you chose that deliberately

Your mortgage sitting calmly under Low-cost on minimum payments is the whole "not all debt is the same" argument in one line. Be able to say why.

## Done when

Every debt on your balance sheet has a job with a reason, and your ceiling is a number you have said out loud.
