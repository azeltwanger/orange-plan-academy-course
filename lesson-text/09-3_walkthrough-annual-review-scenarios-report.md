# Walkthrough: annual review, scenarios, and your report

The whole maintenance module, run in the app. Filmed as one continuous session in three parts, so you can watch it straight through or take one part at a time. Set aside about 45 minutes the first time.

⚠ **Your baseline has to be real first.** Every part of this compares against your plan. Stale balances or a spending number you do not believe means you are comparing against fiction. The built-in what-ifs also read your own settings, so a wrong retirement age or state produces the wrong questions.

---

## Part A: the two rhythms

### The monthly pass (timed, honestly under five minutes)

1. **Cash Flow → This month**: read the surplus verdict; only actual changes get entered. Deficit months pause routing honestly; the pass still happened.
2. **Enter transactions**: Dashboard → Update Transactions (four paths: linked account, file, describe to AI, manual). Nothing enters the plan without your review.
3. **Explain review items (AI)**: Accounts page, when something is flagged. It explains the queue; it approves nothing.

### The annual lap (six screens, ~40 seconds each; reading, not editing)

- **Spending and reserve**: Plan → Income: Review annual update → Apply. Reserve buffer strip; refill on the rule (the target never moves).
- **Allocation**: Strategy → Allocation: Funded/Behind badges, drift band, stress question at today's balance.
- **Debt and LTV**: Strategy → Debt: both ratios, severity chips, and the drop-to-margin-call sentence.
- **Tax**: Strategy → Tax: Moves this year (harvests, conversion). Do this before year-end, while the calendar can still act.
- **Custody**: Protect: security checklist. "Full recovery process tested end-to-end" is the annual item.
- **Estate**: Protect: 5 essentials; the switch check-in is the annual proof of life.

### Close the year

1. Report → **Download PDF**, year in the filename. This is next year's before picture.
2. Settings → Data and backups → **Export Plan**: a fresh encrypted backup. The review is not done until both files are on disk.
3. **Schedule both rhythms** in your calendar (there is no in-app scheduler): a monthly day and November, both recurring.

**Output: 1 to 3 actions**, finishable before the next monthly pass. Longer lists are how reviews stop happening.

## Part B: build and compare a scenario

### The page

**Scenarios** (primary nav). Three zones: **What if...** (built-in presets), **Your scenarios** (*"Saved what-ifs you can reopen and compare"*), and the comparison section below. Top right: **Custom scenario**, plus **Review scenario** once a scenario is selected.

### 1. Create the stress test

**What if... → 50% drawdown after retirement.** One click creates and selects it; the plan re-runs. The description fills in your own retirement age.

The grid shows 4 of 9 presets. **See more scenarios** expands to all of them: Bear Market, 50% drawdown after retirement, Retire earlier, Spend less in retirement, Move to no-tax state, Conservative BTC returns, Moderate BTC returns, Claim Social Security at 62, Claim Social Security at 70.

### 2. Read the comparison

The chip shows the scenario name, then **vs your plan**.

- **Headline row:** Retire at, Spending, Success rate
- **Full comparison metrics** (expand): net worth at retirement, net worth at age 90, lifetime taxes paid, effective tax rate, withdrawal rate at retirement, depletion age, earliest retirement age, BTC at retirement and at life expectancy

Each row shows baseline, scenario, and difference. Matching rows read **no change**. Ask a stress test exactly one question: **would I still be okay?**

### 3. Run the Monte Carlo

Button **Run Monte Carlo** in the comparison section.

⚠ Until it runs, the success rate is not a real comparison. The chart updates instantly off the projection, but confidence is a simulation that has to execute.

### 4. Build a custom scenario

**Custom scenario** → **Create scenario** dialog.

1. **Step 1: Choose scenario type.** Seven cards: Life events, Retirement, Savings, Debt, Tax, Markets, Advanced. ⚠ Pick the specific type, not Advanced.
2. **Name and description.** The placeholder changes with the type. Name it after the *question*, not the answer: "Retire at 55" works, "Better plan" does not.
3. **Change one variable.** Save.

### 5. Compare, then manage

Select it under **Your scenarios**, read the headline row, expand full metrics, run the Monte Carlo.

The **⋯** menu gives **Duplicate**, **Edit**, **Delete**. Duplicate is how you build a second scenario differing by exactly one thing without rebuilding it.

⚠ Nothing on this page touches your plan, which is exactly why you can experiment freely.

### 6. Review scenario (AI)

Only renders with a scenario selected and the editor closed. It reads your saved scenarios, projection years, life events, and assumptions, then asks which scenario to compare against your plan. Run it when a comparison needs explaining to someone else, or when you cannot tell why two scenarios differ. It explains and compares; it does not decide.

Your two saved scenarios also turn on the report's "If Bitcoin follows a different path" section, which does not render without at least one.

## Part C: walk the report and save the PDF

⚠ The reading order is not the page order. The report leads with trajectory; position is section five. Jump deliberately, in planner order: **position → trajectory → risk → actions.**

1. **Open the report**: Account menu → Report. (The button is "Download PDF"; it fires the print dialog underneath.)
2. **Trajectory** (section 1): the ring and the date together. Green at 80+ is the zone; 100 was never the goal.
3. **Position** (jump to section 5): is this true? A stale balance means stop, fix at the source, regenerate. Do not read around a wrong number.
4. **Spending** (sections 3 and 4): the band renders as Lean decade / Your plan / Strong decade, which are the floor, plan, and ceiling from the course.
5. **Risk** (section 2 plus the Bitcoin-path section): ask only "would I still be okay?"
6. **Tax roadmap and allocation** (8 and 6): this is the page to bring your CPA. The BTC-loan gauge here is the one place "Cushion" appears.
7. **Protection** (7): the scoreboard for custody and estate. "Insurance and access" tracks beneficiary and executor items, not policies.
8. **Actions and assumptions** (10 and 11): three actions, not thirty. An assumption you cannot defend gets changed and the report regenerated.
9. **Save the PDF**: year in the filename, e.g. `orangeplan-report-2027.pdf`.

---

## Done when

- The monthly pass came in under five minutes, and you can name what you never opened
- All six annual areas read, with a decision or a deliberate pass on each
- One recovery proven, and a letter your family could act on today
- Two scenarios saved (one stress test, one choice), both Monte Carlo'd, baseline untouched
- Position matches reality, date and ring read as a pair, all three band labels explained
- Next steps ≤ 3, every assumption defensible
- The PDF and a fresh encrypted backup are both on disk, with the year in the filename

One report per year. Five years of before-pictures show you what actually moved and what only felt like it did.
