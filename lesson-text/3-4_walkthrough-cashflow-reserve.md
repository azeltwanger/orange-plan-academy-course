# Walkthrough: build cash flow and reserve

Follow along with the video (~15 minutes). Have 2–3 months of transactions available and your bare-bones number written down.

1. **Read the verdict** — Cash Flow → This month. A deficit isn't failure; this module is the fix.
2. **Map your income** — every stream separately; tax-free items go under Tax-free income.
3. **Sort your spending** — ⚠ your spending number goes in the **Living** row. Not your paycheck, not debt payments.
4. **Verify spending** — walk 2–3 real months; exclude one-timers (don't delete them).
5. **Set the reserve fields** — basis = **Bare-bones**, fill the floor number first, pick target months, set the monthly build cap.
6. **Read the target in the waterfall** — step 1 shows "$X of $Y · full in N mo."
7. **Shield the reserve holding** — the exact holding, not the whole account.
8. **Read the waterfall order** — Reserve → Extra debt → Contributions (extra-debt amounts live on the Debt page).
9. **AI review: Cash Flow & Reserve** — it reviews and explains; it doesn't decide.
10. **AI review: spending calibration** — from Accounts → Spending tab, when synced spending disagrees with your Living number.

**Done when:** positive surplus, every stream listed, Living ≠ paycheck, reserve basis on Bare-bones with no warning, shield on, reserve first in the waterfall.
