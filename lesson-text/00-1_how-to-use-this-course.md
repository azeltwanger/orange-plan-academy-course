# How to use this course

Orange Plan calculates the financial plan. The Academy teaches what the numbers mean, the decisions the app cannot make, and how those decisions become a usable Bitcoin financial plan.

## What Core builds

The learner should be able to explain:

- Current assets, debts, income, and spending
- Assumptions and confidence target
- Earliest date reaching the target
- Cash reserve and debt strategy
- Bitcoin allocation and next-dollar route
- Tax records and planning windows
- Retirement spending and funding
- Custody and family recovery
- Estate, beneficiary, and protection status
- Review cadence and current actions

For every important number, ask:

1. What does it mean?
2. What is it calculated from?
3. Where is the source edited?
4. What else does it affect?

## Four stages

1. **Build the facts** — baseline, life events, assumptions, confidence
2. **Create capacity** — Cash Flow, Reserve, Debt
3. **Direct the money** — Allocation, Tax, Retirement Income
4. **Protect and maintain it** — Custody, Estate, Insurance, Scenarios, Report

## Core versus Advanced

Core is required to build a usable plan. Advanced is conditional and includes topics such as Bitcoin-backed loans, conversion sizing, harvesting, healthcare bridges, complex custody, and trusts.

Skip an Advanced lesson when its opening condition does not apply.

## Lessons, walkthroughs, and Build Your Plan

Concept lessons use the clearest educational order. Future walkthroughs track the relevant Build Your Plan work using one continuous demo household.

An app checkmark means required data exists. Educational completion means the learner also made and can explain the planning decision.

## First-plan accuracy

Use the best supported number and label it verified, estimated, or missing. Do not invent an input to improve an output.

## Security rule

Never enter a seed phrase, private key, passphrase, PIN, password, or wallet backup into Orange Plan, a document, photo, email, or AI tool.

## Scope

US tax, benefit, and legal sections use US rules. Learners elsewhere should map them with qualified local professionals.

## Done when

The learner understands the role of the app, course, walkthroughs, and the no-secrets rule.
