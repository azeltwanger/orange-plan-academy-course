# Walkthrough: walk your report in Orange Plan

Follow along (~10 minutes). Have the plan warm, at least one saved scenario, and a fresh confidence check run; three report sections are conditional on them.

⚠ The reading order isn't the page order: the report leads with trajectory; position is section five. Jump deliberately, planner order: **position → trajectory → risk → actions.**

1. **Open the report**: Account menu → Report. (The button is "Download PDF"; it fires the print dialog underneath.)
2. **Trajectory** (section 1): the ring and the date together. Green at 80+ is the zone; 100 was never the goal.
3. **Position** (jump to section 5): is this true? A stale balance means stop, fix at the source, regenerate. Don't read around a wrong number.
4. **Spending** (sections 3–4): the band renders as Lean decade / Your plan / Strong decade; those are the floor / plan / ceiling from the course.
5. **Risk** (section 2 + the Bitcoin-path section): ask only "would I still be okay?" No saved scenario = no path section.
6. **Tax roadmap + allocation** (8 and 6): this is the page to bring your CPA. The BTC-loan gauge here is the one place "Cushion" appears.
7. **Protection** (7): the scoreboard for custody + estate. "Insurance and access" tracks beneficiary/executor items, not policies.
8. **Actions + assumptions** (10–11): three actions, not thirty; an assumption you can't defend gets changed and the report regenerated.
9. **Review scenario (AI)**: Scenarios → saved scenario → Review scenario, when a path needs plain English before handing the doc over.
10. **Save the PDF**: year in the filename (e.g. `orangeplan-report-2027.pdf`).

**Done when:** position matches reality, date + ring read as a pair, all three band labels explained, "would I still be okay?" answered out loud, next steps ≤ 3, and the PDF is on disk. One report per year: five years of before-pictures show what actually moved.
