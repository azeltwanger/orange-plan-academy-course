# How the AI works: what it reads and what it never needs

## App versus AI

- **Planning engine:** calculates the plan
- **AI:** explains results, traces sources, compares options, identifies missing inputs, and helps prepare professional questions

The AI should not replace the app calculation with a second set of guessed numbers.

## Ask from the relevant area

Use the review attached to the planning area when available. A useful answer points to the displayed result and the source input behind it.

## Never enter secrets

Do not provide:

- Seed phrase or wallet backup
- Private key or passphrase
- PIN or password
- Full account number
- Social Security number
- One-time authentication code

A custody review does not need the thing that moves the Bitcoin.

## It explains; the household decides

AI can show the financial effect and trade-off. It does not know the household's emotional tolerance, family capability, or priorities.

Verify changing tax, legal, insurance, custody-provider, and lending rules with the appropriate professional.

## Optional memory

Memory may hold planning preferences. It should not hold balances, credentials, account numbers, recovery information, or secrets.

## Three checks

1. Does the answer point to an app number?
2. Can the number be traced to its source?
3. Did the answer explain the trade-off and leave the decision with the household?

## Done when

The learner understands that the app calculates, AI explains, and no Bitcoin secret or account credential belongs in an AI tool.
