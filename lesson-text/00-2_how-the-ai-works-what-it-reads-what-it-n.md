# How to use Orange Plan AI

Orange Plan AI is most useful when you open the workflow attached to the decision you are making.

## Start in Plan Guide

Click **AI Review** in the top bar. The first action changes with the page you are on, and the conversation stays open as you move around the app.

Useful requests include:

- Review my full plan and show me the top three issues.
- What changed after I updated these numbers?
- What information is missing or stale?
- Compare these choices and explain the trade-offs.
- What should I model before I speak with a professional?

The app computes the numbers. The AI reads and explains them.

## Daily Bitcoin report

Under **Market brief**, click **Run daily Bitcoin report**. It checks price context, ETF flows, public-company Bitcoin activity, on-chain data, leverage, macro, and material industry news. It ends with what to ignore, what to watch next, and a short read on whether the day changes anything in your plan.

Its job is to replace doomscrolling, not produce a trading signal.

## Use another AI

Go to **Settings → Data & Privacy → Data & backups → AI Strategy Review Export**. **Download AI Review Markdown** creates a privacy-scrubbed file for ChatGPT, Claude, or another AI. Review it before uploading. The full JSON option is available for structured analysis.

This is not the encrypted restore backup.

## The one rule

Never put a seed phrase, private key, wallet backup, passphrase, PIN, password, full account number, Social Security number, or backup-file passphrase into any AI.

## You are done when

You have run one useful review, know where the daily report and export live, and can say the no-secrets rule from memory.
