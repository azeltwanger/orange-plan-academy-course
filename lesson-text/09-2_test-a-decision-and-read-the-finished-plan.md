# Test a decision and read the finished plan

## Begin with one question

Use Scenarios to ask what happens if one decision or condition changes without altering the baseline.

## Change one primary lever first

Test retirement age, spending, return assumptions, state, debt, allocation, contribution route, or borrowing separately before combining them.

## Stress test versus choice

- **Stress test:** would the plan remain workable under an unfavorable condition?
- **Choice:** is a deliberate action worth its benefit, cost, and new risk?

## Five-step comparison

1. Confirm exactly what changed.
2. Read planned age, confidence target, confidence result, and earliest qualifying date together.
3. Review cash flow, tax, contributions, and withdrawals.
4. Review accounts, Bitcoin sold or retained, collateral, and debt.
5. Review ending assets, estate, reserve, and the risk carrying the result.

Do not reorganize the plan around a small difference that does not change what the household would do.

## Move a decision to its source

After the household decides, update the real Plan, Cash Flow, Debt, Allocation, Income, or life-event input. Do not leave a real decision only inside Scenarios.

## Recurring stress set

- Weaker Bitcoin returns
- Major drawdown near retirement
- Higher spending or major cost when relevant
- Loan liquidation risk when a Bitcoin-backed loan exists

Rerun after the annual baseline update.

## Read the report in order

1. Inputs and assumptions
2. Current position
3. Retirement result
4. Cash Flow and Reserve
5. Debt and Allocation
6. Tax and Retirement Income
7. Protect
8. Actions

## Six-sentence summary

Explain:

1. Current position
2. Cost of normal life and monthly capacity
3. Retirement timing, confidence target, and supported spending
4. Next-dollar route and drawdown impact
5. Retirement funding and unresolved professional questions
6. Family recovery and inheritance starting point

## Scenario provenance

- **What it means:** difference from the baseline
- **Calculated from:** the same projection with Scenario overrides
- **Edit source:** Scenario definition until the decision updates the baseline source
- **This affects:** retirement, cash flow, tax, holdings, debt, and estate comparisons

## Done when

The household can explain every saved Scenario, summarize the plan in six sentences, and identify one to three next actions.
