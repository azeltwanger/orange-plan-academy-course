# Check your work (estate)

The read-back on Protect plus your recorded decisions. No new inputs.

## Record (off-app)

- Executor + backup: asked and accepted
- Estate level 1–3 from the self-triage
- Access-split status: set / tested / not yet
- Insurance gaps from the Coverage Audit worksheet
- Trust gate lit? The five attorney questions go to the interview

## The lap

- Readiness reads higher than at the start — letter, switch, beneficiaries green
- Letter completeness: zero flags (Added / Named / Written / Yes)
- Switch armed, right recipients, next check-in visible
- Beneficiary allocations don't contradict the will or policies — the form wins
- Nothing secret typed anywhere; document locations only
- You read **After-tax estate**, not gross — it's the number that reaches heirs
- Both growth cases read; you can name which side of the line each lands on
- Default outcome is "do nothing" — most households are under the line
- Both decisions written down: backstop status + estate-tax outcome

Everything built here — letter, switch, beneficiaries, custody map, tax number — lands on next module's annual lap.
