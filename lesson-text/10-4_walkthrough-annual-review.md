# Walkthrough: run the annual review in Orange Plan

Follow along (~20 minutes first time). Both rhythms, on your plan.

## The monthly pass (timed — honestly under five minutes)

1. **Cash Flow → This month** — read the surplus verdict; only actual changes get entered. Deficit months pause routing honestly; the pass still happened.
2. **Enter transactions** — Dashboard → Update Transactions (four paths: linked account / file / describe to AI / manual). Nothing enters the plan without your review.
3. **Explain review items (AI)** — Accounts page, when something's flagged. It explains the queue; it approves nothing.

## The annual lap (six screens, ~40 seconds each — reading, not editing)

- **Spending & Reserve** — Plan → Income: Review annual update → Apply. Reserve buffer strip; refill on the rule (target never moves).
- **Allocation** — Strategy → Allocation: Funded/Behind badges, drift band, stress question at today's balance.
- **Debt & LTV** — Strategy → Debt: both ratios, severity chips and the drop-to-margin-call sentence.
- **Tax** — Strategy → Tax: Moves this year (harvests, conversion) — before year-end, while the calendar can act.
- **Custody** — Protect: security checklist; "Full recovery process tested end-to-end" is the annual item.
- **Estate** — Protect: 5 essentials; the switch check-in is the annual proof of life.

## Close the year

1. Report → **Download PDF**, year in the filename — next year's before picture.
2. Settings → Data & backups → **Export Plan** — fresh encrypted backup. The review isn't done until both files are on disk.
3. **Schedule both rhythms** in your calendar (no in-app scheduler): a monthly day and an annual month, recurring.

**Output: 1–3 actions**, finishable before the next monthly pass. Longer lists are how reviews stop happening.
