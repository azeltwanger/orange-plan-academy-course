# Walkthrough: build the paycheck in Orange Plan

<!-- PLAN-LIFECYCLE:LESSON-TEXT -->

Retirement spending, Social Security, and the withdrawal order are entered and applied here, not during onboarding or Foundation.


Follow along (~20 minutes). Requires the plan in the retirement phase; run the Monte Carlo once first.

1. **Confirm spending**: Plan → Retirement → Baseline spending ($/yr; the only inline edit point). Healthcare + lumpy in, debt payments out.
2. **Healthcare bridge**: Life events → Expense Change, duration = years to 65.
3. **Build the floor**: Settings → Planning profile → Your SS Benefit. ⚠ **monthly** ($4,300, not $51,600). The floor renders on the income chart, not as a panel.
4. **See the gap**: Income Blueprint → year drawer + the Gap column. Count bridge years × early gap.
5. **Withdrawal order**: read the live outcome strip (Bitcoin at LE · After-tax NW · Lifetime taxes). Set **both** orders: which accounts, and what sells inside them. Bracket-fill lives in Custom phases → Bracket-aware. Apply to plan when you can say why.
6. **Calibrate the operating plan**: What you can spend: 95 / 80 / 60 stops, save your starting target (retirement phase only). Stops = the target; guardrails = the annual triggers around it.
7. **Annual update**: Review annual update → Prior / After inflation / 80% target / Saved target → Apply. The 10% cap is on the *real* move.
8. **AI review: income plan**: after the order and guardrails are set.
9. **Retirement Borrowing**: Bracket-aware / Borrow-first / Custom phases vs the sell-only column. Sandbox until Apply.
10. **AI review: borrowing**: only if borrowing is actually in the plan.
11. **Pressure-test**: Scenarios → 50% drawdown after retirement.

**Done when:** you can say spending / floor / gap from memory, the Bridge bucket matches the bridge price, confidence sits near 80 after saving, an applied withdrawal order you can defend, and the drawdown scenario holds with a guardrail-sized adjustment.
