# Optional: build the college funding stack

Use this lesson only when you expect to help fund education for a child, grandchild, or another family member.

## Decide what you are promising

Do not begin with the largest four-year sticker price. Choose the family commitment first.

Examples:

- Four years of in-state tuition
- A fixed dollar amount
- The current 529 plus an annual amount from cash flow
- A percentage of the final net cost
- A shared plan that includes student work, aid, or limited borrowing

## Build the funding stack

Possible sources include:

- Existing 529 balance
- New 529 contributions
- Parent cash flow during enrollment
- Scholarships and grants
- Student work or family support
- Planned taxable or Bitcoin sales
- Deliberately accepted borrowing

The amount to pre-fund is the commitment after subtracting other credible sources.

### Example

Family commitment: **$80,000**

- Existing 529: **$25,000**
- Parent cash flow during college: **$20,000**
- Student contribution, aid, or defined borrowing: **$10,000**
- Remaining amount to accumulate or fund from assets: **$25,000**

The target is a complete $80,000 funding stack, not necessarily an $80,000 529 balance.

## Reduce volatility as the date approaches

- **7–10+ years away:** keep a reasonable contribution while the broader plan continues
- **3–7 years away:** update likely cost and begin protecting part of the commitment
- **1–3 years away:** give the first year a reliable source that does not depend on Bitcoin's price
- **During enrollment:** rebuild the remaining plan from actual cost and aid information each year

## Where this number comes from

- **What it means:** the part of the family commitment still needing a source
- **Calculated from:** commitment minus savings, cash flow, aid, student contribution, and accepted borrowing
- **Edit source:** college life event, education holding, and contribution route
- **This affects:** contributions, Bridge funding, taxable withdrawals, taxes, confidence, and the earliest retirement date

## Your decision

Choose the family commitment and identify the sources that will cover it.

## Done when

The funding stack adds up to the commitment and the amount due soon is protected from a forced Bitcoin sale.
