# Walkthrough: Protect, the heir letter, and the switch

The whole estate module, run in the app. Filmed as one continuous session in seven chapters, so you can watch it straight through or take one chapter at a time. Set aside about 45 minutes the first time.

⚠ **Storage mode must be Cloud**, or the switch cannot arm and chapter E has nothing to show. In Local Only the panel reads *"Automatic check-in emails require cloud sync."*

## A. Protect, and what "ready" means

**Protect** (primary nav). The readiness bar has five segments: **Dead man's switch · Heir letter · Protection tier · Checklist · Beneficiaries**. It measures whether your family could act, not how much you own.

Below it, **Needs attention** gives one row per incomplete essential, each with one action. That queue is the method: it hands you the next thing so you are never choosing between nine.

⚠ Two different "checklist" strings live on Protect. The readiness bar says **Checklist**; the Needs attention row says **Security checklist**. Both are real.

## B. Beneficiaries and the executor

**Protect → Needs attention → Beneficiaries → Assign.** Each name, relationship, allocation percentage, and approximate dollars of the estate.

⚠ **For accounts with a valid beneficiary, POD, or TOD designation, the provider's form generally controls instead of the will**, and insurance works the same way. Orange Plan holds the *intent*; you mirror it on each account's own designation, because **the institution holds the binding record**. The app is your checklist, not the legal fact — verify each account with the provider and your attorney.

The executor step happens off-app, with the actual person, using the printed **Executor Packet**: their role, their first week, the team, the Bitcoin process, asset handling, and the acceptance page you both sign. An executor who signed it has been briefed. One who did not, has not.

## C. Confirm the access design you already built

Nothing new is decided here. Read your **Family Custody Map** and answer both tests out loud:

1. Can one person spend alone?
2. Can one lost copy, or one unreachable person, permanently stop recovery?

⚠ **Say the answer you do not like.** A Level 2 design fails test 1. At Level 1, this chapter is different: your dual control is delegated, so the action is verifying **the institution's death-claim procedure**, because that procedure is the plan.

## D. The heir letter

**Protect → Needs attention → Heir letter → Finish.** Four status rows: Emergency contacts · Executor · Letter content · PDF saved.

⚠ **The executor you actually asked** goes in here. If the conversation has not happened, do not fill the row yet.

**Edit heir letter** opens the dialog. The red banner is the rule: *"NEVER include seed phrases, private keys, passwords, or PINs in this letter."* Three sections: **Key contacts** (the professionals), **Where to begin** (where the *documents* live, never the keys), and **Your letter** (where your first two lines go).

⚠ **Key contacts are not emergency contacts.** Emergency contacts are the switch's recipients. Both lists are required.

⚠ **"Letter content" stays Missing until you customize it.** Template text does not count. That is intentional, not a bug.

**Draft with AI** exists for when the blank page is what is stopping you. It drafts the inventory and the call order, never the secrets. The first two lines are still yours.

Finish with **Download PDF**, and store it separately from your seeds and hardware wallets.

## E. Arm the switch, and test it

**Protect → Needs attention → Dead man's switch → Turn on.** Default check-in is 90 days. It delivers the letter and nothing else.

⚠ **Tell the recipients in advance.** A message arriving out of nowhere reads like a scam, and the first instinct is to ignore it. That is a phone call today, not a setting.

## F. Projected legacy and the estate summary

**Protect → Projected legacy → Projected estate after debt**, then **See details →** for the after-tax number.

⚠ There is no "estate value to heirs" figure on the retirement pages; the family-facing number is **After-tax net worth** in the Income tab. This is the real estate surface.

⚠ The federal exemption comparison is **not filing-status aware**: it applies one flat exemption regardless of married or single.

Finish with **Download estate summary** and **Protection tier → Mark as reviewed**.

## G. The trust gate (optional)

Most households stop above this. A will, current beneficiary forms, a briefed executor, a letter, and an armed switch is a complete estate plan for the large majority.

If the gate does light up, go to **Advanced Estate Planning → "Do you need a trust, and which one?"** for the prudent-investor duty and the waiver that stops a trustee being obliged to sell the exact asset the trust was built to hold.

## You are done when

- The Needs attention queue is shorter than when you started
- Beneficiaries are assigned in the app **and** on the custodian's own form
- Your executor has been asked out loud and signed the acceptance page
- Both access tests are answered aloud, including the one your design fails
- The heir letter is written in your own words, the PDF is saved, stored away from seeds and devices
- The switch is armed and its recipients have been told
- The estate summary is downloaded and the tier marked reviewed
- The trust gate has been run once and given an actual answer
