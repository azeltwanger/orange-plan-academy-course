# Harvest gains or losses only when the opportunity is real

> **Watch this only when supported taxable lots contain a meaningful current opportunity.**

## Loss example

- Current value: $25,000
- Basis: $40,000
- Unrealized loss: $15,000

A realized loss may offset gains or create a carryforward under current rules. Its usefulness depends on the household's actual return and future gains.

## Exposure after the sale

The tax action and investment decision are separate. Verify current repurchase rules for direct Bitcoin, ETFs, related parties, and the exact transaction.

## Gain example

- Current value: $30,000
- Basis: $10,000
- Long-term gain: $20,000

A lower-income year may allow a favorable gain realization and higher basis, but taxable income, state tax, NIIT, healthcare subsidies, and Social Security interactions matter.

## Compare three paths

- no action
- proposed amount
- smaller amount

Review current tax, remaining basis, carryforwards, exposure, friction, and the future sale the strategy is meant to improve.

## Identification

The real lot sold must match the planned lot and current documentation rules.

## Done when

The household can name the lot, amount, current effect, future purpose, and exposure plan.
