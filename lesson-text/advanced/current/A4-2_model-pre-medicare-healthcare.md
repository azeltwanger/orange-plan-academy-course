# Model pre-Medicare healthcare and income interactions

> **Watch this only when retirement begins before Medicare without durable coverage.**

## Price current options

Use household-specific quotes for spouse/employer coverage, COBRA, Marketplace, private coverage, or another arrangement genuinely available.

A health-sharing membership is not insurance and requires separate review of payment guarantees, exclusions, legal protection, HSA treatment, and reserves.

## Use full cost

Include:

- premium or membership,
- deductible / member responsibility,
- expected out-of-pocket spending,
- maximum or non-guaranteed exposure,
- network and prescription restrictions,
- and reserve.

## MAGI interaction

Traditional withdrawals, conversions, interest, dividends, gains, wages, and other income can affect Marketplace savings.

Spendable cash and MAGI are not the same number. Model the healthcare and tax decision together.

## Worked example

Assume the current quote shows:

- $12,000 annual net premium
- $6,000 expected out-of-pocket spending
- $18,000 first-year planning cost

If a proposed conversion raises the quote by $5,000, include that amount with the conversion tax comparison. Replace all figures with the current household quote.

## HSA

Use supported qualified expenses and preserve receipts. Not every premium or health-related payment is automatically eligible.

## End date

Model the bridge until Medicare eligibility, then replace it with the accepted Medicare coverage cost and enrollment plan.

## Done when

The household has a current coverage choice, full annual cost, MAGI sensitivity, funding source, and Medicare transition date.
