# Build the loan survival and repayment plan

> **Watch this only when a Bitcoin-backed loan remains under consideration.**

## Four possible responses

- Add collateral
- Pay down the balance
- Refinance or close
- Accept contractual liquidation

The agreement controls timing and available options.

## Top-up example

For a $30,000 balance at $30,769/BTC:

- Existing collateral: 1.50 BTC
- Target after action: 50% LTV
- Required total collateral: about 1.95 BTC
- Required top-up: about 0.45 BTC

At $25,000/BTC, the same target requires 2.40 BTC total. A limited reserve can be exhausted.

## Paydown example

At the 65% action line, reducing the same position to 50% LTV requires about $6,923 of cash.

Loan survival money should not be silently double-counted as emergency, tax, or spending cash.

## Written ladder

- Review line
- Action line
- Hard exit line
- Contract liquidation outcome

Use the real agreement and household policy.

## Repayment

Name the primary and backup repayment source. A plan that only works when Bitcoin rises is not a repayment plan.

## Interest and estate

Paid versus capitalized interest changes cash flow and LTV. The spouse or estate process must know how to identify, service, repay, or close the loan without exposing Bitcoin secrets.

## Done when

Every line has a written response, sufficient named resources, and a repayment/exit source.
