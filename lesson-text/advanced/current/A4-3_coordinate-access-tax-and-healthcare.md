# Test account access, tax, and healthcare together

> **Watch this only when the funding source changes the pre-Medicare healthcare result.**

## Same cash need

Compare each strategy against the same $100,000 of spendable cash.

## Three illustrative paths

### Taxable first

- $30,000 cash
- $70,000 taxable sale
- $25,000 supported gain
- Illustrative net premium: $8,000

### Add a $40,000 Roth conversion

- Same spending sources
- $40,000 additional ordinary income
- Illustrative net premium: $13,000
- Possible future traditional-balance benefit

### Use $40,000 from an eligible employer plan

- $30,000 cash
- $40,000 traditional distribution
- $30,000 taxable sale
- Illustrative net premium: $15,000
- Less taxable Bridge used, more current ordinary income

Replace all tax and premium figures with current household results.

## Review together

- current tax
- healthcare premium / credit
- taxable Bridge used
- remaining basis
- future traditional and Roth balances
- HSA opportunity
- later Medicare-related costs
- asset sold in a drawdown

## Annual range

Set a range for taxable funding, ordinary income, conversions, tax-payment cash, and the backup source. Recheck annually.

## Done when

The chosen mix funds the same lifestyle, includes healthcare and tax, and preserves a workable Bridge.
