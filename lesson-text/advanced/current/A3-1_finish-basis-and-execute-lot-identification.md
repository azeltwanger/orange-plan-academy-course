## Reconcile quantity first

Explain every unit through acquisitions, transfers, fees, gifts, and dispositions before attaching a final basis.

## Reconstruct the history

For each lot, preserve date/time, quantity, value, fees, original location, and the transfer chain to the current account or wallet.

Prior filed treatment matters. Do not change which lot supplied an old sale merely because another method is better today.

## Unknown is not automatically zero

Zero basis can be a labelled stress assumption. It is not automatically the filing position.

## Execute the same lot the app models

Use the current broker or non-broker identification and documentation rules. Choose the lot on time, preserve the evidence, and make the tax record match the disposition.

## Sale packet

- account or wallet
- quantity
- intended lots
- acquisition records and basis
- required identification evidence
- transaction confirmation and ID
- proceeds and fees
- CPA handoff

## Number provenance

- **Basis-covered quantity:** reconciled units supported by complete records
- **Selected-lot gain:** proceeds minus basis in the units actually identified

## Done when

The quantity reconciles, known lots are defensible, unresolved units remain visible, and the modeled sale can be executed and documented.
