# Choose collaborative custody or DIY multisig

> **Watch this only when one-key risk is unacceptable and the household can maintain a multi-key wallet.**

## 2-of-3

Three keys exist and any two sign. It can survive one lost key and prevent one-key spending when the full design supports those claims.

Configuration records, compatible software, distribution, and human capability still matter.

## Collaborative custody

Possible benefits:

- separately managed key,
- setup and transaction help,
- configuration backup,
- recovery and inheritance guidance.

Costs include fees, privacy, identity, provider policy, software, and long-term service dependence.

Verify provider-independent recovery.

## DIY multisig

Possible benefits:

- privacy,
- independence,
- full household control.

The household owns all device, backup, descriptor, software, migration, privacy, and heir-support work.

## Demo judgment

Alex's technical ability is not enough. Jordan's ability to recover without Alex is part of the decision.

Collaborative may be worth the fee when independent recovery works and guided family support is valuable. Tested single signature may still be safer than unproven multisig.

## Done when

The threshold works, one specified failure is survivable, and family recovery does not depend on the technical owner or provider remaining available.
