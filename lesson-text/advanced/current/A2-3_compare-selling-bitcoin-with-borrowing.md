# Compare selling Bitcoin with borrowing against it

> **Watch this only when both strategies are being considered for the same real cash need.**

## Same need, same date

Compare equal proceeds. Do not compare a smaller sale with a larger loan.

## Sale example

- Cash need: $30,000
- Bitcoin price: $100,000
- Bitcoin sold: 0.30 BTC
- Selected-lot basis: $10,000
- Gain before tax calculation: $20,000

The sale removes lender and interest risk but gives up future exposure on the units sold.

## Borrow example

- Cash proceeds: $30,000
- Collateral: 1.50 BTC
- Starting LTV: 20%

The loan keeps price exposure and adds interest, debt, collateral, liquidation, counterparty, and estate risk.

## Comparison order

1. Same cash need
2. Current tax
3. Interest and fees
4. Bitcoin retained
5. Collateral at lender
6. Drawdown behavior
7. Later / death balance
8. Ending net worth after debt

## Avoid the false shortcut

Equal current tax and interest costs do not make the strategies equal. One sells exposure; the other keeps exposure and creates a managed liability.

## Done when

The household can explain the full cost, risk, and future assumption carrying each option.
