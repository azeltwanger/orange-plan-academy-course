# Coordinate the trust, beneficiaries, and Bitcoin custody

> **Watch this only when a trust exists or counsel recommends one.**

## Ownership and authority matrix

For each asset, record legal owner, intended owner, beneficiary/provider record, life and post-death authority, technical access, binding record holder, and proof of acceptance.

Never record Bitcoin secrets.

## Worked mismatch

A trust says it owns 1.50 BTC and names Jordan successor trustee, but the wallet remains Alex's individual property and only Alex knows recovery.

The legal and technical stories conflict. Fix ownership documentation, tax treatment, custody authority, and recovery without putting the seed in the trust.

## Provider assets versus direct Bitcoin

Provider accounts require accepted ownership, beneficiary, and claim records. Direct Bitcoin requires legal ownership evidence plus a custody path the authorized fiduciary can operate.

## Key distribution

Do not give the trustee unilateral access by default. Coordinate threshold, investment authority, distribution authority, incapacity, succession, and provider roles.

## Retirement accounts

Do not casually retitle retirement accounts to a living trust. A trust-beneficiary designation can change distribution and tax results and requires the plan administrator, attorney, and CPA.

## Tax and basis

Trust income-tax treatment, estate inclusion, gift completion, and basis adjustment are separate questions.

## Proof

Keep legal, provider, tax, custody, test, and review records.

## Done when

Legal owner, provider record, tax documentation, and tested access all agree for every material asset.
