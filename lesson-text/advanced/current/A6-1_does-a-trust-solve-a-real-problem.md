# Does a trust solve a real problem in this plan?

> **Watch this only when the Core estate review identifies a real trust trigger.**

## Start with the job

Possible trust jobs include incapacity management, probate planning, beneficiary control, minors or special needs, privacy, business or multi-state property, role separation, and specific tax or protection planning.

A trust solving one job does not automatically solve the others.

## Demo gate

The demo has minor children, a beneficiary mismatch, self-custodied Bitcoin, and incomplete legal roles/documents.

The immediate work is to correct provider records, choose roles, update legal documents, and coordinate custody. An attorney then decides whether a testamentary provision, revocable trust, custodial structure, or another option best handles the children.

The gate is “get design advice,” not “buy a trust.”

## Revocable trust

Can provide management continuity and probate/privacy benefits for properly funded assets. Retained control generally means the assets remain in the federal gross estate. An unfunded trust does not control assets never transferred or coordinated.

## Irrevocable trust

The tax, basis, control, gift, estate, beneficiary, and administration results depend on the actual structure and retained powers. It is not one universal strategy.

## Trustee and Bitcoin

Ask how concentration, diversification, custody, transaction authority, valuation, and delegation are handled under the document and state law.

## Done when

The household can name the problem, compare the baseline with professional trust options, and explain why the selected structure is worth maintaining.
