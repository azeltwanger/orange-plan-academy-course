# Evaluate state relocation as a life decision

> **Watch this only for a genuine move or material taxable event.**

## Use a Scenario

Model move date, state, housing, income, spending, property, and the taxable event. Do not change the Baseline for tax curiosity.

## Illustrative comparison

A $500,000 gain taxed at an illustrative 4% state rate creates $20,000 of state tax versus $0 at an illustrative 0% rate.

Then include housing, insurance, property tax, travel, work, healthcare, and moving costs. A one-time tax difference may not pay for the full move.

## Residency must be real

Domicile, time, home, work, family, licenses, registration, business ties, property, and intent can matter. Some income may remain sourced elsewhere.

Use a state CPA or attorney for the departure, arrival, sourcing, and evidence plan.

## Sequence the event

Confirm when residency changes and which state can tax the sale, conversion, business event, or earned income before executing it.

## Compare the full plan

Review tax, cost of living, healthcare, work, family, estate rules, retirement timing, and quality of life.

## Done when

The household can explain the total financial and lifestyle trade-off and has a professional residency plan before changing the Baseline.
