# The price context check: naming the emotion before a big move

**Watch this if:** you are about to make a Bitcoin-heavy move. A large buy, selling to lock in gains, or taking a Bitcoin-backed loan. Your allocation decision is complete without it.

## The two lookbacks

- **Recent (3, 6, 9, 12 months):** tells you what you are *feeling*. Up 40% in three months is FOMO whether you admit it or not. Down 40% is fear.
- **Long (2 to 5 years):** tells you what is *true*. The direction of the trend, not the mood of last week.

Run both. The recent window names the emotion you are carrying into the decision. The long window tells you whether that emotion is aligned with reality or reacting against it.

## What it does and does not do

It does not decide the move for you. It names the emotion in the room so you act on the plan instead of the mood.

Before any big move, ask it straight: if the recent 3-to-6 month price move is dramatic, am I reacting to it? If so, wait a beat, then ask whether it is the plan making this decision or the price.

## Your decision

**Whether this move is the plan talking or the price talking.**
