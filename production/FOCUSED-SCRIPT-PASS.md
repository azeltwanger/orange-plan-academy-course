# Focused teaching and cumulative walkthroughs

Austin approved the course-wide tightening review with “okay do it,” then clarified: “yes not every one needs an example probablyu.” This follows his request for conversational introductions, transitions and handoffs, generic household placeholders, and keeping AI-question guidance in the walkthrough rather than Teaching 4.

The purpose is to reduce repeated explanations and repeated work while preserving the reasoning needed to build and maintain a retirement plan. A lesson does not need its own invented scenario. Keep a worked example when it explains a difficult relationship, calculation or trade-off; reuse an earlier result when the next decision depends on it. A clear explanation can stand on its own.

## Delivery and preservation

Keep a short, relevant conversational opening and a natural handoff. The lesson body earns its length by explaining the decision. Avoid replacing complete sentences with an instruction checklist or repeating the same introduction in every lesson.

Walkthroughs begin from the paired teaching and demonstrate the action, interpret the meaningful result and complete the decision. Brief reminders remain where useful. A separately filmable take can have a clear starting point without introducing the whole subject again.

Keep the original, reduced-spending and post-payoff household states distinct. Keep individual account ownership and dates distinct under [Client] / [Partner] display labels. Current facts, an unchosen comparison and an executed provider action remain distinguishable where the action depends on that distinction.

Preserve consequential tax, access, legal, insurance, collateral and recovery conditions at their point of use. The isolated-wallet D07 procedure is outside the repetition cuts. Material loan explanations moved to A3.1 remain subject to the existing factual checks at that new destination. The original household data, source archives, toolkit and capture evidence are unchanged.

The 25 main and eight conditional source IDs and 72 capture takes remain stable. Short core borrowing lessons retain general judgment; detailed offer and collateral work is conditional.
