## What changed

Every teaching script opens with an action and explains the judgment needed to perform it. The slide sequence supplies the steps: review holdings, choose their jobs, test the allocation, choose usable accounts, route affordable contributions and verify purchases; establish sale records, identify an income window and prepare one tax action; establish retirement costs, income dates and accessible funding, then choose annual spending and a funded Reserve response.

Each teaching file now includes Do this, full Read aloud text, exact-phrase overlay cues, a link to the paired walkthrough chapters and an observable finish check. Narration and app filming remain separate. The 22 walkthrough chapters each contain one full Narration block plus Show, Overlay, Verify and Capture dependency fields. Old retired-lesson references were updated to the current consolidated lessons without adding recordings.

Long explanations were shortened by removing repeated setups, rhetorical hooks, generic comparisons and end-of-lesson recaps. Material decisions remain beside the action. Research-performance exposition was removed from the annual-spending speech; its dated research record remains available. Walkthrough narration grew because the former short cues did not supply complete spoken instructions.

## Separate voice and readback pass

After drafting, each spoken block was reviewed for an actionable opening, the information to obtain, a decision criterion, a result to read and a clear finish or missing-evidence path. Definitions were restored where shortening obscured them: HSA, qualified medical expenses, vesting, principal and capital-loss carryforward. Repeated legal disclaimers were removed from introductions; consequential conditions remain next to the affected action. Rhetorical-hook, generic-recap and canned-transition checks found none in these teaching bodies. Exact overlay cues were checked against the final speech rather than approximate paraphrases.

Local structural validation passed for all 15 files: UTF-8/LF; existing local links; one required section per teaching file; exact overlay anchors; all five chapter fields and one complete narration block in each of 22 chapters; no placeholder or production HOLD inside spoken blocks. Retained guardrail phrases and the core account/healthcare concepts were checked, as were the relevant contribution, target, guardrail and sequence arithmetic. Generated masters and repository-wide tests are owned by the coordinating pass and were not rebuilt here.

The remaining dependency on outside expertise is visible: actual legal/account eligibility, tax execution, provider terms, healthcare enrollment and unmodeled effects. It is not an unexplained instruction for a coach to fill in. Missing documents lead to a named evidence task; weak results lead to a specific funding/spending/work comparison. Actual app captures and a target-user pilot are still necessary to establish that a normal user completes the path without private rescue. No learner outcome, device proof, provider action or deployed feature was invented.
