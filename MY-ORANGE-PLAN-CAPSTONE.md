# My Orange Plan — capstone summary

**Purpose:** the learner finishes Core with one page that explains the plan in plain language. This is the graduation requirement, not another lesson or a second financial plan.

Every number comes from an accepted Orange Plan receipt or a documented planning decision. The page contains no seed phrase, private key, passphrase, PIN, password, account credential, wallet address, exact custody location, or full recovery instruction.

3. **Retirement:** The plan first reaches our target around __________________ while supporting __________________.

## Completion standard

The capstone is complete when:

- every number traces to an accepted source or calculated output,
- the Allocation denominator is named,
- extra debt is not routed twice,
- the funding and account-source totals reconcile,
- Bitcoin sale dollars, projected price, and units agree,
- unresolved basis remains visible,
- the household can explain the six sentences without opening the app,
- one to three actions have owners and dates,
- app status is distinguished from real-world proof,
- the PDF and encrypted export are stored separately,
- current restore availability is understood rather than assumed,
- and no Bitcoin secret or account credential appears anywhere on the page.
