# Professional-review outreach templates

Use the send-ready packet for the recipient. Do not send the entire repository.

## Bitcoin-aware CPA or tax attorney

**Subject:** Narrow factual review of Bitcoin financial-planning course

I'm preparing Orange Plan Academy, an educational course that helps Bitcoin holders build and understand a financial plan. Before I approve the final scripts, I need a narrow factual review of the tax claims—not a rewrite of the course or a tax return.

The attached packet lists the exact claims in scope, the affected lessons, and the response format. Please mark each item `OK`, `QUALIFY`, `CHANGE`, `CURRENT FACT`, `PROFESSIONAL ONLY`, or `REMOVE`, and provide the minimum corrected wording plus the primary authority you would rely on.

The main areas are digital-asset basis and specific identification, transfers, Roth/traditional/HSA language, RMDs, conversion and gain windows, early account access, retirement withdrawal phases, and sell-versus-borrow tax caveats.

Would you be available for this scoped review, and what fee and turnaround would you propose?
