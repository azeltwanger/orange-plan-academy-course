## Clearance rule

A public source, provider explanation, product representative, or generic “looks good” does not clear a lesson. The reviewer must address the claim IDs in scope and disclose relevant conflicts or compensation.

Accepted changes are applied to both script and lesson text. Changing facts move to maintained reference. The applicable app/agreement/quote/device/document receipt is attached. Audits pass. Austin then completes the final clean read.

One reviewer clears only their own scope. For example:

- a lender can explain its agreement but cannot independently clear the generic lender comparison,
- a healthcare reviewer does not clear MAGI tax treatment,
- a CPA does not clear Medicare enrollment or legal trust authority,
- and a custody practitioner does not validate a Colorado estate document.

No reviewer receives customer data, Austin's personal plan, repository credentials, wallet addresses, account credentials, or Bitcoin secrets.
