# Orange Plan Academy — external professional send checklist

The source research and claim packets are complete. This page controls what is actually sent so a reviewer receives the smallest useful package.

## Shared cover note

Include:

- Orange Plan Academy is financial education.
- The household and all numbers are fictional.
- The reviewer is evaluating only the claims in the packet.
- A response does not approve the software calculation, Austin's planning judgment, another professional area, or the final spoken wording.
- No Bitcoin secret, account credential, customer data, or Austin household information will be provided.
- Ask for the minimum corrected wording and the primary source, not a full rewrite.
