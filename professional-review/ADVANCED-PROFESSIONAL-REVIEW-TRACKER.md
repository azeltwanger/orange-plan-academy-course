## Clearance rule

A source review, public bio, provider explanation, or generic “looks good” response does not clear a lesson. The reviewer must address the claim IDs in scope and disclose relevant conflicts.

Accepted corrections are applied to script and lesson text, changing facts move to maintained reference, required app/agreement/quote/device/document evidence is attached, audits rerun, and Austin completes one clean final read.

A returned packet clears only the claims within that reviewer's scope. It does not clear another discipline, the app calculation, the learner example, or Austin's planning judgment.
