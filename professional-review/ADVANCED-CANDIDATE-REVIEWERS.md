An independent lending practitioner may review operational terminology after counsel reviews the agreement/legal claims. A lender or custody provider can clarify its own product but should not be the only reviewer of generic comparisons.

## Marketplace and Medicare review

Use official directories rather than guessing at a national reviewer:

- **HealthCare.gov Find Local Help** lists Marketplace-certified assisters and licensed agents/brokers. Assisters are required to provide fair, impartial, and accurate Marketplace information; agents/brokers may be insurer-paid and may not represent every plan.  
  https://www.healthcare.gov/find-assistance/
- **NABIP Find an Agent** can identify licensed health-insurance specialists by geography and practice area. Verify current license, Marketplace certification, carrier appointments, compensation, and individual-market/COBRA experience.  
  https://nabip.org/looking-for-an-agent/find-an-agent
- **State Health Insurance Assistance Programs (SHIP)** provide government-funded, unbiased Medicare counseling. A local SHIP can review the Medicare-transition checklist but may not agree to review commercial course copy.  
  https://www.shiphelp.org/

Recommended review split:

1. Marketplace-certified assister or multi-carrier broker for Marketplace/COBRA/plan terminology
2. CPA for MAGI and tax-source interactions
3. SHIP or qualified Medicare specialist for the Medicare handoff

No single health-insurance reviewer clears the tax claims.
