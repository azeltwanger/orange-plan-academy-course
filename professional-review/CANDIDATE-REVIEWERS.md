# Orange Plan Academy — external reviewer candidate pool

**Research date:** 2026-08-20  
**Status:** researched, not contacted, not endorsed  
**Purpose:** remove the “who could review this?” research step before Austin begins the final voice pass.

A listing here means the public materials appear relevant to the packet. It does not verify current licensure, availability, fee, independence, disciplinary history, or willingness to review educational content. Confirm all of those before engagement.

## Selection rules

Prefer a reviewer who:

- regularly works in the exact subject area,
- is willing to provide claim-level written corrections,
- can cite primary authority or current technical documentation,
- has no need for customer data or Bitcoin secrets,
- understands the difference between educational framing and individual implementation,
- discloses provider, product, carrier, or referral conflicts,
- and agrees that the review does not authorize use of their name as an endorsement without separate permission.

Use one primary reviewer per packet. A second specialist is useful only when the first reviewer identifies a genuine boundary outside their competence.
