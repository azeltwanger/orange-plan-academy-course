## Tax and retirement accounts

### Verified from current IRS authority

- Digital-asset lot selection must match the real transaction and records. For digital assets outside a broker, specific identification is made on the taxpayer's books and records no later than the date and time of the disposition, with adequate records showing which unit left the wallet.
- For broker-custodied digital assets, the taxpayer must provide an adequate identification by the date and time of the disposition. Without adequate identification, the final regulations use an earliest-acquired default within the relevant broker account.
- Temporary broker-selection relief applied to the 2025 transition period and should not be taught as the permanent 2026 procedure.
- The applicable RMD age is 73 for an individual who reaches 73 before 2033 and 75 for an individual who reaches 74 after 2032.
- Roth IRAs and designated Roth accounts generally have no lifetime RMD for the owner; beneficiary rules still apply.
- RMD amount generally depends on the prior year-end balance and the applicable IRS life-expectancy factor.
- Life-insurance death proceeds received because of the insured's death are generally excluded from federal gross income, while interest and transfer-for-value/reportable-sale rules can change the result.
- Disability-benefit taxation can depend on who paid the premium and whether the employee share was paid after tax.

### Course treatment after verification

- Keep exact digital-asset identification mechanics in maintained reference and professional review rather than evergreen spoken video.
- Keep the exact birth-year RMD rule in lesson text/reference; spoken Core can say the age depends on birth year and current law.
- Keep the app HIFO/FIFO result framed as a planning comparison, never proof that the provider or tax records executed the selection.
- Retire the old $18,000 first-year tax/debt example and $60,000/$38,000 source split from the CPA send packet. The current course waits for the `demo-v1-income` app receipt.

## Colorado estate and incapacity

### Verified from Colorado authority

- Colorado probate includes appointment of personal representatives and administration of decedents' estates.
- A person named in a will is not treated in the course as possessing automatic operational authority; the court or applicable probate process establishes and documents authority.
- Colorado enacted the Revised Uniform Fiduciary Access to Digital Assets Act.
- The course is correct to separate legal authority from possession of a Bitcoin key or backup.

### Course treatment after verification

- Keep the spoken lesson generally applicable across the United States.
- Use Colorado only inside the professional packet and demo-specific review.
- Ask Colorado counsel to verify document names, signing requirements, spousal rights, fiduciary digital-asset powers, provider processes, minor-beneficiary design, professional-fiduciary eligibility, and trust triggers.
- Do not use the old slide slogan that beneficiary designations always override a will.
- Do not prescribe a universal seed/passphrase split between heirs and executor.

### Primary authorities monitored

- Colorado Judicial Branch probate and personal-representative resources
- Colorado SB16-088, Revised Uniform Fiduciary Access to Digital Assets Act

## Custody and account security

### Verified from current security authorities

- NIST SP 800-63-4 distinguishes phishing-resistant cryptographic authentication from manually entered one-time codes.
- SMS/PSTN authentication remains restricted, and OTP methods are not phishing-resistant.
- The course's preference for a FIDO security key or properly implemented passkey when available is supported.
- The FTC repeatedly warns that unexpected technical-support contacts may request remote access in order to steal passwords, financial information, or install malware.

### Applied course correction

The custody lesson now explicitly says:

> Never install remote-access software, share the screen, or let an unsolicited support person control a computer or phone used for financial accounts or Bitcoin.

## Insurance

### Verified from current federal/regulatory sources

- A personal umbrella policy can add liability and defense coverage above or outside parts of underlying policies, while generally not covering damage to the policyholder's own home or vehicle.
- Medicare does not pay for most long-term or custodial care and generally does not cover custodial nursing-home care when that is the only care required.
- Life-insurance death proceeds are generally excluded from federal gross income, subject to exceptions and taxable interest.
- Disability-benefit tax treatment depends materially on premium payment and tax treatment.

### Course treatment after verification

- Keep the $62,000 demo amount as only the first annual survivor cash-flow gap:

```text
$80,000 living + $22,000 required debt payments − $40,000 surviving income = $62,000
```

- Do not convert that annual gap into a coverage recommendation until the licensed reviewer evaluates survivor spending, duration, benefits, existing coverage, usable assets, inflation, and timing.
- Keep product, rider, replacement, state, carrier, and contract details outside evergreen Core.

### Primary authorities monitored

- IRS Publication 525 and current life/disability FAQs
- Medicare long-term-care and nursing-home coverage guidance
- NAIC consumer guidance on personal umbrella coverage
