# Professional review — official source starters

**Purpose:** reduce reviewer time and keep changing claims tied to primary or official sources.

This is a starting list, not authority for every situation. The reviewer remains responsible for the current law, contract, device procedure, provider process, and jurisdiction within their scope.

Record the **accessed date**, the **specific section used**, and whether the claim belongs in evergreen spoken video or maintained lesson text.

# Bitcoin custody and account security

## Authentication and phishing resistance

- NIST Digital Identity Guidelines — Authentication and Authenticator Management  
  https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63b.html

- CISA — Implementing Phishing-Resistant MFA  
  https://www.cisa.gov/resources-tools/resources/implementing-phishing-resistant-mfa

- FTC — What to Know About Cryptocurrency and Scams  
  https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-cryptocurrency-and-scams

Reviewer should confirm the distinction between phishing-resistant authenticators, authenticator apps, SMS, account recovery, carrier protection, provider withdrawal controls, and backup lockout risk.

## Hardware wallet and backup procedures

Use the **current official documentation for the exact device and firmware shown in the walkthrough**.

Before filming, record:

- manufacturer,
- device model,
- firmware version,
- official setup link,
- official backup-check or recovery-test link,
- supported backup standard,
- and date verified.

Do not treat one manufacturer's wipe, backup-check, passphrase, compatibility, or recovery procedure as universal.

## Multi-key and descriptors

Use the applicable wallet/provider documentation and Bitcoin technical standards for the exact setup. The professional reviewer should confirm:

- threshold and total key count,
- independent spending paths,
- configuration or descriptor requirements,
- privacy information exposed by non-secret records,
- provider failure path,
- and family/legal recovery process.

The course must not invent a threshold system by manually splitting an ordinary seed phrase.

# Estate attorney

Estate, probate, incapacity, marital-rights, beneficiary, trustee, executor, and digital-asset authority questions require **state-specific primary sources** after the fictional demo state is approved.

For the selected state, the attorney should cite:

- state probate code,
- state power-of-attorney statute,
- state healthcare-directive statute,
- applicable digital-asset / fiduciary-access statute,
- state trust code when relevant,
- community-property or elective-share law when relevant,
- and provider/plan documents for non-probate account transfers.

Federal or general starting points:

- Internal Revenue Service estate and gift tax resources  
  https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/estate-and-gift-taxes

- US Department of Labor retirement-plan participant resources  
  https://www.dol.gov/general/topic/retirement

The course should not state that one beneficiary form always overrides a will. The attorney should confirm the account terms, designation validity, spousal rights, state law, and provider procedure in the scenario being taught.

The attorney should also confirm that the heir letter and executor packet are described as nonbinding process guides rather than substitutes for legal authority.

# Licensed insurance professional

## Life and disability proceeds / tax

- IRS interactive guidance on life-insurance proceeds  
  https://www.irs.gov/help/ita/are-the-life-insurance-proceeds-i-received-taxable

- IRS Publication 525 — Taxable and Nontaxable Income  
  https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-publication-525

The professional and CPA should confirm ownership, transfer-for-value, interest, estate inclusion, employer-paid disability premium, and benefit-tax questions for the actual policy design.

## Product and consumer guidance

- National Association of Insurance Commissioners consumer resources  
  https://content.naic.org/consumer

- Administration for Community Living — LongTermCare.gov  
  https://acl.gov/ltc

- Social Security survivor benefits  
  https://www.ssa.gov/survivor

The reviewer should use current state insurance-department and carrier contract materials for:

- term and permanent policy guarantees and nonguaranteed values,
- disability definition, cap, elimination period, and benefit period,
- umbrella underlying-limit requirements and exclusions,
- long-term-care benefit, inflation, waiting-period, and premium terms,
- replacement and cancellation procedure,
- and beneficiary/ownership records.

The demo's first-pass survivor cash-flow gap is not a coverage recommendation.
