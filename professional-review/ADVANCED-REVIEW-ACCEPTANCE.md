# Advanced professional-review acceptance gate

**Purpose:** make outside review as rigorous and scoped as the Core course review.

A reviewer is not being asked to endorse Orange Plan, approve Austin's planning philosophy, or certify the entire Academy. They are being asked to correct the claims identified in their packet.

## Reviewer qualification

Before sending, record:

- current credential, license, or directly relevant technical role,
- jurisdiction and subject-matter experience,
- recent experience with the exact issue in scope,
- conflicts, provider relationships, referral arrangements, and compensation,
- whether the review is independent or product/provider-specific,
- fee and turnaround,
- and whether the reviewer agrees to claim-level written responses.

A product employee may clarify that product's agreement or procedure. A product employee is not the only reviewer for a generic legal, tax, safety, or comparison claim involving their own product.

## Minimum response standard

For every claim ID, the reviewer returns one code:

- `OK`
- `QUALIFY`
- `CHANGE`
- `CURRENT FACT`
- `AGREEMENT / DEVICE / STATE / PLAN SPECIFIC`
- `PROFESSIONAL ONLY`
- `REMOVE`

Every response other than `OK` includes:

1. minimum replacement wording,
2. the authority, agreement clause, plan document, provider manual, or other source,
3. whether the corrected point belongs in evergreen video, maintained lesson text, a versioned demonstration, or a professional-only handoff,
4. and the date/version reviewed.

A generic “looks good” does not clear the packet.

## Scope matrix

| Area | Reviewer clears | Reviewer does not clear alone |
|---|---|---|
| Bitcoin lending/legal | Agreement structure, collateral control, lender claim, notice/cure, liquidation, release, incapacity/death, legal wording | Federal tax classification, app math, lender creditworthiness, future performance |
| CPA/tax | Basis, identification, conversions, harvesting, access-tax rules, MAGI interactions, loan tax classification, trust/gift/estate/basis tax | Legal authority, enrollment, device recovery, provider promises |
| Healthcare | COBRA/Marketplace/individual-plan language, full-cost fields, current quote/enrollment terminology | Federal/state tax treatment, Medicare legal advice, policy guarantees beyond contract |
| Medicare/SHIP | Medicare enrollment and transition facts | Marketplace tax strategy or commercial-policy recommendation |
| Custody | Device/provider-neutral safety, passphrase/multisig mechanics, recovery evidence, migration sequence | Legal ownership/authority, tax, provider agreement enforceability |
| Colorado estate attorney | Roles, documents, beneficiary/provider coordination, trusts, fiduciary authority, digital-asset powers | Tax return, device recovery, investment recommendation |
| Insurance | Life/disability/umbrella/LTC contract and consumer language | Tax, legal ownership, estate drafting |

## Cross-disciplinary claims

A claim remains held until every required owner responds.

Examples:

- **Bitcoin loan tax treatment:** lending/legal + CPA/tax attorney
- **Marketplace withdrawal strategy:** healthcare + CPA
- **Age-55 plan access:** CPA + actual employer plan administrator/document
- **Trust holding direct Bitcoin:** Colorado attorney + CPA + custody practitioner
- **Collaborative multisig inheritance:** provider agreement + custody practitioner + estate attorney

## Acceptance workflow

1. Verify reviewer and conflicts.
2. Send the smallest packet and affected scripts/lesson texts.
3. Log claim-level responses in the tracker.
4. Resolve conflicting reviewer conclusions explicitly; do not silently choose one.
5. Apply only accepted factual corrections.
6. Move changing facts to maintained reference.
7. Attach the agreement/quote/device/document/app receipt where required.
8. Rerun Advanced structure, gate, voice, provenance, and full-course audits.
9. Give Austin the corrected lesson and a short change note.
10. Austin completes one clean final read before `AUSTIN APPROVED`.

## Required change note for Austin

For each returned packet, prepare:

- lessons changed,
- claim IDs changed,
- old wording,
- new wording,
- why it changed,
- whether the spoken lesson or only maintained reference changed,
- and whether the worked example or decision changed.

Austin should never have to compare a professional's raw packet with the script to understand what changed.

## Rejection criteria

Do not clear a packet when:

- reviewer identity or credential cannot be verified,
- response is generic or promotional,
- material conflicts are undisclosed,
- sources are missing for changed facts,
- provider marketing is treated as independent authority,
- review relies on a different agreement, state, device, tax year, or product without saying so,
- or the response drifts into recommending a product rather than checking the educational claim.
