# WALKTHROUGH 11.2 — walk your report in Orange Plan (screen share)

**One session, 10 steps (~8 min final)** — the capstone read, in planner order: **position → trajectory → risk → actions**

**Before recording:**
□ Plan warm right after a monthly pass · ≥1 saved scenario · fresh confidence check run (3 report sections are conditional on these)
□ ⚠ Reading order ≠ page order — the report leads with trajectory; position is section FIVE. You will jump, and say so.
□ ⚠ Spending band renders only with explicit bands — else "Run a fresh confidence check…" line. Run the check, then generate.
□ Conditional sections: "If Bitcoin follows a different path" (needs a saved scenario) · "Your next steps" · "What these terms mean"

---

## □ 1 · Open + name the reading order
**Account menu (hamburger, aria "Account menu") → Report**
- header: "{Prepared for}" eyebrow · title · **Download PDF** top-right (⚠ label is "Download PDF," not "Print" — it fires the print dialog underneath)
- say the frame: "Nine modules assembled into one document. We read it the way a planner reads it: position, trajectory, risk, actions. The page isn't in that order, so we're going to jump."

## □ 2 · "Are you on track" — TRAJECTORY (section 1)
- confidence ring ("confidence") + age stat + earliest line — **read the date and the ring together**
- green at 80+ = healthy · "a plan that only survives at 99% confidence is one built on optimism — 100 was never the goal"
- one fine, one not → "the disagreement is where the signal lives"

## □ 3 · "Where you stand today" — POSITION (jump to section 5, deliberately)
- **Net worth** + quarter delta + allocation donut + ledger groups
- the first question of the whole read: **is this true?**
- ⚠ stale balance → STOP, fix at the source, regenerate. "A review of wrong numbers is worthless. Don't read around it."

## □ 4 · "What you can spend" + "How retirement gets funded" (sections 3-4)
- big number + band: **Lean decade / Your plan / Strong decade** · funding stages + legend
- "your spending is a band, not a number — floor to fall back to, ceiling as permission"
- ⚠ floor/plan/ceiling are COURSE names, not on-screen words — bridge them: "the band the app calls Lean decade is the floor we've been talking about"

## □ 5 · "How your plan plays out" + "If Bitcoin follows a different path" — RISK (section 2 + conditional ~9)
- year-by-year area chart with retirement + RMD markers
- scenario delta bars ("Safe annual spending. Baseline highlighted") + matrix
- ask ONE question of it: **"Would I still be okay?"** — not which line is prettiest
- ⚠ no saved scenarios = section absent (that's why pre-flight said save one)

## □ 6 · "Tax roadmap" + "Allocation" (sections 8 + 6)
- tax timeline · allocation drift bars · BTC loan card: gauge **"Liquidation risk" / "Cushion"**
- say: "bring this page to your CPA — it turns the tax conversation into a document read"
- ⚠ "Cushion" exists HERE, on the report gauge only — not on Strategy → Debt. Match your language to the label.

## □ 7 · "Protecting what you've built" (section 7)
- readiness segment bar ("{n} of 5 essentials") + ledgers **Estate** / **Insurance and access**
- "the scoreboard for the custody and estate modules — could your family act on this today?"
- ⚠ "Insurance and access" does NOT track policies (rows come from Beneficiaries/Executor items; Protect marks insurance "Coming soon") — name that once

## □ 8 · "Your next steps" + "Assumptions & methodology" — ACTIONS (sections 10-11)
- numbered steps · assumptions grid · footer "Prepared with Orange Plan"
- two lines out loud: **"Three actions, not thirty."** · **"If you can't defend an assumption out loud, change it and regenerate."**

## □ 9 · AI: Review scenario
**Scenarios → select a saved scenario → Review scenario** (⚠ renders only with a scenario selected + editor closed)
- asks: "Which saved scenario would you like to compare with your current plan?"
- read one line it surfaced, agree/disagree · use when a scenario needs plain English before handing the doc to a spouse/CPA/adult child
- ⚠ "explains and compares. Doesn't decide."

## □ 10 · Save the artifact
**Report → Download PDF** — year in the filename, e.g. `orangeplan-report-2027.pdf`
- close: "this is your after picture for this year and your before picture for next year. One report per year, for as long as you have a plan."

## □ WRAP — spot check
- position matches reality (the read IS the audit) · date + ring read as a pair · a band, not a point, all three labels explained
- Bitcoin-path section actually read with "would I still be okay?" answered · protection lines green or the owning module named
- next steps ≤ 3, every assumption defensible · **PDF on disk with the year in the filename**
- "five years of before pictures show you what actually moved and what only felt like it did" → END
