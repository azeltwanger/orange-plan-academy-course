## □ 1 · Enter every debt

**DO** Strategy → Debt → **Add debt**

**SEE** The dialog wants balance, rate, payment, and term. All four, for every debt.

**⚠** A missing rate is the one that quietly breaks this whole lesson. The tier is set BY the rate, so a blank rate means no tier, which means no job.

**⚠** Log in and check the rate rather than trusting the number you remember. A card that was 18% two years ago is not 18% now.

---

## □ 2 · Read the vitals strip

**DO** Strategy → Debt → the four-cell **Debt vitals** strip

**SEE** Both ratios live here. **Debt-to-income** and **debt-to-assets**, current, no arithmetic needed.

**DO** Say which is which out loud: DTI is whether you survive this month, DTA is how much room you have to make a move at all.

**⚠** Point at DTA and say it moves with Bitcoin. That is the whole reason the ceiling gets set against this one.

---

## □ 3 · Read the verdict line

**DO** Same page → **Financial position**, the sentence above the vitals strip

**SEE** The verdict word: **Strong** · **Elevated** · **Stretched**

**⚠** Elevated is not failed. It is a to-do for this year.

**SEE** The stress clause: *"stress test reaches N% of assets"*

**⚠ THIS is the number to manage against.** If a normal bear pushes it past what you could sit through, the leverage is too big, whatever today's ratio says.

---

## □ 4 · Give every debt a job

**DO** Strategy → Debt → the ledger, grouped into **High-cost** and **Low-cost**

**DO** Set the job on each row from the six options: minimum only · extra principal · refinance · consolidate · pay off in full · monitor

**DO** Say the reason out loud as you set each one. That is the deliverable, not the dropdown.

**SEE** The mortgage sitting calmly under Low-cost on minimum payments

**⚠** Say why. That row is the entire "not all debt is the same" argument in one line.

**⚠** No row should read **no payoff path** unless you chose that deliberately and can say why.

**⚠** With no high-cost debts, only Low-cost renders. The app hides empty groups, and that is the healthy read, not a bug.

---

## □ 5 · Route the extra dollars

**DO** Cash Flow → **Routing** · waterfall order → step 2 **Extra debt**

**⚠** Managed on the Debt page, shown on Cash Flow. One number, two screens. Say that, or it reads like two different settings.

---

## □ 6 · Review Debt Strategy (AI)

**DO** Strategy → Debt → **Review Debt Strategy** (page header, beside Add debt). Mobile label: **Review debt**.

**SEE** It asks whether you want the fastest next move or a full review of payoff order, payment burden, and leverage

**⚠** Run it after every debt has a job, not before. It is a second read on your decisions, not a way to make them.
