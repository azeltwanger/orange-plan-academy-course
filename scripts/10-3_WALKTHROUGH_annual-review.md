# 10.3 · WALKTHROUGH — Run the annual review

**Screen capture · 6 steps · ~8 min final** — one timed monthly pass, then the six-area annual lap.

> **DO** = click path · **SEE** = point at this · **⚠** = don't get this wrong
> Narrate in your own words. Nothing here is scripted.

---

## Before you record

- [ ] A couple of transactions ready to enter
- [ ] At least one linked account with something **FLAGGED** — else the AI beat's button won't render
- [ ] Last year's report open in another tab if you have one
- [ ] Set the 18-month / $120k reserve target on Cash Flow → Reserve settings → Target months → **18 mo**. The picker goes to 36 months now
- [ ] Visible timer ready

---

## □ 1 · The monthly pass — TIMED, LIVE

**DO** Start the timer → Cash Flow → **This month**

**SEE** Surplus verdict *"You have $X/mo left to put to work"* — or deficit *"Spending runs $X/mo ahead of income"*

**SEE** Flow rows: Income · Taxes · Living · Debt payments

**DO** **Verify spending → Review → By month** tab

**DO** **Routing · waterfall order** — leave it alone unless something real changed

**⚠** Four inputs, and only ACTUAL changes get entered. The monthly pass isn't a re-planning session. A steady month is 60 seconds.

**⚠** In deficit mode the routing block reads *"This month has no surplus to allocate…"* That's an honest read, and the pass still happened.

---

## □ 2 · Enter transactions

**DO** Dashboard → **Update Transactions** → dialog

**⚠** It's on the Dashboard, NOT Cash Flow. Budget the hop into the timer.

**SEE** Four choices: A linked account · A downloaded file · Describe one transaction to AI · I'll enter them myself

**DO** File path → which account? → **review step before anything saves**

**SEE** Footnote: checked against earlier imports

**⚠** Nothing imported enters the plan silently. Everything waits in review until you approve it.

---

## □ 3 · AI — Explain review items

**DO** Account menu → Accounts → **Explain review items**

**⚠** Only renders when something is flagged.

**SEE** Sub-line: *"{n} institutions · {n} accounts · {n} needs review · last sync {date}"*

**SEE** Asks: *"Which synced item looks missing or wrong if these review counts do not match the page?"*

**DO** Click → stop talking until it lands → read one flagged item back, agree or disagree

**⚠** It explains the queue. It does not approve anything.

---

## □ 4 · The annual lap — six screens, ~40 sec each

**⚠** This is reading, not editing. Fixes belong to a separate session.

### Spending & Reserve — Plan → Income

**DO** **Review annual update** → tiles Prior / After inflation / 80% target / Saved target → **Apply annual update**

**SEE** Reserve read: **Reserve buffer** strip *"{N} yrs · without selling investments"*

**⚠** Refill by hand on the L3 rule: good year → $120k (18 months) · cut year → stop around 12 of 18 (~$80k). A bad market doesn't change the target.

**⚠** Don't invent status words. There is no "Refilling/Paused/Critical" in the app. Read the tiles and the strip as they are.

### Allocation — Strategy → Allocation

**SEE** Funding your timeframes → Reserve/Bridge/Legacy badges Funded/Behind · drift line *"All assets are inside the {N}% drift band"*

**⚠** Re-ask the stress question at TODAY'S balance, not last year's. That's the point of doing it annually.

### Debt & LTV — Strategy → Debt

**SEE** Debt-to-assets (*"caution above {N}%"*) · Debt-to-income (*"high-risk above {N}%"*) · tooltip *"Ratio bands"*

**SEE** BTC loan severity chips: near margin call / margin call / liquidation zone · plus the *"A {N}% drop triggers…"* sentence

**⚠** There's no "loan cushion" label on this page. "Cushion" exists in exactly one place — the report's BTC-loan gauge, in Module 10.

### Tax — Strategy → Tax

**SEE** Card **Moves this year** (eyebrow *"Modeled, not advice"*): Harvest gains · Harvest losses · Roth conversion

**⚠** This one runs BEFORE year-end. In January it's a plan-next-year read, not a do-it-now action.

### Custody — Protect

**SEE** Needs attention → Security checklist *"{n} of {n} for your tier"* · groups Hardware / Distribution / Legal / Access after death

**⚠** The one annual item is **"Full recovery process tested end-to-end."** Unchecked makes it a candidate for the three actions.

### Estate — Protect

**SEE** Five segments → *"{n} of 5 essentials in place"*

**SEE** Switch row (armed): *"warns at 90 days · {n} contacts"* + *"{n} days left"* → **Check in**

**⚠** That's the annual proof of life.

---

## □ 5 · Close the year — report + backup

**DO** Account menu → Report → **Download PDF**

**⚠** Year in the filename. It's next year's before-picture.

**DO** Settings → Data & Privacy → Data & backups → Backup & Restore → **Export Plan**

**⚠** Plaintext passphrase prompt — off-camera, or a visible throwaway. Backup passphrase, not a wallet passphrase.

**SEE** Toast *"Plan exported"*

**⚠** The review isn't done until both files are on disk, dated.

---

## □ 6 · Schedule both rhythms

**DO** Calendar app ON CAMERA: monthly day (first Saturday, recurring) + annual month (January, recurring)

**⚠** There's no native scheduler. This step happens outside the app on purpose.

**⚠** Bonus rhythm worth naming: the 90-day switch email is already a built-in reminder.

**⚠** The close: confidence only flips to Recheck needed when plan INPUTS change. A moving price doesn't touch it. The app won't panic on a red candle, and neither should you.

---

## □ WRAP — spot check off the screen

- [ ] Timer under 5 minutes (say it: most months are quieter than this one)
- [ ] Nothing touched that shouldn't be — name what you never opened
- [ ] Reserve at target, or rebuilding on the rule (half target two years running is the bad case)
- [ ] Allocation passes the stress question at today's number
- [ ] Ratios in band, no loan a 50% drawdown liquidates
- [ ] Tax window used, or deliberately passed, before year-end
- [ ] One recovery proven
- [ ] Letter the family could act on today, switch armed
- [ ] **Output = 1–3 actions, finishable before next month's pass**

**⚠** Longer lists are how reviews stop happening.

**END**
