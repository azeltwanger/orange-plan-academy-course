4.2 Give every account a holding mix and a time-horizon job

============================================================

An account name does not tell you what the household owns or when the money will be needed.

This lesson reads every account in two ways:

1. What holdings are inside it?
2. What time-horizon job does it serve?

== AN ACCOUNT IS A WRAPPER ==

A 401(k), Roth IRA, HSA, 529, and taxable brokerage are account wrappers.

The holdings inside them create the exposure: Bitcoin, stocks, bonds, cash, real estate, or something else.

Two households can each have a $200,000 401(k) and own completely different portfolios.

The first job is to verify every holding rather than inferring the asset from the account name.

== READ THE DEMO BY HOLDING TYPE ==

The demo has $295,000 of investable assets:

- $175,000 Bitcoin
- $75,000 stocks
- $15,000 bonds
- $30,000 cash

Those holdings are spread across cash accounts, taxable brokerage, workplace retirement, Roth, HSA, 529, self-custody, and an exchange.

That combined holding view is what produces the current mix from 4.1.

If a spot Bitcoin ETF is classified like a stock, correct the holding assumption the way Module 1 taught. This lesson does not need to reteach every override control.

== GIVE THE MONEY A TIME-HORIZON JOB ==

**Reserve** is near-term, liquid, low-volatility money. It protects the working household, known near-term bills, and the retirement cash buffer.

**Bridge** is accessible money for goals and retirement gaps inside roughly the next 4 to 10 years. It can take more risk than Reserve, but it has a date and needs a de-risking rule.

**Legacy** is long-term growth capital generally not needed for 10 or more years. Bitcoin and retirement holdings can fit here when the risk tolerance and custody support them.

Legacy does not mean the money can never be used. It means the plan gives it enough time before it has a near-term job.

== ACCOUNT JOB AND HOLDING RISK MUST AGREE ==

A taxable account can be the Bridge and still hold Bitcoin. The label does not remove Bitcoin volatility.

A Roth can be assigned to Legacy and still hold a money-market fund. The wrapper does not create growth.

An HSA can support the healthcare Bridge and still hold volatile investments. The qualified healthcare job does not remove market risk, so near-term expected withdrawals need enough stability.

Review the intended job and the actual holdings together.

== RUN THE DEMO BY TIMEFRAME ==

- The $30,000 cash account is Reserve.
- The taxable brokerage is Bridge.
- The 529 supports the total-family college commitment.
- The HSA is assigned to the **Healthcare Bridge** because the household may use qualified HSA funds before and early in retirement. It is not treated as unrestricted Bridge spending.
- The workplace and Roth retirement accounts are Legacy.
- The 1.50 BTC hardware-wallet balance is Legacy.
- The 0.25 BTC exchange balance is an operating balance, not a separate long-term target.

The account type determines tax and access rules. The intended use determines the planning job.

== TARGETS BEGIN WITH DOLLAR NEEDS ==

Do not begin by guessing that Reserve should be 10%, Bridge 20%, and Legacy 70%.

Reserve comes from spending basis and target months.

Bridge comes from known costs and the accessible retirement-income gap before later income begins.

The Healthcare Bridge comes from expected qualified medical costs and the timing of those costs.

Legacy is the long-term capital remaining after near- and medium-term needs are protected.

The exact Bridge requirement is a checkpoint output because it depends on the year-by-year retirement draw. Do not invent a percentage before that calculation exists.

== WHERE THESE NUMBERS COME FROM ==

### Current holding mix

**What it means:** combined exposure across every account.

**Calculated from:** the holdings inside the accounts.

**Edit source:** holding and account records.

**This affects:** allocation, projection, tax placement, and drawdown exposure.

### Timeframe funding

**What it means:** money assigned to Reserve, Bridge, Healthcare Bridge, and Legacy jobs.

**Calculated from:** assigned accounts and holdings compared with the dollar needs.

**Edit source:** account jobs, reserve settings, known costs, healthcare needs, and retirement-income plan.

**This affects:** contribution routing and forced-sale risk.

== YOUR DECISION ==

Verify the holding mix and choose the primary timeframe job for every account or meaningful balance.

== PUT IT IN ORANGE PLAN ==

Correct the holdings, review the combined current mix, and assign the Reserve, Bridge, Healthcare Bridge, and Legacy roles supported by the current Allocation page and account records.

== YOU ARE DONE WHEN ==

You can describe what every account holds, what job it serves, and where the Reserve and Bridge dollar needs come from.
