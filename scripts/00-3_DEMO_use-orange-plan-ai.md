# 0.3 · DEMO — Use Orange Plan AI to get useful feedback

**Screen capture · narrated, not scripted · target 7–10 minutes**  
**Status:** pre-production structure complete; exact deployed labels and clicks require final UI verification

## What this demo must prove

The learner should see that Orange Plan AI can do more than explain limitations.

It can:

1. Review the current plan and rank what matters
2. Explain a confusing number from its sources
3. Compare a real decision
4. Challenge an assumption or preferred strategy
5. End with one practical next action

The demo should not become a tour of every AI workflow.

## Beat 1 · Start broad when you do not know what matters

**DO** Open AI Review and choose the current full-plan review entry.

**ASK**

> Review my full plan. Rank the top three things that deserve attention by impact. Tell me what looks strong, what is holding the plan back, and what can wait.

**SEE** The response should prioritize rather than list every page.

**TEACH**

- Broad review is for finding the bottleneck.
- It should recognize genuine strengths as well as gaps.
- The next step is to choose one area, not continue a full-plan lecture.

**⚠** Verify the actual current label for `Review my full plan` before recording.

---

## Beat 2 · Explain a number that looks inconsistent

**DO** Move to the Cash Flow-focused review using the current page action or guided workflow.

**ASK**

> Why does Cash Flow show $3,761 when the household is putting $4,000 to work every month? Reconcile the numbers and tell me which amount is already included in Debt.

**SEE** The answer should identify:

- $500 extra auto principal is already inside planned Debt
- $3,500 is routed to accounts
- $261 remains as operating cushion after the account route

**TEACH**

A useful answer points to the source rows and resolves the exact confusion. It does not give generic budgeting advice.

**FOLLOW UP**

> Where would I edit each source, and what else would move if one of them changed?

---

## Beat 3 · Compare a real choice

**DO** Open the retirement-income-focused review from the current Income area.

**ASK**

> Compare keeping our retirement paycheck at $100,000 with using the Balanced amount of $170,216. Show the monthly difference, what happens to confidence and timing, what each choice buys, and what flexibility we give up.

**SEE** The answer should treat the spending references as choices, not instructions to maximize spending.

**TEACH**

The AI is useful when the household is choosing between two real paths. It should make the benefit, cost, and risk visible without making the personal value judgment for them.

---

## Beat 4 · Ask it to challenge the plan

**ASK**

> What is the strongest argument against keeping the $100,000 paycheck, and what is the strongest argument against raising it?

Then ask:

> Which assumption is doing the most work in this retirement result?

**TEACH**

Do not use AI only as a confirmation machine. Ask it to find the fragile assumption, forced-sale risk, or alternative case.

---

## Beat 5 · Use a Scenario for judgment, not another chart

**DO** Open the selected 4% inflation Scenario and use the current Scenario review action.

**ASK**

> The 4% inflation Scenario lowers confidence from 94.6% to 91.6%. Why did it fall by 3 points, which future costs changed most, and does this difference change the retirement decision?

**SEE** The answer should name the actual Scenario change and interpret whether the delta is decision-relevant.

**TEACH**

First understand the Baseline. Then use a Scenario for a specific question.

---

## Beat 6 · Turn the answer into action

**ASK**

> Give me one action to take now, one thing to model next, and one thing that can wait.

**SEE** A useful result separates:

- real-world action,
- model/Scenario work,
- and lower-priority cleanup.

**TEACH**

This is how the conversation ends. The goal is not more chat. The goal is a better next decision.

---

## Beat 7 · Show the professional handoff

**ASK**

> We own 1.75 Bitcoin. Basis is complete for 1.25, reconstruction is pending for 0.40, and 0.10 is unknown. Summarize the facts, the records to gather, and the exact questions to bring to the CPA. Do not recommend filing or execution.

**TEACH**

The AI can make the professional conversation much more useful by organizing the facts and questions. The CPA, attorney, custody professional, insurer, lender, or provider confirms the final execution.
