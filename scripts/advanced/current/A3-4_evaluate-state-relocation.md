> **Watch this only if the household is genuinely considering a move or expects a large taxable event that makes state residence material. Otherwise keep the current state in the Baseline.**

A state-tax difference can be large.

It is still not the whole moving decision.

The question is: **after tax, housing, insurance, work, family, and the years the household expects to stay, does the move improve the life and the plan?**

== KEEP A POSSIBLE MOVE IN SCENARIOS ==

I would keep the current state in the Baseline until the move is real.

A useful relocation Scenario changes:

- move date and destination,
- housing and insurance,
- income and spending,
- property purchase or sale,
- travel and family costs,
- and the taxable event being compared.

Changing only the state-tax field does not model a move.

== WORK THE FULL-LIFE COMPARISON ==

Assume the household expects to realize a $500,000 long-term gain after a legitimate move.

At an illustrative 4% state rate, the tax is $20,000. At an illustrative 0% rate, it is $0.

Those are teaching figures, not a promise about Colorado or another state.

Now include the rest of the move:

- housing costs $12,000 more per year,
- insurance and property tax cost $4,000 more,
- travel back to family costs $4,000,
- and the household expects to stay 3 years.

That is about $60,000 of extra cost before the move itself.

A one-time $20,000 tax difference does not pay for that decision.

The answer can change when the tax benefit repeats, life costs are lower, work improves, or the household already wants to live there. The point is to compare the whole move rather than celebrate one tax line.

== RESIDENCY HAS TO BE REAL ==

A mailbox or one changed account address does not establish domicile.

States can consider the home, time spent, work, family, licenses, registration, voting, business ties, property, and intent. Some income can also remain sourced to another state after the move.

The state CPA or attorney supplies the departure, arrival, sourcing, and evidence plan. The course does not provide one universal day count or proof.

== SEQUENCE THE TAX EVENT ==

Before a connected sale, conversion, business event, or compensation payment, confirm:

- when old residency ends,
- when new residency begins,
- which state can tax the income,
- and which records support the conclusion.

Do not execute a major taxable event from a Scenario alone.

== READ THE RESULT AS A LIFE DECISION ==

Compare the Scenario across tax, housing, insurance, healthcare, work, family, estate rules, retirement timing, and quality of life.

A lower tax estimate can improve ending assets and still produce a worse life. Orange Plan can show the financial result. It cannot choose where the household wants to live.

== WHERE THE NUMBERS COME FROM ==

### State-tax difference

**What it means:** modeled state-tax change between the Baseline and relocation Scenario.

**Calculated from:** state, income type, amount, year, and current app tax rules.

**Edit source:** Scenario state, date, income, and taxable event.

### Net move effect

**What it means:** tax difference plus the financial effects of housing, insurance, work, healthcare, and other changed costs.

**This affects:** surplus, withdrawals, confidence, earliest date, and estate.
