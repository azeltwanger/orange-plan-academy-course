> **Watch this only if a trust already exists or an attorney has recommended one. Otherwise finish the baseline estate and custody work first.**

A signed trust does not move an asset.

The question is: **do legal ownership, provider records, tax records, and Bitcoin access all point to the same intended process?**

== BUILD THE OWNERSHIP-AND-AUTHORITY MATRIX ==

For every meaningful asset, record:

- legal owner today and intended owner after implementation,
- beneficiary or successor record,
- authority during life, incapacity, and after death,
- technical access required,
- provider or professional holding the binding record,
- and proof the change was accepted.

The matrix describes the process. It never contains a seed phrase, private key, passphrase, PIN, password, or complete spending path.

== WORK THE 1.50 BTC MISMATCH ==

Assume the attorney drafts a revocable trust intended to own the household's 1.50 BTC and names Jordan as successor trustee.

The Bitcoin still sits in Alex's individual wallet. Only Alex knows the recovery process, and no assignment or custody record shows how the trust acquired the asset.

The family now has two conflicting stories:

- the document says the trust owns the Bitcoin,
- while the legal and technical process still points only to Alex.

Jordan may have trustee language and no tested way to identify or recover the wallet.

The fix is not putting the seed phrase into the trust.

The attorney, CPA, and custody practitioner must coordinate ownership documentation, tax treatment, living control, incapacity and death authority, and the technical threshold.

== PROVIDER ASSETS AND DIRECT BITCOIN ARE DIFFERENT ==

An exchange, broker, IRA custodian, collaborative provider, bank, or insurer holds its own ownership, beneficiary, authorized-party, and claim records.

A trust document does not update those records automatically.

Direct Bitcoin has no administrator who can correct the mismatch later. The household needs defensible ownership records and a custody path the authorized fiduciary can use.

Possession of a key can move Bitcoin without proving legal authority. Legal authority cannot sign a transaction without a key path. The plan has to solve both.

== MATCH THE THRESHOLD TO THE LEGAL ROLES ==

A trust does not mean the trustee should receive a complete backup or unilateral spending path.

For a 2-of-3 wallet, three keys exist and any two sign. The legal documents and custody process must agree on who can produce each intended combination during life, incapacity, and after death.

I would never start with a universal key split and force the legal plan into it afterward.

The custody practitioner designs and tests the technical path. The estate attorney defines the authority and succession.

== REVIEW RETIREMENT ACCOUNTS SEPARATELY ==

A retirement account generally remains in the individual's name rather than being casually retitled to a living trust.

Naming a trust as beneficiary can change distribution, spouse, minor, tax, and administration results. The plan administrator holds the binding record, and the attorney and CPA review the actual account before any change.

The same account-by-account discipline applies to insurance, transfer-on-death accounts, business interests, and titled property.

== KEEP THE TAX QUESTIONS WITH THE PROFESSIONALS ==

A transfer to a trust can affect gift reporting, income tax, estate inclusion, and basis at death. Those are separate questions and depend on the actual structure.

Orange Plan can show projected asset and estate values. It cannot determine legal ownership, completed-gift status, or filing treatment from the word “trust.”

== PROVE THE PROCESS ==

Use a practice wallet or provider test to show that:

1. The successor knows where the legal and process records are.
2. The intended technical combination works.
3. One unauthorized participant cannot spend when dual control is intended.
4. One specified unavailable key or provider does not block recovery when redundancy is claimed.
5. The provider's authority and claim process is current.

Do not make the first test the main wallet after death.

For each asset, keep the signed document, accepted provider confirmation, ownership or assignment record, tax record, custody process, test date, and next review date.

Orange Plan tracks the checklist. The proof remains with the attorney, provider, tax file, and tested custody process.
