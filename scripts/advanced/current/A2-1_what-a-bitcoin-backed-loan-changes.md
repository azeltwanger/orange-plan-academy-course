A2.1 What does a Bitcoin-backed loan change, and how does LTV move?

> **Watch this only if you already have a Bitcoin-backed loan or are actively comparing one. Otherwise keep borrowing out of the saved plan and return to the Core funding strategy.**

The first question is not, “How much will the lender let me borrow?”

It is: **what changes in the financial plan the moment Bitcoin becomes collateral, and how far can the price fall before the lender controls the outcome?**

== THE LOAN DOES NOT CREATE WEALTH ==

A Bitcoin-backed loan exchanges one form of access for another.

The household receives cash and creates a liability. The Bitcoin remains economically exposed to price changes, but it is now pledged under a lender agreement.

That means four things change at once:

- cash available today rises,
- debt and interest appear,
- Bitcoin held with or under the lender structure rises,
- and part of the household's Bitcoin can no longer be treated like ordinary available custody.

The loan can defer a sale. It does not make the spending free and it does not increase net worth on the day it is opened.

== LTV IS THE RISK GAUGE ==

Loan-to-value is:

> **Loan balance ÷ collateral value = LTV**

If the household borrows $30,000 against 1.50 BTC worth $150,000, the starting LTV is 20%.

Two things move it.

The loan balance can rise through interest or fees when they are not paid separately.

The collateral value moves with Bitcoin, often much faster.

If Bitcoin falls by half while the loan balance stays $30,000, the collateral is worth $75,000 and LTV rises from 20% to 40%.

A 50% price decline does not create a 50% LTV increase. The ratio doubles because the value in the denominator was cut in half.

== THE THRESHOLD PRICE FORMULA ==

Every real agreement has its own rules, but the arithmetic is the same.

The Bitcoin price at a given LTV threshold is:

> **Loan balance ÷ collateral BTC ÷ threshold LTV**

Use an illustrative agreement with:

- $30,000 loan,
- 1.50 BTC collateral,
- a 65% action or margin-call threshold,
- and an 80% liquidation threshold.

Those thresholds are for teaching only. They are not a claim about any current lender.

At 65% LTV:

> $30,000 ÷ 1.50 ÷ 0.65 = about **$30,769 per BTC**

At 80% LTV:

> $30,000 ÷ 1.50 ÷ 0.80 = **$25,000 per BTC**

Starting from $100,000 Bitcoin, the action line is about a 69% decline and the liquidation line is a 75% decline.

That is the real meaning of a 20% starting LTV under this agreement. It does not mean the loan is “safe.” It means a 75% price drop reaches the liquidation threshold before interest, fees, operational delays, or lender discretion are considered.

== WORK THE PRICE PATH ==

| Bitcoin price | Collateral value | LTV |
|---:|---:|---:|
| $100,000 | $150,000 | 20% |
| $75,000 | $112,500 | 26.7% |
| $50,000 | $75,000 | 40% |
| $35,000 | $52,500 | 57.1% |
| $30,769 | $46,154 | 65% |
| $25,000 | $37,500 | 80% |

The table makes the trade-off visible.

A low starting LTV creates room. It also means pledging much more Bitcoin for each dollar borrowed.

A higher starting LTV gives more cash against the same collateral. It brings the lender's action lines much closer.

The correct starting point is based on the drawdown the household wants the position to survive—not on the product maximum.

== THREE DIFFERENT RISKS ARE HIDING IN ONE LOAN ==

**Market risk:** Bitcoin falls and LTV rises.

**Contract risk:** the agreement controls notices, top-ups, liquidation, fees, and release of collateral.

**Counterparty and operational risk:** the lender, custodian, key structure, legal claim, or transfer process fails even when Bitcoin's price is fine.

A strong price cushion does not remove lender risk. A strong lender does not remove price risk.

Both belong in the decision.

== TAX DEFERRAL IS NOT TAX ELIMINATION ==

Receiving proceeds from a bona fide loan is generally different from selling Bitcoin, but the full tax result depends on what happens next.

Interest treatment, liquidation, debt cancellation, refinance, entity ownership, use of proceeds, and eventual repayment can create separate consequences.

The app can compare the planned sale and loan paths. The CPA or tax attorney confirms the actual tax treatment and records.

== WHERE THE NUMBERS COME FROM ==

### Current LTV

**What it means:** loan balance compared with current pledged-collateral value.

**Calculated from:** current balance, collateral BTC, and current Bitcoin price.

**Edit source:** the real loan record and collateral amount.

**This affects:** action status, top-up need, liquidation risk, collateral at the lender, and remaining borrowing capacity.

### Threshold Bitcoin price

**What it means:** the price at which the position reaches a selected agreement LTV.

**Calculated from:** loan balance ÷ collateral BTC ÷ threshold LTV.

**Edit source:** lender terms, loan balance, and collateral.

**This affects:** the drawdown cushion and the action plan.
