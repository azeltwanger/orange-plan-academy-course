A3.3 Harvest gains or losses only when the opportunity is real

============================================================

> **Watch this only if taxable lots currently contain a meaningful gain or loss and the household has a reason to realize it. Otherwise leave the lots alone.**

Bitcoin volatility creates tax opportunities.

It also creates a temptation to manufacture activity because the price moved.

The useful question is: **does realizing this gain or loss improve the full plan after tax, basis, transaction, exposure, and execution are considered?**

== LOSS HARVESTING ==

Suppose a taxable Bitcoin lot has:

- current value of $25,000,
- basis of $40,000,
- and an unrealized loss of $15,000.

Selling the supported lot realizes the loss before the return applies the current capital-loss rules, other gains, carryforwards, and any allowable ordinary-income offset.

The loss can be valuable when the household has gains to offset now or expects the carryforward to be useful later.

It is less useful when transaction costs, spread, custody movement, or the risk of losing the intended Bitcoin exposure outweigh the benefit.

== KEEP THE INVESTMENT DECISION SEPARATE ==

A tax loss is not a reason to abandon the long-term allocation.

The household may want to maintain Bitcoin exposure after the disposition.

Current repurchase rules can differ between direct Bitcoin and securities such as a Bitcoin ETF, and the law can change. The CPA confirms the current treatment and any related-party, substance, or anti-abuse issue.

Do not carry an old “Bitcoin has no wash-sale rule” sentence forever inside a video.

The durable instruction is to verify the current rule before the trade and document both the tax purpose and the exposure decision.

== GAIN HARVESTING ==

Now assume a supported lot has:

- value of $30,000,
- basis of $10,000,
- and an unrealized long-term gain of $20,000.

A lower-income year may allow the household to realize some gain at a favorable federal rate and repurchase or reposition the asset with a higher basis.

That can reduce tax on a later sale.

But “the 0% bracket exists” does not mean the household can realize an unlimited gain for free.

The gain stacks with taxable income. State tax, net investment income tax, Marketplace subsidies, Social Security taxation, and other interactions can change the result.

The amount of gain inside a favorable band must come from the full return, not one headline threshold.

== WORK THE CHOICE, NOT THE SLOGAN ==

I would compare three paths:

1. Do nothing
2. Realize the proposed loss or gain
3. Realize a smaller amount

For each path, read:

- current tax,
- remaining basis,
- loss carryforward,
- Bitcoin exposure after execution,
- transaction and custody friction,
- and the future sale the strategy is supposed to improve.

A gain harvest that saves tax only if a later sale happens may still be useful. Say what future action creates the benefit.

A loss harvest with no likely use for the loss may be lower priority than cleaning missing basis or funding the Reserve.

Orange Plan can compare the paths. It cannot prove that the intended lot was the one actually sold.

== LOT IDENTIFICATION STILL CONTROLS ==

The intended lot must be identified and documented under the current account or wallet rules.

If the household sells the wrong lot, the app comparison is no longer the transaction that occurred.

Finish A3.1 before using harvesting as an implementation strategy.

== WHERE THE NUMBERS COME FROM ==

### Harvestable gain or loss

**What it means:** unrealized difference on the specific supported lot being considered.

**Calculated from:** current value minus remaining lot basis.

**Edit source:** quantity, current price, and tax-lot record.

### Modeled tax benefit

**What it means:** difference in current and future modeled tax between the chosen strategy and doing nothing.

**Calculated from:** the full return and projection under both paths.

**This affects:** current tax, basis, carryforwards, future withdrawals, healthcare costs, and net proceeds.

== YOUR DECISION ==

Choose whether the opportunity is meaningful now, which lot and amount are being considered, and what future gain or sale makes the strategy useful.
