> **Watch this only if a Bitcoin-backed loan remains under consideration after the LTV lesson. Otherwise leave the loan out and return to the Core Debt strategy.**

Knowing the liquidation price is not a survival plan.

The real question is: **what exactly will the household do at each risk line, what resources are available, and how does the loan end?**

== WRITE THE ACTIONS BEFORE THE PRICE FALLS ==

A lender event usually gives the borrower some combination of four choices:

1. Add collateral
2. Pay down the balance
3. Refinance or close the position
4. Allow contractual liquidation

The agreement decides which choices are available, how quickly the borrower must act, and whether the lender can liquidate before or without a manual response.

Do not wait for the alert to decide.

During a drawdown, the household is making the decision with less time, lower net worth, stressed networks, and possibly a lender receiving more calls than it can handle.

== TOP-UP CAPACITY IS FINITE ==

Return to the illustrative $30,000 loan backed by 1.50 BTC.

The position reaches the 65% action line around $30,769 per BTC.

Suppose the household wants to top the loan back down to 50% LTV.

The total collateral required is:

> $30,000 ÷ 0.50 ÷ $30,769 = about **1.95 BTC**

The loan already has 1.50 BTC. The top-up requires about **0.45 BTC**.

If the household kept only 0.50 unpledged BTC, one action uses almost the entire reserve.

At $25,000 Bitcoin, returning the same $30,000 balance to 50% LTV would require:

> $30,000 ÷ 0.50 ÷ $25,000 = **2.40 BTC**

The position would need another 0.90 BTC beyond the original collateral. A 0.50 BTC reserve cannot do it.

That is why “auto top-up is on” is not a complete answer. Auto top-up can prevent an early liquidation and also pull most or all remaining Bitcoin into the lender structure during a continuing decline.

== CASH PAYDOWN HAS THE SAME LIMIT ==

At the 65% line, the collateral is worth about $46,154.

To reduce the position to 50% LTV without adding Bitcoin, the balance must fall to about $23,077.

The household needs roughly **$6,923 of cash** immediately.

That cash cannot also be the emergency reserve, quarterly tax money, or next month's spending unless the household deliberately accepts weakening those jobs.

A loan survival reserve is a separate named resource.

== USE A LADDER, NOT ONE VAGUE PROMISE ==

A written action ladder can look like this:

- **Review line:** verify the lender account, communication channels, collateral, cash, and exit options.
- **Action line:** top up only to the written target or pay down the written amount.
- **Hard exit line:** close or reduce the position before contractual liquidation when the household no longer has enough reserve for the next step.
- **Contract liquidation:** understand what the lender can sell, how much, when, and what remains afterward.

The exact percentages come from the agreement and household policy. Do not copy the illustrative 65/80 lines.

== THE LOAN NEEDS A REPAYMENT SOURCE ==

A loan that only works if Bitcoin rises is a price bet, not a repayment plan.

Name the primary source:

- future cash flow,
- a scheduled asset sale,
- a business or property event,
- refinance under defined conditions,
- or eventual estate repayment.

Then name the backup source.

If the household expects a Bitcoin sale later, the loan may be delaying the sale rather than eliminating it. That can still be useful, but the comparison should include interest and the eventual tax event.

== INTEREST CHANGES THE CUSHION ==

If interest is paid from cash, the balance may stay stable while cash flow falls.

If interest is added to the balance, LTV rises even when Bitcoin's price is flat.

A $30,000 balance growing to $33,000 changes every threshold price by 10%.

The plan must state whether interest is paid, capitalized, deducted from proceeds, variable, or reset at renewal.

== PLAN FOR INCAPACITY AND DEATH ==

The spouse, executor, or agent should know that the loan exists, which provider holds collateral, where the agreement is stored, who can communicate with the lender, and how repayment or release works.

They do not need a seed phrase in the plan documents.

A loan balance at death can reduce the estate even when the projected Bitcoin value is large. The estate attorney and lender agreement control the legal process.

== WHERE THE NUMBERS COME FROM ==

### Required top-up

**What it means:** additional BTC needed to reach the written target LTV.

**Calculated from:** required total collateral at target LTV minus BTC already pledged.

**Edit source:** current balance, price, pledged BTC, and target.

**This affects:** unpledged custody, lender exposure, and future top-up capacity.

### Required paydown

**What it means:** cash needed to reach the written target LTV without adding collateral.

**Calculated from:** current balance minus the balance allowed at target LTV.

**This affects:** cash reserve, spending liquidity, and the hard-exit decision.
