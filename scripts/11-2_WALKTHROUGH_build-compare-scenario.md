# 11.2 · WALKTHROUGH — Build and compare a scenario

**Screen capture · 7 steps · ~9 min final**

> **DO** = click path · **SEE** = point at this · **⚠** = don't get this wrong
> Narrate in your own words. Nothing here is scripted.

---

## Before you record

- [ ] Plan complete and accurate — this whole page is a comparison against it
- [ ] Retirement age + state + retirement spending correct in settings (the presets read them)
- [ ] No saved scenarios yet if you want the empty state on camera, or one saved if you don't
- [ ] Clean browser, notifications off

---

## □ 1 · Orient on the page

**DO** Scenarios (primary nav) → h1 **Scenarios**

**SEE** Three zones, top to bottom: **What if...** presets · **Your scenarios** (*"Saved what-ifs you can reopen and compare"*) · the comparison section below

**SEE** Empty states: *"No custom scenarios yet."* · *"Choose a saved scenario to see the chart, key metrics, and Monte Carlo comparison."*

**SEE** Top right: **Custom scenario** · **Review scenario** (appears only once a scenario is selected)

---

## □ 2 · The stress test, one click

**DO** What if... → **50% drawdown after retirement**

**SEE** The description fills in YOUR retirement age: *"BTC and stocks drop 50% the year after you retire at {age}. The classic sequence-of-returns stress test."*

**SEE** One click creates it, selects it under **Your scenarios**, and re-runs the plan

**⚠** The grid shows 4 of 9. **See more scenarios** expands, **Show fewer scenarios** collapses.

**DO** Name the other eight while expanded: Bear Market · Retire earlier · Spend less in retirement · Move to no-tax state · Conservative BTC returns · Moderate BTC returns · Claim SS at 62 · Claim SS at 70

---

## □ 3 · Read the comparison

**DO** Comparison section → chip with the scenario name → **vs your plan**

**SEE** Headline row, three tiles: **Retire at** · **Spending** · **Success rate**

**DO** Expand **Full comparison metrics**

**SEE** Net worth at retirement · net worth at age 90 · lifetime taxes paid · effective tax rate · withdrawal rate at retirement · depletion age · earliest retirement age · BTC at retirement · BTC at life expectancy

**SEE** Each row shows baseline · scenario · difference — matching rows read **no change**

**⚠ Say it:** one question only. Would I still be okay? Not which line is prettier.

---

## □ 4 · Run the Monte Carlo — let it breathe

**DO** **Run Monte Carlo** (comparison section)

**SEE** Spinner + phase label

**⚠** Until this runs, the success rate isn't a real comparison. The chart updates instantly off the projection, but confidence has to actually simulate.

**DO** Read the before/after success rate out loud

---

## □ 5 · Build the custom one

**DO** **Custom scenario** (top right) → dialog **Create scenario**

**SEE** Step 1: Choose scenario type → seven cards: Life events · Retirement · Savings · Debt · Tax · Markets · Advanced

**⚠** Pick the specific type, NOT Advanced. Advanced is every control at once, and it's for real multi-part scenarios only.

**DO** **Name** + **Description**

**SEE** The placeholder changes with the type (Retirement → *"e.g., Retire at 55"*; Tax → *"e.g., Roth conversion window"*)

**⚠** Name it after the QUESTION, not the answer. "Retire at 55" works. "Better plan" doesn't.

**DO** Change ONE variable → Save

**⚠** Land the line: if I change three things and it comes back worse, I've learned nothing.

---

## □ 6 · Compare, then manage

**DO** Select the new scenario under Your scenarios → same read: headline row → **Full comparison metrics** → **Run Monte Carlo**

**DO** **⋯** menu (aria *"Scenario comparison actions"*) → **Duplicate** · **Edit** · **Delete**

**SEE** **Duplicate** is the one to point at — it's how you build a second scenario that differs by exactly one thing

**⚠** Nothing here touches your plan. Say it out loud, because it's why you can experiment freely.

---

## □ 7 · AI — Review scenario

**DO** **Review scenario** (top right — renders only with a scenario selected AND the editor closed)

**SEE** It reads saved scenarios, projection years, life events, assumptions

**SEE** Asks: *"Which saved scenario would you like to compare with your current plan?"*

**DO** Answer → stop talking until it lands → read one line back, say whether you agree

**⚠** When to actually run it: when you're explaining a comparison to someone else, or when you can't tell why two scenarios differ.

**⚠** It explains and compares. It doesn't decide.

---

## □ WRAP — spot check off the screen

- [ ] Two scenarios saved: one stress test, one choice
- [ ] Both Monte Carlo'd (real success rates, not placeholders)
- [ ] Stress test answered out loud — and if the answer was no, the lever named
- [ ] One variable per scenario, and you can say which
- [ ] Baseline untouched
- [ ] Names that are questions, still readable a year from now

**⚠** Close: these also unlock the report's Bitcoin-path section. No saved scenario, no section.
