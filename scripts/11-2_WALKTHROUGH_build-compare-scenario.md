# WALKTHROUGH 11.2 — build and compare a scenario (screen share)

**One session, 7 steps (~9 min final) · capture segment `11.2`**

**Before recording:**
□ Plan complete and accurate (this whole page is a comparison against it)
□ Retirement age + state + retirement spending correct in settings — the presets read them
□ No saved scenarios yet if you want the empty state on camera, or one saved if you don't
□ Clean browser · notifications off

---

**Open:** "Your plan says where you land if things go how you assumed. Scenarios ask what happens if they don't. We're going to build two: one stress test, one choice."

---

## □ 1 · Orient on the page
**Scenarios** (primary nav) → h1 **Scenarios**
- 👀 three zones top to bottom: **What if...** presets · **Your scenarios** (*"Saved what-ifs you can reopen and compare"*) · comparison section below
- empty states: *"No custom scenarios yet."* · *"Choose a saved scenario to see the chart, key metrics, and Monte Carlo comparison."*
- top right: **Custom scenario** · **Review scenario** (only appears once a scenario is selected)

## □ 2 · The stress test, one click
**What if... → 50% drawdown after retirement**
- 👀 description fills in YOUR retirement age: *"BTC and stocks drop 50% the year after you retire at {age}. The classic sequence-of-returns stress test."*
- one click creates + selects it under **Your scenarios** · plan re-runs
- ⚠ grid shows 4 of 9 — **See more scenarios** expands, **Show fewer scenarios** collapses
- name the other eight while expanded: Bear Market · Retire earlier · Spend less in retirement · Move to no-tax state · Conservative BTC returns · Moderate BTC returns · Claim SS at 62 · Claim SS at 70

## □ 3 · Read the comparison
**Comparison section** → chip with scenario name → **vs your plan**
- headline row, 3 tiles: **Retire at** · **Spending** · **Success rate**
- expand **Full comparison metrics** → net worth at retirement · net worth at age 90 · lifetime taxes paid · effective tax rate · withdrawal rate at retirement · depletion age · earliest retirement age · BTC at retirement · BTC at life expectancy
- 👀 each row: baseline · scenario · difference — matching rows read **no change**
- **say it:** "one question only — would I still be okay? Not which line is prettier."

## □ 4 · Run the Monte Carlo — 🎬 let it breathe
**Run Monte Carlo** (comparison section)
- 👀 spinner + phase label
- ⚠ **until this runs, the success rate isn't a real comparison** — chart updates instantly off the projection, confidence has to actually simulate
- read the before/after success rate out loud

## □ 5 · Build the custom one
**Custom scenario** (top right) → dialog **Create scenario**
- **Step 1: Choose scenario type** → 7 cards: Life events · Retirement · Savings · Debt · Tax · Markets · Advanced
- ⚠ **pick the specific type, NOT Advanced.** Advanced = every control at once, for real multi-part scenarios only
- **Name** + **Description** — 👀 placeholder changes with type (Retirement → *"e.g., Retire at 55"*; Tax → *"e.g., Roth conversion window"*)
- **name it after the QUESTION, not the answer.** "Retire at 55" ✓ · "Better plan" ✗
- **change ONE variable** → Save
- line: "if I change three things and it comes back worse, I've learned nothing"

## □ 6 · Compare, then manage
**Select the new scenario under Your scenarios**
- same read: headline row → **Full comparison metrics** → **Run Monte Carlo**
- **⋯** menu (aria *"Scenario comparison actions"*) → **Duplicate** · **Edit** · **Delete**
- 👀 **Duplicate** is the one to point at: how you build a second scenario differing by exactly one thing
- ⚠ **nothing here touches your plan** — say it, it's why you can experiment freely

## □ 7 · AI · Review scenario
**Review scenario** (top right — only with a scenario selected AND editor closed)
- **reads:** saved scenarios, projection years, life events, assumptions
- **asks:** *"Which saved scenario would you like to compare with your current plan?"*
- answer → 🎬 stop talking until it lands → read one line back, say whether you agree
- **when to run it:** explaining a comparison to someone else, or when you can't tell why two scenarios differ
- ⚠ it explains and compares. It doesn't decide.

## □ WRAP — what good looks like
- two scenarios saved: one stress test, one choice
- both Monte Carlo'd (real success rates, not placeholders)
- stress test answered out loud — and if "no," the lever named
- one variable per scenario, and you can say which
- baseline untouched
- names that are questions, still readable a year from now
- close: "these also unlock the report's Bitcoin-path section — no saved scenario, no section" → END
