# WALKTHROUGH 3.4 — cash flow + reserve (screen share)

**One session, 10 steps (~15 min capture)**

**Before recording:**
□ Two-3 months of categorized transactions linked or imported (else Verify Spending is empty)
□ Bare-bones number on a card: couple = $5,000/mo floor · $30,000 target (6 mo)

---

**Open:** "This walkthrough puts the last three lessons into the app. Cash flow and reserve are the engine of every projection — this is where they get accurate."

## □ 1 · Read the verdict
**Cash Flow → This month**
- headline: "You have $X/mo left to put to work" · sub: income covers taxes, living, debt → what's left routes below
- ⚠ deficit mode headline: "Spending runs $X/mo ahead of income" — "not a failure, this module is the fix; routing pauses honestly"

## □ 2 · Map your income
**Income row → + Add income source**
- every stream listed separately: salary per earner · self-employment · rental · dividends
- Other group: **Tax-free income** (gifts, VA, munis — never taxed in projections) · **Other recurring**
- note which streams are reliable vs variable — feeds reserve sizing
- ⚠ add menu only shows types NOT already listed — "if you can't find it, it's already there"

## □ 3 · Sort your spending — ⚠ SLOW DOWN
**3 rows: Taxes · Living · Debt payments**
- **Living = your spending number. Not your paycheck. Not debt payments. The biggest data-entry mistake dies here.**
- enter the Keep/Cut/Reduce result as the new Living number
- ⚠ don't click into Debt payments — that's the per-debt editor, Module 4's job

## □ 4 · Verify spending against reality
**Verify Spending card → Review → By month tab**
- card reads: "You spend ~$X. Your plan says $Y" + % difference
- walk 2-3 real months · one-timers: three-dot → **Exclude · one-time** (recover under status filter → Ignored / one-time)
- ⚠ never-synced card shows "Connect" instead of Review — link an account first
- line: "two or 3 months of real transactions beats any estimate"

## □ 5 · Set the reserve fields
**Reserve settings (below Routing)** — four fields in render order:
1. **Target months** (None/1/3/6/12) → 6
2. **Reserve basis** → **Bare-bones** ⚠ (floor, not lifestyle)
3. **Monthly build cap** → their routing pace
4. **Bare-bones essentials** → $5,000
- ⚠ fill Bare-bones BEFORE setting basis, else warning strip: "Add a bare-bones number to use it…"
- math on screen: floor × months = target

## □ 6 · Read the target in the waterfall
**Routing · waterfall order → step 1 Cash reserve**
- progress: "$X of $Y" + "full in N mo" / "fully funded" · caption "first claim until funded"
- "full in N months = the build cap doing its job"
- ⚠ caption "Surplus routes past reserve until a target is set…" = settings didn't save → back to step 5

## □ 7 · Shield the reserve holding
**Dashboard → account group → holding row three-dot ("Holding actions for {asset}") → Add shield**
- toast: "Designated as emergency fund" · row gets **Shield** pill
- ⚠ shield the exact holding, not the whole account
- desktop: menu appears on row hover only

## □ 8 · Read the waterfall order
**Cash Flow → Routing · waterfall order** — 3 steps:
1. Cash reserve — *first claim until funded*
2. Extra debt — *all N debts on minimums · manage* ⚠ read-only here, amounts live on Strategy → Debt (Module 4)
3. Contributions — *remainder after reserve*
- "the order of operations the app runs every month so you don't re-decide it"
- deficit mode: routing block replaced by "This month has no surplus to allocate…"

## □ 9 · AI review: Cash Flow & Reserve
**Account menu → AI Review → For this page: "Review Cash Flow & Reserve"**
- reads your cash flow + life events
- clarifying Q: "fastest next move, or full check of income, spending, reserve, routing?"
- answer → wait → read ONE thing it surfaced, agree or disagree out loud
- ⚠ "it reviews and explains. It does not decide." · skip **Route my next dollar** (Module 3's centerpiece)

## □ 10 · AI review: spending calibration
**Account menu → Accounts → Spending tab → "Verify linked spending"**
- ⚠ NOT on the Cash Flow AI panel — reach from Accounts
- reads saved living spending vs linked-account average + coverage gaps
- asks: "Does the missing spending account contain regular purchases that would materially change this average?"
- hands you back to the Verify Spending drawer — "same tool, now with a reason"

## □ WRAP — spot check off the screen
- positive surplus the household could actually move · every stream listed · Living ≠ paycheck (if Living ≈ Income, wrong row)
- Verify within shouting distance 2 months running · reserve basis = Bare-bones, no warning strip
- routing step 1 shows real $X of $Y + full in N mo (no end date = build cap is $0)
- shield on the exact holding · reserve first in the waterfall
- "that's the engine built — Module 3 decides where the surplus goes" → END
