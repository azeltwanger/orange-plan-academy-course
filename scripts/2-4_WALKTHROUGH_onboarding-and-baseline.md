WALKTHROUGH SCRIPT — segment 2.4 (screen share)
2.4 Set up your plan and build your baseline in Orange Plan
Film as TWO sessions: Part A = onboarding (13 steps) · Part B = baseline lap (9 steps)
============================================================

BEFORE RECORDING:
□ Fresh demo account — Part A starts the wizard from zero
□ The couple's numbers on a card next to you (income $190k, spending $80k, 1.75 BTC, $280k mortgage @3.25%, $18k car @7%, 401k $12k/yr, Roth $7k/yr)
□ Clean browser profile, notifications off
□ 5 seconds of stillness before the first click and after the last

============================================================
PART A — ONBOARDING
============================================================

INTRO (before clicking):
SAY: In this walkthrough, we're going to set up your plan in Orange Plan from scratch. This first part is the onboarding, and it's going to take about 15 minutes. By the end of it, you're going to have a working plan with a retirement date on it. Have your account balances, your debt balances and rates, and last year's spending nearby before you start.

□ ORIENT — the wizard layout (10 seconds, don't dwell)
SAY: A couple things about the layout. The counter at the top is going to show you where you are, 1 of 13 through 13 of 13. The app saves as you go — you'll see this green Auto-saved pill — so you can leave at any point and come back. And on most steps you've got Back, Skip for now, and Save & finish later down at the bottom.

□ STEP 1 — Privacy
CLICK: the Cloud card → Continue
SAY: Two options here. Cloud is going to sync across your devices, back up your data, and let you link bank accounts. Local Only stays in this browser — it's more private, but if the browser data is lost, that's on you. I'd pick Cloud, and you can change this later in Settings. One thing to know: the dead man's switch we set up in the estate module needs Cloud to work.

□ STEP 2 — About You (7 sub-slides)
CLICK: through each slide, entering the couple's answers
SAY: (as you go) Age... when you want to retire — and notice the subtitle updates with how many years that gives you to build wealth... how you file taxes — this one won't let you continue until you answer it... spouse's age... dependents — the app uses these so child tax credits phase out correctly... where you live — watch for the No state tax badge on states like Texas and Florida, we cover why that matters in the tax module... and how you earn income.
WATCH FOR: the "That gives you N years to build wealth" subtitle; the No state tax badge

□ STEP 3 — Income and Spending  ⚠ THE critical step — slow down here
CLICK: Your income → Spouse income → Living spending → Retirement spending
SAY: Income first — you can use the quick-pick chips or type your own. Spouse income goes in its own field, and there are two more fields below for self-employment income that we'll leave blank.
SAY: (at Living spending — read the subtitle on screen) Now this is the single most important number to get right in the whole setup. Living spending only. Housing, food, bills, travel, everyday spending. Not your paycheck, and not your debt payments — the app tracks those separately. If you enter your salary here, every projection downstream is going to be wrong.
WATCH FOR: two panels appear — Savings rate (green at 30%+) and Approx. monthly room before debt (red if negative). Point at both.
SAY: (at Retirement spending) Usually the same as today unless you know it's going to change. And same rule — this excludes debt payments.

□ STEP 4 — Accounts
CLICK: Add Account → pick type → name it → pick owner → Add Account. Repeat for each.
SAY: You can skip the bank-linking card and stay manual — that's what we're going to do here. Now we're going to add every real account. Hardware wallet, exchange, brokerage, 401k, savings. And notice there's no generic "Bitcoin account" — the app makes you say where your Bitcoin is actually held. That distinction feeds the custody module later.
WATCH FOR: the green "✓ N accounts ready to track" line when done

□ STEP 5 — Assets
CLICK: on each account → + Add Holding → pick type → fill Holding Details → Save Holding
SAY: (Bitcoin) For Bitcoin, you enter the quantity — how much BTC — never dollars. And you enter your total cost basis if you know it. If you don't know it, leave it blank. The app never invents a basis — a missing one becomes homework in the tax module, not a made-up number.
SAY: (stocks) For stocks and ETFs, ticker and share count. Watch it fetch the live price.
SAY: (cash) Cash and savings, just the current dollar value.
⚠ NOTE: never type a price for a live asset — point at the dollar line updating under the quantity as you type
WATCH FOR: the live Total Portfolio Value card at the top climbing as you add

□ STEP 6 — Transactions → SKIP
CLICK: Skip for now (bottom bar)
SAY: We're building the plan right now, not reconciling the books. We're going to skip this — and the footnote on screen says it: you can use Update Transactions from the Dashboard anytime.

□ STEP 7 — Debts
CLICK: Add Debt → pick type → enter balance, rate, term. Repeat.
SAY: Every debt type comes with a default rate and term — a mortgage defaults to 7%, a credit card to 22%. These are just defaults, so overtype them with your actual numbers. Leave the monthly payment blank and the app estimates it from the balance, rate, and term — you'll see the estimate line, and you can edit it if your servicer payment is different.
⚠ NOTE: Auto Loan prefills 8.5% — say out loud that you're overtyping it
SAY: (pointing at the two tiles) Total Debt and Monthly Payments up top. In the debt module, every one of these is going to get a job — a 22% credit card and a 3.25% mortgage are not the same problem.
WATCH FOR: BTC-Backed Loan in the type list — worth a one-line mention: it's a first-class debt type with collateral tracking and live LTV, covered in the debt module

□ STEP 8 — Contributions
CLICK: enter annual amounts on the rows generated from your accounts; toggle Employer match on the 401(k)
SAY: These rows come from the accounts you just added. Enter what you're actually contributing today — your current reality, not the optimized version. Where money should go is the allocation module's job, and the app hands that to Savings Strategy after onboarding.
SAY: (401k match) Toggle the employer match, enter the match rate and the pay percentage it applies to, and it computes the estimated employer contribution for you.
SAY: (Roth) Quick note on Roth — that means you pay the tax now, and the growth and withdrawals come out tax-free later. And watch this IRA total meter: Traditional and Roth share one annual IRA limit per person, and the app warns you if you go over.

□ STEP 9 — Retirement Benefits (Social Security)
CLICK: enter monthly amount → pick a start-age card → spouse's → planning horizon chip
SAY: The field wants your monthly amount at Full Retirement Age — the helper points you to ssa.gov/myaccount to get your real estimate. There's an opt-out if you're planning without Social Security entirely. Then three cards for when you'll start: 62 early with about 30% less, 67 full, or 70 max with about 24% more.
SAY: (Plan through what age) I'd take the 90 chip. Longer horizons are more conservative — running out at 90 because you planned to 80 is exactly the failure the plan is designed against.

□ STEP 10 — Assumptions  (NOT skippable)
CLICK: actively pick a card (don't accept the pre-selection silently)
SAY: Four presets, and notice every single one of them declines over time — as Bitcoin gets larger, growth slows down. That's the whole logic from the assumptions lesson. Pick the one that lines up with the choice you made there. My default is Power law. Conservative if you want more headroom.
⚠ NOTE: Moderate comes pre-selected — say you're actively choosing, the app doesn't want a passive click here
NEVER READ: the per-card return percentages as durable facts — the cards are on screen

□ STEP 11 — Strategy Profile (10 required questions)
CLICK: answer all ten; Continue stays disabled until they're done
SAY: Ten questions, all required. Answer these honestly, not aspirationally — especially the one about what you'd do if Bitcoin dropped 50 to 80%. That question is the allocation module's stress test, asked in advance. Your honest answer here shapes what the app suggests later.

□ STEP 12 — Life Events
CLICK: quick-add an event → age, total cost, description → Add Event
SAY: Five quick-add types — job change, windfall, large purchase, college, expense change. Add the events you're likely to have. Not what-ifs — speculative stuff belongs in Scenarios later. And for multi-year events like college, the app spreads the cost across the years automatically.
WATCH FOR: Continue relabels to "Continue with N event(s)"

□ STEP 13 — Review  🎬 the payoff moment — let it breathe
CLICK: nothing — watch the spinner
SAY: (as the three spinner lines rotate) It's projecting your balances year by year... running 300 market paths... checking your spending target...
WATCH FOR: the EARLIEST RETIREMENT · AGE {X} label, then the ring
SAY: And there it is. This plan succeeds in that percentage of 300 market paths. One thing to know — this setup screen runs 300 paths to stay fast. The full Monte Carlo on the Plan page runs 1,000. Same math, bigger sample — we're going to run that one in Part B.
SAY: (scrolling the review) Your snapshot — net worth, Bitcoin, other assets, annual savings, time to retirement — and the modeled details underneath. And read this line at the bottom: nothing is locked in here. You can edit everything once you're in the app.
CLICK: Go to your dashboard
— END PART A —

============================================================
PART B — BASELINE LAP (separate session, ~15 min)
============================================================

INTRO:
SAY: Onboarding gave you a plan. Now we're going to make it accurate and get it ready to actually use. This takes about 15 minutes.

□ STEP 1 — Verify the baseline on the Dashboard
CLICK: expand each account group under Accounts & Holdings
SAY: Go through every account group and check every holding. Each row has an Edit link and a three-dot menu. If a number is wrong, fix it at the source — the holding itself. Don't nudge a total to make a mismatch go away. And you're not making this perfect, you're making it accurate.

□ STEP 2 — Add anything missing
CLICK: Add Account / Add Holding as needed
SAY: Anything that didn't make it in during onboarding goes in now. Same flow as before. Every real account should be in here — hardware wallet, exchange, brokerage, retirement, checking, savings.

□ STEP 3 — Update Transactions (show the dialog, don't run an import)
CLICK: Dashboard → Update Transactions
SAY: This dialog asks how you'd like to update transactions, and there are four answers: a linked account that pulls automatically, a downloaded CSV or Excel file from your exchange or brokerage, describing one transaction to the AI in plain language, or entering them yourself. You don't have to do this now — this button is always here on the Dashboard.
CLICK: close the dialog

□ STEP 4 — Shield your reserve holdings
CLICK: the shield icon on a non-cash reserve holding
SAY: Not all reserves are cash. If your reserve is sitting in short-term treasuries or a money market fund, click the shield icon on that holding and the app counts it as part of your safety net. Cash is counted automatically.

□ STEP 5 — Set assumptions on the Plan page
CLICK: Plan → gear icon (top right) → pick preset, set inflation → save
SAY: Same four presets from onboarding, plus custom overrides, inflation, and life expectancy. I'd pick the preset you chose in the assumptions lesson — mine's Power law. Inflation defaults to 3%; if you think it'll run hotter, 4 or 5 is the more conservative choice.

□ STEP 6 — Read your earliest retirement date
CLICK: point at the hero; check the chart toggle
SAY: This is your baseline freedom date — the year the plan says you could stop working under the assumptions you just set. Two chips under it: how many years away, and the spending it's funding.
⚠ NOTE: the chart toggle top-right — leave it on Today's $. A million dollars in 2050 isn't a million dollars in today's money. Today's $ is the honest view.

□ STEP 7 — Run the confidence ring
CLICK: Run Monte Carlo (under PLAN CONFIDENCE)
SAY: (during the 10-20 seconds) This is running a thousand market paths through your plan...
WATCH FOR: the ring + the verdict (Plan on track 80+ / needs review 60-79 / needs attention under 60) + the sampling range with its as-of date
SAY: Read the date and the ring together, the way we covered in the confidence lesson. The date tells you when. The ring tells you how sturdy that date is.

□ STEP 8 — Add likely life events
CLICK: scroll to Life events → Add event
SAY: You've already got at least one from onboarding. Add anything else that's likely — a home sale, an income change, a big planned expense. Likely events, not what-ifs.
WATCH FOR: the Recheck chip appearing next to the ring after adding — click it and re-run

□ STEP 9 — Save your baseline PDF
CLICK: Account menu → Report → Download PDF → Save as PDF
SAY: Put the year in the filename. The report module covers how to read this document like a planner — for now, just save it. It's your starting-point snapshot, and next year's before picture.

□ WRAP — the spot check (read off the screen, camera on the Dashboard)
SAY: Quick check before we're done. Savings rate green or high amber. Monthly room before debt positive. Portfolio value matches your own rough math. Debt and payments match what actually leaves your account. Shield on any non-cash reserve. A retirement date and a confidence ring you can read. And a PDF saved somewhere you'll find it. That's your baseline — everything else in this course builds on it.
— END PART B —
