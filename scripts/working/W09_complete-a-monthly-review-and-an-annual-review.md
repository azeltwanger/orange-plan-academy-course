#### Chapter 1 — Complete a quiet monthly review

**Show and do:** After 9.1, review actual account changes, current cash flow/Reserve, upcoming events and result freshness. Show an honestly quiet month and finish with no invented task. Timing may be measured but not promised universally.

**Narration:**

“We are checking what changed. These facts are current, the expected transfers are accounted for, and there is no new decision to make. A quiet month can end here.”

**Verify before recording:** Verify Home/needs-attention and actual source state. No mandatory number of actions, five-minute guarantee, repeated forced allocation change or lender monitoring reduced to monthly cadence.

**Member finish:** The member can repeat the factual check and knows when to stop.

#### Chapter 2 — Resolve one meaningful exception

**Show and do:** Use a synthetic discrepancy, such as a transfer counted on both sides without the source reduction or a contribution left uninvested. Inspect the actual cause and supported resolution. Compare resolved, deferred, uncertain and snoozed states.

**Narration:**

“The number is different from what we expected. Find which record explains it before adding another entry. A postponed question is still open. Once the fact is corrected, check the result it actually changes.”

**Verify before recording:** Verify contextual action and receipt; no fabricated balancing purchase/cash, silent deletion of evidence or double import. Protect holdings/history distinctions and current result identity.

**Member finish:** A supported correction or precise open task with its consequence understood.

#### Chapter 3 — Complete the annual system review

**Show and do:** After 9.2, use the prior dated plan and Annual Plan Refresh. Review spending/Reserve, Allocation, Debt, Tax, Custody, and family/insurance decisions within their actual screen owners. Inspect real debt-payoff and Reserve-completion milestones before routing new money.

**Narration:**

“Start with the choices already made and ask what changed. This payment ended, but before assigning it again, check the current cash needs and contribution plan. We are updating the system, not retaking the course or searching for a new strategy every year.”

**Verify before recording:** Actual supported scenario and annual-spending window only. Tax, security and collateral deadlines retain separate timing. No destructive funded-wallet test, automatic rebalance or assertion old beneficiary documents remain legally sufficient.

**Member finish:** One coherent annual record of reviewed choices, adopted changes and dated outside actions.

#### Chapter 4 — Handle a real change and a proposed response separately

**Show and do:** After 9.3, adopt one reviewed fictional income/event change at the correct date, then compare a strategy response. Read essential needs first, and the resulting cash flow and Plan update state. Reconcile a future event once actual.

**Narration:**

“This income changed in real life. Update that fact first. This contribution change is our proposed response. Keeping them separate lets us see whether the response solves the cash problem rather than hide it.”

**Verify before recording:** Verify actual save/update and freshness, event lifecycle and Current/Preview. No invented Recalculate control, assumed exact date or compensating return override. Immediate contract obligations do not wait for app support.

**Member finish:** Current reflects the fact and the response is an explicit choice.

#### Chapter 5 — Keep the next review usable

**Show and do:** Save a dated readable summary, Annual Plan Refresh and action list through supported paths. Identify the separate protected backup and verified restoration scope; include monthly/annual and specific urgent review triggers.

**Narration:**

“Save this summary so the next review starts with the decisions and reasons in front of you. Keep the plan backup separately and check that its restore process works before relying on it.”

**Verify before recording:** Actual export, save/reload and restore capability verified separately. A written reminder is not a calendar event or a completed outside action. No sensitive file sent or old version left as the current instruction.

**Member finish:** A maintained dated plan with a repeatable review path and honest implementation status.

### Readback and finish

An ordinary month, a material exception and the full annual loop can be followed without unnecessary strategy changes. The latest plan and action record form a usable starting point for the next review.
