### Run sheet

**Chapter 1, after 9.1:** time a genuinely quiet monthly pass. Home account/needs-attention check → Cash Flow baseline/reserve → upcoming events → current Plan receipt. Stop with zero to three actions. Do not promise the same timing for a broken account or major event.

**Chapter 2:** resolve one synthetic material exception using the supported contextual action and receipt. Distinguish resolved, snoozed, uncertain, and deliberately deferred tax history. Never fabricate an activity event to balance totals.

**Chapter 3, after 9.2:** annual loop across Home / Cash Flow / Plan / Protect. Include Allocation, Debt, Tax, Retirement Income, and all original deck review areas inside their current owners. Inspect one supported scenario and an annual spending review only when due. Tax and collateral monitoring have separate deadlines/cadences.

**Chapter 4, after 9.3:** adopt one actual income/event change; contrast it with a hypothetical strategy. Read resulting cash flow and Plan update state. No customer-facing Recalculate button invented for redesign.

**Chapter 5:** record decision reasons, outside actions, monthly/annual dates, and relevant tax/maturity/security reminders. Save readable report and encrypted data export only as supported, with restoration separately verified.

### Readback and finish

A quiet month stays short, a material change is handled honestly, and the annual plan has a complete review path. No automatic reassignment of a paid-off debt payment is presumed.
