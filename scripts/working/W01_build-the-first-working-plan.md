### Run sheet

**Stage:** a clean fictional demo and its private fixture records. Existing-user variant starts with existing accounts and reviews them instead of creating duplicates. Exact onboarding state, minimal required inputs, and initial result are verified on the release.

**Chapter 1, after 1.1:** show Home, Plan, Cash Flow, Protect, then Plan → Build & improve. Identify its one next task and the canonical owner it opens. State that this panel guides the work rather than storing another copy of each field. Show the blank/rough starting plan without a fabricated simulation percentage.

**Chapter 2, after 1.2:** add or review household owners and the initial account group. Demonstrate one manual cash account, one investment account, and a direct-Bitcoin custody location. Show account type, tax treatment, owner, and holdings as different fields. Use synthetic names only.

**Chapter 3:** demonstrate a supported linked or staged account and its actual capability receipt. Distinguish reported balance, investments, available activity, and relevant purchase details. Do not infer coverage from institution name. No live credentials, paid-refresh demonstration on navigation, or unsupported Fully synced claim.

**Chapter 4:** show the D65 balance-only investment example. A positive total with no trustworthy holdings is Holdings needed, not cash. Use Add investment to identify quantity/current value and supported classification. Preserve unknown acquisition date/basis and do not create a fake purchase. Retirement holdings do not require taxable-history reconstruction to be entered.

**Chapter 5:** inspect one current holding and one supported transfer/history situation. Establish current position once; history corroborates it. Show Add a purchase only for an actual acquisition. A self-custody transfer retains ownership quantity and known history. If the release cannot perform the transfer safely, hold this chapter rather than use sale-and-repurchase substitutes.

**Chapter 6, after 1.3:** enter income by person/source, explain gross/net/business conventions, living spending, tax-payment inputs, current debts, and employee contributions. Reconcile the fixture's cash-flow bridge. The $4,000 tax provision is explicitly a teaching assumption; do not portray it as the app's computed liability. Demonstrate that debt terms are edited in Debt even when Cash Flow summarizes payments.

**Chapter 7:** review planned retirement timing, initial spending, horizon, and available benefit estimates. Unknown benefit amounts stay explicitly provisional. Do not invent SSA or pension quotes for the household.

**Chapter 8, after 1.4:** read active assumptions and units. Open one supported lower-growth Preview and show what remains Current. Verify holding classification for spot Bitcoin funds versus operating companies. Save only the intended baseline. No old default CAGR values carried over from the deck.

**Chapter 9, with 1.5:** complete the minimum required facts, wait for the approved update path, and read the actual result. Show percentage headline, exact count beneath, horizon, planned date, earliest modeled date, and freshness. Record input/receipt identity. If no valid full result is available, retain the capture hold rather than stage a winning number.

**Chapter 10:** demonstrate one Ask question from 0.2 using the now-populated plan. Trace the explanation to an actual input/result. Verify market-report and AI-export availability before showing them; omit unavailable features from the final cut.
