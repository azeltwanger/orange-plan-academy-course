# WALKTHROUGH 10.4 — run the annual review in Orange Plan (screen share)

**One session, 6 steps (~8 min final: timed monthly pass + six-area lap)**

**Before recording:**
□ A couple of transactions ready to enter · ≥1 linked account with something FLAGGED (else the AI beat's button won't render)
□ Last year's report open in another tab if you have one
□ ⚠ 18-month/$120k reserve target is NOT settable from the UI (Target months = None/1/3/6/12) — seed it in the plan data, don't click for it
□ Visible timer ready

---

**Open:** "Two rhythms on camera: one timed monthly pass, under five minutes honestly, then the six-area annual lap, about 40 seconds per screen."

## □ 1 · The monthly pass — TIMED, LIVE
**Start the timer → Cash Flow → This month**
- surplus verdict: "You have $X/mo left to put to work" / deficit: "Spending runs $X/mo ahead of income"
- flow rows: Income · Taxes · Living · Debt payments
- **Verify spending → Review → By month tab**
- **Routing · waterfall order** — leave alone unless something real changed
- ⚠ four inputs, only ACTUAL changes get entered — "the monthly pass isn't a re-planning session; a steady month is 60 seconds"
- ⚠ deficit mode: routing block → "This month has no surplus to allocate…" — honest read, the pass still happened

## □ 2 · Enter transactions
**Dashboard → Update Transactions → dialog** (⚠ on Dashboard, NOT Cash Flow — budget the hop in the timer)
- four choices: A linked account · A downloaded file · Describe one transaction to AI · I'll enter them myself
- file path → which account? → **review step before anything saves** · footnote: checked against earlier imports
- ⚠ "nothing imported enters the plan silently — everything waits in review until you approve"

## □ 3 · AI: Explain review items
**Account menu → Accounts → Explain review items** (⚠ only renders when something's flagged)
- sub-line: "{n} institutions · {n} accounts · {n} needs review · last sync {date}"
- asks: "Which synced item looks missing or wrong if these review counts do not match the page?"
- click → STOP TALKING until it lands → read one flagged item back, agree/disagree
- ⚠ "it explains the queue. It does not approve anything."

## □ 4 · The annual lap — six screens, ~40 sec each (reading, not editing; fixes belong to a separate session)
**Spending & Reserve · Plan → Income**
- Review annual update → tiles Prior / After inflation / 80% target / Saved target → Apply annual update
- reserve read: **Reserve buffer strip** "{N} yrs · without selling investments"
- refill by hand on the L3 rule: good year → $120k (18 mo) · cut year → stop ~12 of 18 (~$80k) · target never moves
- ⛔ don't invent status words — no "Refilling/Paused/Critical" exists in the app. Read the tiles and strip as they are.

**Allocation · Strategy → Allocation**
- Funding your timeframes → Reserve/Bridge/Legacy badges Funded/Behind · drift line "All assets are inside the {N}% drift band"
- re-ask the stress question at TODAY'S balance, not last year's

**Debt & LTV · Strategy → Debt**
- Debt-to-assets ("caution above {N}%") · Debt-to-income ("high-risk above {N}%") · tooltip "Ratio bands"
- BTC loan: severity chips "near margin call / margin call / liquidation zone" + "A {N}% drop triggers…" sentence
- ⚠ no "loan cushion" label on this page — "cushion" exists in ONE place: the report's BTC-loan gauge (Module 10)

**Tax · Strategy → Tax**
- card **Moves this year** (eyebrow "Modeled, not advice"): Harvest gains · Harvest losses · Roth conversion
- ⚠ this one runs BEFORE year-end — in January it's a plan-next-year read, not a do-it-now action

**Custody · Protect**
- Needs attention → Security checklist "{n} of {n} for your tier" · groups Hardware/Distribution/Legal/Access after death
- the one annual item: **"Full recovery process tested end-to-end"** — unchecked = candidate for the three actions

**Estate · Protect**
- 5 segments → "{n} of 5 essentials in place"
- switch row (armed): "warns at 90 days · {n} contacts" + "{n} days left" → **Check in** — "the annual proof of life"

## □ 5 · Close the year: report + backup
**Account menu → Report → Download PDF** — year in the filename ("next year's before picture")
**Settings → Data & Privacy → Data & backups → Backup & Restore → Export Plan**
- ⚠ plaintext passphrase prompt — off-camera or visible throwaway · "backup passphrase, not a wallet passphrase"
- toast "Plan exported" · ⚠ "the review isn't done until both files are on disk, dated"

## □ 6 · Schedule both rhythms
- ⚠ no native scheduler — calendar app ON CAMERA: monthly day (first Saturday, recurring) + annual month (January, recurring)
- bonus rhythm: the 90-day switch email is a built-in reminder
- close: "confidence only flips to Recheck needed when plan INPUTS change. A moving price doesn't touch it — the app won't panic on a red candle, and neither should you."

## □ WRAP — spot check
- timer under 5 min ("most months are quieter than this one") · nothing touched that shouldn't be — name what you never opened
- reserve at target or rebuilding on the rule (bad: half target 2 years running) · allocation passes the stress question at today's number
- ratios in band, no loan a 50% drawdown liquidates · tax window used or deliberately passed before year-end
- one recovery proven · letter the family could act on today, switch armed
- **output = 1-3 actions, finishable before next month's pass** — "longer lists are how reviews stop happening" → END
