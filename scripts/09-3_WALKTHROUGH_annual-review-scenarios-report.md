# PART A — the two rhythms

*The timed monthly pass, then the six-area annual lap. Ends with this year's
report saved and the backup exported.*

---

## □ A1 · The monthly pass — TIMED, LIVE

**DO** Start the timer → Cash Flow → **This month**

**SEE** Surplus verdict *"You have $X/mo left to put to work"* — or deficit *"Spending runs $X/mo ahead of income"*

**SEE** Flow rows: Income · Taxes · Living · Debt payments

**DO** **Verify spending → Review → By month** tab

**DO** **Routing · waterfall order** — leave it alone unless something real changed

**⚠** Four inputs, and only ACTUAL changes get entered. The monthly pass isn't a re-planning session. A steady month is 60 seconds.

**⚠** In deficit mode the routing block reads *"This month has no surplus to allocate…"* That's an honest read, and the pass still happened.

---

## □ A2 · Enter transactions

**DO** Dashboard → **Update Transactions** → dialog

**⚠** It's on the Dashboard, NOT Cash Flow. Budget the hop into the timer.

**SEE** Four choices: A linked account · A downloaded file · Describe one transaction to AI · I'll enter them myself

**DO** File path → which account? → **review step before anything saves**

**SEE** Footnote: checked against earlier imports

**⚠** Nothing imported enters the plan silently. Everything waits in review until you approve it.

## □ A4 · The annual lap — six screens, ~40 sec each

**⚠** This is reading, not editing. Fixes belong to a separate session.

### Spending & Reserve — Plan → Income

**DO** **Review annual update** → tiles Prior / After inflation / 80% target / Saved target → **Apply annual update**

**SEE** Reserve read: **Reserve buffer** strip *"{N} yrs · without selling investments"*

**⚠** Refill by hand on the L3 rule: good year → $120k (18 months) · cut year → stop around 12 of 18 (~$80k). A bad market doesn't change the target.

**⚠** Don't invent status words. There is no "Refilling/Paused/Critical" in the app. Read the tiles and the strip as they are.

### Allocation — Strategy → Allocation

**SEE** Funding your timeframes → Reserve/Bridge/Legacy badges Funded/Behind · drift line *"All assets are inside the {N}% drift band"*

**⚠** Re-ask the stress question at TODAY'S balance, not last year's. That's the point of doing it annually.

### Debt & LTV — Strategy → Debt

**SEE** Debt-to-assets (*"caution above {N}%"*) · Debt-to-income (*"high-risk above {N}%"*) · tooltip *"Ratio bands"*

**SEE** BTC loan severity chips: near margin call / margin call / liquidation zone · plus the *"A {N}% drop triggers…"* sentence

**⚠** There's no "loan cushion" label on this page. "Cushion" exists in exactly one place — the report's BTC-loan gauge, later in this module.

### Tax — Strategy → Tax

**SEE** Card **Moves this year** (eyebrow *"Modeled, not advice"*): Harvest gains · Harvest losses · Roth conversion

**⚠** This one runs BEFORE year-end, which is the whole reason the review is scheduled for November. Run the lap in January and the tax card is a plan-next-year read, not a do-it-now action.

### Custody — Protect

**SEE** Needs attention → Security checklist *"{n} of {n} for your tier"* · groups Hardware / Distribution / Legal / Access after death

**⚠** The one annual item is **"Full recovery process tested end-to-end."** Unchecked makes it a candidate for the three actions.

### Estate — Protect

**SEE** Five segments → *"{n} of 5 essentials in place"*

**SEE** Switch row (armed): *"warns at 90 days · {n} contacts"* + *"{n} days left"* → **Check in**

**⚠** That's the annual proof of life.

## □ B3 · Read the comparison

**DO** Comparison section → chip with the scenario name → **vs your plan**

**SEE** Headline row, three tiles: **Retire at** · **Spending** · **Success rate**

**DO** Expand **Full comparison metrics**

**SEE** Net worth at retirement · net worth at age 90 · lifetime taxes paid · effective tax rate · withdrawal rate at retirement · depletion age · earliest retirement age · BTC at retirement · BTC at life expectancy

**SEE** Each row shows baseline · scenario · difference — matching rows read **no change**

## □ B4 · Run the Monte Carlo — let it breathe

**DO** **Run Monte Carlo** (comparison section)

**SEE** Spinner + phase label

**⚠** Until this runs, the success rate isn't a real comparison. The chart updates instantly off the projection, but confidence has to actually simulate.

**DO** Read the before/after success rate out loud

## □ B5 · Build the custom one

**DO** **Custom scenario** (top right) → dialog **Create scenario**

**SEE** Step 1: Choose scenario type → seven cards: Life events · Retirement · Savings · Debt · Tax · Markets · Advanced

**⚠** Pick the specific type, NOT Advanced. Advanced is every control at once, and it's for real multi-part scenarios only.

**DO** **Name** + **Description**

**SEE** The placeholder changes with the type (Retirement → *"e.g., Retire at 55"*; Tax → *"e.g., Roth conversion window"*)

**⚠** Name it after the QUESTION, not the answer. "Retire at 55" works. "Better plan" doesn't.

**DO** Change ONE variable → Save

**⚠** If I change three things and it comes back worse, I've learned nothing.

## □ C10 · The 15-minute household plan review

*The single most-requested thing across the client calls. The report is the
agenda; you are not teaching your spouse Bitcoin, you are showing them the
plan. If you don't have a spouse, do this with whoever would get the call.*

**DO** Sit down with the report open and answer seven questions out loud, in order. Time it on camera.

1. **What do we spend?**
2. **How much cash protects us, and for how long?**
3. **Where does the next dollar go?**
4. **When does work become optional?**
5. **How does retirement actually get funded?** (spending, minus durable income, equals the portfolio-funded gap, drawn in the saved account order)
6. **Who do we call if something happens to me?**
7. **Where do the non-secret planning documents live?**

**⚠ Say what this is not.** It is not a Bitcoin conversation. The other person does not have to share the thesis to run the plan. They need to know what to do, and where things are.

**⚠** If you cannot answer one of the seven in a sentence, that is the gap, and it points at exactly one module. Go back to that module rather than explaining harder.

**⚠** Fifteen minutes is the target, and it is achievable because the report is doing the work. If it runs to an hour, the report isn't finished.
