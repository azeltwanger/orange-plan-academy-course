# 11.4 · WALKTHROUGH — Walk your report

**Screen capture · 10 steps · ~8 min final** — the capstone read, in planner order: **position → trajectory → risk → actions**

> **DO** = click path · **SEE** = point at this · **⚠** = don't get this wrong
> Narrate in your own words. Nothing here is scripted.

---

## Before you record

- [ ] Plan warm, right after a monthly pass · at least one saved scenario · a fresh confidence check run (three report sections are conditional on these)
- [ ] **Reading order ≠ page order.** The report leads with trajectory; position is section FIVE. You will jump, and you should say so
- [ ] Spending band renders only with explicit bands — otherwise you get the *"Run a fresh confidence check…"* line. Run the check, then generate
- [ ] Conditional sections: *"If Bitcoin follows a different path"* (needs a saved scenario) · *"Your next steps"* · *"What these terms mean"*

---

## □ 1 · Open + name the reading order

**DO** Account menu (hamburger, aria *"Account menu"*) → **Report**

**SEE** Header: *"{Prepared for}"* eyebrow · title · **Download PDF** top-right

**⚠** The label is "Download PDF," not "Print" — it fires the print dialog underneath.

**⚠** Say the frame up front: nine modules assembled into one document, read the way a planner reads it — position, trajectory, risk, actions. The page isn't in that order, so we're going to jump.

---

## □ 2 · "Are you on track" — TRAJECTORY (section 1)

**SEE** Confidence ring + age stat + earliest line

**⚠** Read the date and the ring together. Neither one means anything alone.

**⚠** Green at 80+ is healthy. A plan that only survives at 99% confidence is one built on optimism — 100 was never the goal.

**⚠** If one is fine and the other isn't, that disagreement is where the signal lives.

---

## □ 3 · "Where you stand today" — POSITION (jump to section 5, deliberately)

**SEE** Net worth + quarter delta + allocation donut + ledger groups

**⚠** The first question of the whole read: is this true?

**⚠** Stale balance → STOP. Fix at the source, regenerate. A review of wrong numbers is worthless — don't read around it.

---

## □ 4 · "What you can spend" + "How retirement gets funded" (sections 3–4)

**SEE** Big number + band: **Lean decade / Your plan / Strong decade** · funding stages + legend

**⚠** Your spending is a band, not a number. A floor to fall back to, a ceiling as permission.

**⚠** Floor / plan / ceiling are COURSE names, not on-screen words. Bridge them out loud: the band the app calls Lean decade is the floor we've been talking about.

---

## □ 5 · "How your plan plays out" + "If Bitcoin follows a different path" — RISK (section 2 + conditional ~9)

**SEE** Year-by-year area chart with retirement + RMD markers

**SEE** Scenario delta bars (*"Safe annual spending. Baseline highlighted"*) + matrix

**⚠** Ask ONE question of it: would I still be okay? Not which line is prettiest.

**⚠** No saved scenarios means this section is absent entirely — that's why the pre-flight said save one.

---

## □ 6 · "Tax roadmap" + "Allocation" (sections 8 + 6)

**SEE** Tax timeline · allocation drift bars · BTC loan card with the gauge **"Liquidation risk" / "Cushion"**

**⚠** Bring this page to your CPA. It turns the tax conversation into a document read.

**⚠** "Cushion" exists HERE, on the report gauge only — not on Strategy → Debt. Match your language to the label on screen.

---

## □ 7 · "Protecting what you've built" (section 7)

**SEE** Readiness segment bar (*"{n} of 5 essentials"*) + ledgers **Estate** / **Insurance and access**

**⚠** This is the scoreboard for the custody and estate modules. The question is whether your family could act on this today.

**⚠** "Insurance and access" does NOT track policies — the rows come from Beneficiaries and Executor items, and Protect marks insurance "Coming soon." Name that once so nobody trusts it as a coverage read.

---

## □ 8 · "Your next steps" + "Assumptions & methodology" — ACTIONS (sections 10–11)

**SEE** Numbered steps · assumptions grid · footer *"Prepared with Orange Plan"*

**⚠** Two lines out loud: three actions, not thirty. And: if you can't defend an assumption out loud, change it and regenerate.

---

## □ 9 · AI — Review scenario

**DO** Scenarios → select a saved scenario → **Review scenario**

**⚠** It renders only with a scenario selected and the editor closed.

**⚠** Use it when a scenario needs plain English before you hand the document to a spouse, a CPA, or an adult child.

---

## □ 10 · Save the artifact

**DO** Report → **Download PDF** — year in the filename, e.g. `orangeplan-report-2027.pdf`

**⚠** Close on this: it's your after picture for this year and your before picture for next year. One report per year, for as long as you have a plan.

---

## □ WRAP — spot check off the screen

- [ ] Position matches reality (the read IS the audit)
- [ ] Date + ring read as a pair
- [ ] A band, not a point, with all three labels explained
- [ ] Bitcoin-path section actually read, with "would I still be okay?" answered
- [ ] Protection lines green, or the owning module named
- [ ] Next steps ≤ 3, every assumption defensible
- [ ] PDF on disk with the year in the filename

**⚠** Five years of before-pictures show you what actually moved and what only felt like it did.

**END**
