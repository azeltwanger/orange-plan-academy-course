# WALKTHROUGH 4.6 — route it in Orange Plan (screen share)

**One session, 10 steps (~16 min capture)**

**Before recording:**
□ 401(k) owner has a **salary income source** entered (else Fill to match is disabled)
□ Surplus routed into **Step 3** of the waterfall (contribution rows only appear with a surplus)
□ Conviction tier + target range from 4.1 written on a card

---

**Open:** "You've picked your tier, your buckets, and your waterfall. This walkthrough puts it in the app so every future contribution follows the plan on its own."

## □ 1 · Reality check on Dashboard
**Dashboard → expand account groups**
- sublines carry tax labels: Pre-Tax · Roth · HSA · Tax-Free · Real Estate
- stale quantity → three-dot → Edit holding — "everything downstream is decoration if the donut lies"
- flip the header **$ / ₿ toggle** → read net worth as a Bitcoin number → sit with it → flip back
- "neither denomination is wrong. Both are honest." (toggle is app-wide)

## □ 2 · Three timeframes + badges
**Strategy → Allocation → Funding your timeframes**
- rows: **Reserve** (now) · **Bridge** (before 59½) · **Legacy** (long) — badge each: **Funded / Behind**
- strip above: "Assigned accounts · projected" vs "Projected values" (= not sorted yet, numbers are a preview)
- badge reads: Reserve Behind → fix FIRST (rung 1) · Bridge Behind → route to taxable/HSA/529 · Legacy Behind → retirement wrappers
- ⚠ classic misfit: **Reserve Behind + Legacy Funded** = volatile long-term asset doing bucket-1 work

## □ 3 · Sort accounts into buckets
**Funding your timeframes → Assign accounts → dialog "Sort accounts into buckets"**
- drag: cash → Reserve · earmarked brokerage/529 → Bridge · HSA → Bridge (or Legacy if Roth-like) · 401(k)/IRA/Roth → Legacy · Bitcoin → Legacy
- footer counter: "N accounts still unsorted" → **"All accounts sorted"**
- **Done** → 👀 watch the Funded/Behind badges update live

## □ 4 · Save your target mix
**Allocation → Your target mix → Edit targets / Set target mix**
- presets: Bitcoin-forward · Balanced · Conservative · Build your own — pick the tier you can defend
- must read **✓ 100% allocated** or it won't save
- set **drift band** ("Flag drift beyond ±_%") — default ±5, "pick a number you'd actually act on"
- **Save target** · note on screen: "You don't assign accounts here…" — app records YOUR number, doesn't prescribe one

## □ 5 · How the target gets used
**Stay on Your target mix**
- new savings with no named holding route by this target · change it → plan re-solves
- "the number you just saved runs the waterfall from tomorrow forward"

## □ 6 · Configure the 401(k) row — ⚠ SLOW DOWN
**Cash Flow → Routing → Step 3 Contributions → 401(k) row** (mode pill: fixed / fill to match / max / leftover)
- **mode → Fill to match** → prints "targets $X/yr" · beside it: annual limit $X / "· at annual limit"
- **match fields:** Match rate _% · up to _% of pay → 👀 "captures $X/mo"
- greyed "Fill to match is off" tells you the missing prerequisite (salary source, or match fields)
- **tax treatment:** Traditional / Split / Roth — two questions in order: can it hold what I want to own → which wrapper treats it best
- **how it invests:** Current mix / Set mix / Choose holdings (+ Add holding tags "· new holding")
- missing account type → **+ Add account type** at bottom

## □ 7 · Route with AI
**Routing section header → Route with AI** — reads cash flow + contribution plan + debts
- message: "Route my next dollar" · asks: current available amount, or test a different amount?
- answer → wait → read one surfaced item, agree/disagree
- ⚠ "reviews and explains. Does not decide."

## □ 8 · Compare in Scenarios
**Scenarios → Custom scenario → save current setup** (lives under Your scenarios)
- run **What if... → Bear Market** (BTC −60%, stocks −40%, 3-yr recovery)
- ask the stress question on camera: "could you sit through this and do nothing?" — no → lower target in step 4, re-run

## □ 9 · Review portfolio (AI)
**Strategy → Allocation → Review portfolio** (page header)
- reads portfolio + saved target + funding timeframes
- its question IS the module: "What would force you to sell investments during a deep Bitcoin drawdown?"
- answer out loud · if it's "a lifestyle expense I forgot to fund" → fix Reserve/Bridge, not the mix
- ⚠ won't tell you what your Bitcoin % should be

## □ 10 · Confirm the record
**Back to Your target mix**
- target + drift band saved — "your number, on the record"
- waterfall order Reserve → Extra debt → Contributions, runs monthly

## □ WRAP — spot check
- donut matches reality · "All accounts sorted" · three Funded badges (any Behind = next action)
- nothing Legacy-volatile parked in Bridge money · mix at 100% with a band you'd act on
- "captures $X/mo" shows the FULL match — anything less is free money on the table, visible on screen
- Bear Market survives + honest yes to the sit-still question
- "the waterfall runs monthly from here. Your job is to fund it; the app does the routing." → END
