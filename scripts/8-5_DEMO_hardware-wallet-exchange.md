## □ 1 · Hardware wallet: the 6 steps (~5:30)
1. **Buy direct from manufacturer** — never used, never third-party
2. **Generate a new wallet on the device** — seed from the device's own randomness; never type a seed from anywhere else
3. **Write the seed offline + set PIN** — show the ACT (hand on paper/steel), never the words · no PIN pattern on screen
4. **Send a small test transaction** — trivial, real on-chain record
5. **Wipe the device** — factory reset, on purpose. "The scary step — the whole reason we're on a throwaway."
6. **Restore from seed** — type the words back from the offline copy → balance + history reappear
- say when the balance reappears: **"only now has this device earned real money"**
- if the restore fails on camera → retake (and the point makes itself)
- ⚠ three moments the camera never sees directly: seed display · PIN entry · restore input

## □ 2 · Exchange hardening (~2:30) — name vendors as examples, not endorsements
1. **Strong unique password** — password manager fine here (it's not a wallet)
2. **2FA off SMS** → authenticator app or physical key — "SIM-swap is the boring attack that keeps working"
3. **Secure the email account FIRST** — the master key; every reset flow routes through it
4. **Withdrawal delays + allow-listing on** — no fast path out; takeover can only send to your own destinations
- frame line: "most losses are account takeovers, not broken cryptography"

## □ 3 · The never-list (~1:00) — camera, NO screen
- Never type seed words into a computer, phone, website, screenshot, or AI chat.
- Never buy a used or third-party device.
- Urgency is the red flag. Close the app and slow down.
- No real company ever asks for your seed.
