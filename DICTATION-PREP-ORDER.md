# Orange Plan Academy — chronological dictation prep order

Use this as the recording and dictation index.

## Which file to open

- **Austin-dictated lesson:** open the canonical file in `scripts/`.
- **Lesson Austin has not dictated:** open the matching section in `DICTATION-PREP-CORE.md` or `DICTATION-PREP-ADVANCED.md` first.
- **Walkthrough or demo:** use the DO / SEE / warning sheet in `scripts/`; do not dictate it as a teleprompter video.
- **Research detail:** open the longer canonical script only when a number, legal qualification, technical caveat, or example needs to be checked.

After Austin dictates a lesson, his words replace the working draft. Clean transcription mistakes and verify facts, but do not compress useful repetition or replace his planning judgment.
