# Course structure — action, judgment, separate implementation

Austin's current direction is: **do this, how to think about it, how to implement in a walkthrough video**. The slide steps supply the action sequence. Current scripts retain 25 main lesson families and eight conditional recordings, with short ordered actions inside each family.

The main path builds a first working plan early, then improves cash flow and Reserve, debt, allocation and contribution routing, tax, retirement income, custody, family protection and maintenance. Final review joins those decisions into a plan the household can explain and follow. The first retirement date is provisional and revisited as the plan improves.

## Teaching

Open with the task. Explain only the reasoning needed to perform it. Use a source example where it makes the choice understandable. Name the information to gather, the decision criterion, and what the member will have at the end. A conceptual slide such as debt's purpose belongs inside the relevant decision; it is not automatically a separate lecture.

Text overlays reinforce the useful number, choice or step. Their cues are exact spoken phrases, allowing the editor to place them after Austin records. Speech and production notes remain separate.

## Implementation

Film app walkthroughs separately. Each chapter implements the paired teaching through one visible operation or tightly connected group of operations, with complete spoken narration and an observable finish check. Ten working-session files organize the takes; members encounter the relevant chapters beside their teaching lesson.

PR #227's accepted contracts govern the intended app paths. Current facts, a chosen strategy Preview and a Scenario have different save behavior. Account linking, purchase history and balances have different evidence. A supported calculation, a saved intention and an outside action must remain distinct. See [future-app alignment](V1-COURSE-ALIGNMENT.md).

## Completion and confidence

The member uses their own Orange Plan. No required quiz, alternate practice portfolio, submission or community post. A completed step produces a saved fact, an explained decision, a specific outside action or a clearly identified unresolved requirement.

The finished plan includes understandable assumptions, a funding source for the important years, responses to meaningful changes, an accessible family process and a review schedule. The course does not promise a retirement date or a particular simulation percentage.

Maintenance has three return points: the quiet monthly review, the annual review and a material change or discrepancy. A quiet month can end without a strategy change.
