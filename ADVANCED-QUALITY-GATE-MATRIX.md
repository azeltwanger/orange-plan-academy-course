## Gate definitions

### Ready for Austin review

A lesson may enter Austin's first review when:

- the gate, question, example, decision, and return to Core are stable,
- the example arithmetic or process state is versioned,
- app ownership and proof ownership are named,
- primary-source verification is complete,
- and every unresolved passage is visible in the hold register.

All 18 current Advanced lessons meet this standard.

### Ready for final approval

A lesson may be marked `AUSTIN APPROVED` only when:

- Austin has approved the judgment and example,
- the current app/agreement/quote/device/document language is verified,
- applicable external claim responses are returned and applied,
- script and lesson text match after corrections,
- and Austin completes one clean final read.

### Ready to film

In addition to final approval:

- the primary visual uses accepted evidence,
- the versioned demonstration is ready or deliberately unnecessary,
- no blocking pilot misunderstanding remains,
- and every changing fact has a maintained-reference owner.

## Known honest holds

Advanced is **not** professionally reviewed merely because the packets and source research are complete.

The remaining human/evidence gates are:

1. specific lender agreement and lending/legal review,
2. CPA/tax review,
3. current healthcare quote and qualified coverage/Medicare review,
4. employer-plan and account-access records,
5. device/provider-specific custody review and practice proof,
6. Colorado estate/trust and tax review,
7. provider ownership/beneficiary acceptance,
8. deployed app receipts,
9. high-risk learner pilot,
10. Austin's final clean read.

## Release rule

Clear lessons individually. A returned CPA packet does not clear lending law, healthcare coverage, custody safety, or estate authority. A successful app calculation does not clear any external implementation.
