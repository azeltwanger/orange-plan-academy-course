# Debt jobs and financing comparison

Inventory each debt: balance, required payment, APR, fixed/variable status, term/reset/maturity, collateral, guarantees, repayment source and source document date. Unused credit is access, not an owned asset.

Your monthly lens: required payments ÷ gross monthly income. Your balance-sheet lens: total debt ÷ included gross assets. State what is excluded. Stress assets and income separately; a ratio alone does not prove payments are affordable.

Reed included assets $1,996,000; debt $444,500; scoped net worth $1,551,500; debt/assets about 22.27%. Vehicle and equipment/business values are omitted while their debts remain. The deliberately partial stress leaves $1,217,200 of included assets and about 36.52% debt/assets. This is not a full worst case.

Give each debt one job, a reason for keep/pay-extra/refinance/pay-off/review, and a trigger. The adopted Reed plan gives the card $1,200 extra; the final claim must return to the cash-flow bridge.

LTV = full debt ÷ collateral value. With fixed debt and an illustrative 80% liquidation threshold, 50% initial LTV reaches it after a 37.5% collateral-price fall; 25% initial reaches it after 68.75%. Accrued interest, fees, changing terms, access and counterparty failure can worsen the result. These are not safe levels or lender terms.
