# Maintain and explain the plan

Monthly date: ____  Annual review month: ____  Separate tax, maturity or collateral deadlines: ____

A quiet monthly pass checks changed accounts/activity, income/spending, expected events and the saved result's as-of state. Resolve one meaningful issue at a time. Record zero to three actions; zero is valid when nothing needs attention. Source-proven automatic updates do not require re-entering the same activity.

Annual review covers spending/reserve, allocation, all relevant debts and LTV, taxes, custody and family/estate arrangements. Update facts first. Compare strategy changes separately. Save the supported readable report and appropriate data backup; verify restoration rather than treating file creation as proof.
