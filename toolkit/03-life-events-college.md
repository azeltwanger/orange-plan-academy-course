Use school cost-of-attendance and net-price estimates. Grants/scholarships reduce the amount to fund; loans create repayment obligations. Check the actual borrower's current limits, terms and repayment capacity before depending on a loan.

Worked benchmark: $20,000 × four years = $80,000 commitment. For illustration, $29,000 of the household's $58,000 education resources is assigned to the older child; the other $29,000 remains for the younger child. Confirm actual beneficiaries before moving money. Gap: $80,000 − $29,000 = $51,000. Prefunding that gap over 60 months at flat costs and zero growth is $850/month.

This is a first comparison, not a complete education projection. Add cost inflation, payment dates, investment outcomes, taxable sales, future cash flow and overlap between children. A later saving start needs a new monthly calculation. During the Reed card-payoff phase there is no unassigned $850: the household must change a claim, source, commitment or timing.
