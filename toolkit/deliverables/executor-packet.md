# Executor Packet

Use with 8.1-8.3 and W08, then W10. This is a document index and coordination checklist. Its title does not appoint an executor or confer authority. A coordinator, legal representative and technical helper may be different people. Confirm the applicable documents and account procedures with the relevant institution and qualified professional.

Print and fill personal details privately. Keep this packet with protected household records. Do not include account numbers, credentials, wallet addresses, recovery words, keys, passphrases, PINs, xpubs, descriptors or exact recovery locations. Do not upload the completed packet to the course community or an AI tool.

Proposed coordinator for the teaching scenario: the available spouse. Backup, formal representative, attorney, tax professional and technical helper are not selected in the fixture. These roles must be resolved before the packet can be treated as a usable absence plan.

First coordination task: verify how the family obtains the formal documents and institution instructions. First financial task: check current bills and time-sensitive loan notices; never assume home equity, an unused credit line or a retirement balance is immediately spendable cash.
