# Heir Letter

Use with 8.3 and W08 chapters 3-4. This is a plain-language starting letter, not a will, power of attorney, beneficiary designation or set of wallet recovery instructions. Print and complete personal details privately. Keep secrets and exact recovery locations out of this letter and out of course submissions.

Contact ____________________ using the contact details we have already verified. Their job is to coordinate the next steps with ____________________. Our backup contact is ____________________. Our non-secret plan summary and document index can be found through ____________________. This is a reference to the coordination packet, not the location of wallet secrets. The packet explains our accounts, regular bills, open tasks and professional contacts.

### The first practical priorities

The person helping you should first check these time-sensitive bills, commitments or notices: _______________________________________________________________

Our ordinary bill-paying arrangements are described in ____________________. Do not assume that a displayed balance is money you are authorized or able to use. Verify ownership, access and any restrictions before moving it.

Ask ____________________ to help establish which formal documents and institution procedures apply. The intended family coordinator, appointed legal representative and technical helper can have different roles.

### Protect yourself while getting help

Move slowly on unfamiliar financial requests. Verify anyone offering help through a contact you already know. Never type recovery words into a website or share them in a message. Do not send wallet secrets, passwords or credentials to the community, an AI assistant or an unsolicited helper. Do not reset a funded wallet or attempt recovery from this letter alone.
