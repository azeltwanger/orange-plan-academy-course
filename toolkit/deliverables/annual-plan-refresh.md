# Annual Plan Refresh

Return here with your prior saved plan and action list. Use 9.2 and W09 for the full review, 6.8 for a retirement spending decision, and W10 to save and explain the result. Use the existing app records and worksheets; this page only records changes and next priorities.

A loan alert, legal or tax deadline, coverage transition or major life change may need attention before the annual review. This page does not replace those separate obligations.

## Filled fictional example

Alex and Morgan Reed. A conditional teaching rehearsal, not a report that a year has passed or a debt has been repaid.

**Trigger.** Suppose a future statement proves the card has been paid off. Verify the zero balance and the release of the actual payment first. If that evidence is absent, the prior card-payoff route remains in place.

**Cash-flow change.** In the frozen example, $405 of required payment plus $1,200 of extra payment releases $1,605 monthly. The $500 Reserve contribution continues until a separate review changes it. Confirm taxes, income, spending and other payments again; the historic teaching amounts are not automatically the new year's facts.

**Comparison.** Test the already-specified future route: $1,000 direct Bitcoin and $605 taxable stock fund. Keep account access, household allocation and event funding visible. Record the actual app result when available. No improvement in retirement age or success rate has been established here.

**Other reviews.** Recheck the reserve gap, the college commitment, the HELOC terms, healthcare timing, beneficiaries, recovery status and whether both spouses can use the starting instructions. The college saving benchmark is not an additional funded claim on the same $1,605 without a new affordability decision.

**Finish.** Save the revised plan only after the household chooses it, record provider changes separately, preserve a dated report and verified restoration process, and refresh the family packet. Those outputs, future dates and appointments remain unperformed in this teaching rehearsal.
