# Insurance coverage audit worksheet

Use with 8.4 and W08 chapter 5. Organize current records and questions for a licensed review. This worksheet does not choose a policy, interpret a contract or certify adequate coverage. Keep policy numbers, identification numbers, wallet recovery information, medical records and private quotes out of community or AI submissions. Use nicknames in this worksheet; keep source documents privately.

Finish check: every material risk has a known arrangement or an explicit open question; costs are reflected in the plan without double counting; important gaps have an owner and next step. Requested quotes and prepared questions are not coverage in force.

## Filled fictional example

Alex and Morgan Reed. No actual policy, quote, benefit limit or professional assessment exists in the source fixture. This is a completed issue inventory, not a completed coverage assessment.

| Risk | Source-supported concern | Next evidence / action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Loss of income or a key operator | One W-2 income and one variable self-employed income; two children | Gather employer benefits and private arrangements; identify what changes when work ends |
| Disability / business slowdown | Morgan's income can vary; equipment debt remains | Obtain actual coverage and business-loan terms; test the cash-flow interruption separately |
| Healthcare transition | Work-optional target precedes Medicare; no private quote supplied | Price actual available paths and dates with qualified help; do not treat $12,000 teaching cost as a quote |
| Home and vehicle | Residence, mortgage, HELOC and auto debt | Read actual policy limits, deductibles and relevant contract obligations |
| Liability | Household property and business activity | Ask a licensed professional which exposures and arrangements need review |
| Business / guarantees | Equipment debt; possible guarantee not verified | Obtain the signed agreement and relevant insurance records |

Current coverage verdict: unverified. The $4,000 monthly teaching tax provision and $12,000 retirement healthcare illustration do not establish insurance adequacy or actual household cost.

First action: Alex gathers employer benefit records; Morgan gathers business and private arrangements. Both bring the specific unanswered questions to the licensed professional. This is an illustrative assignment for the worksheet, not a claim the work has happened. Timing: before relying on coverage assumptions for a work transition.

No cancellation or policy purchase is directed by this example. Enter actual costs and transition dates only when verified; then check the saved plan.
