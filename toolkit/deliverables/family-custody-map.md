# Family Custody Map

Use with 7.1, 7.4 and W07; revisit with 8.2 and W08. This is a non-secret coordination document, not wallet recovery instructions. Print the blank pages and complete personal details privately by hand. Keep them in protected household records. Do not post completed pages in the community or send them to an AI tool.

Exclude recovery words, private keys, passphrases, PINs, passwords, account numbers, xpubs, descriptors, wallet addresses and exact recovery locations. Use account nicknames. Naming a helper does not establish legal authority.

First response: the available spouse coordinates using already-known contacts and the non-secret starting instructions. If neither can coordinate, the backup person must first be selected and briefed. No appointment or legal authority is inferred.
