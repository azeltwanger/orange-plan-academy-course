# Tax decision and professional handoff

Start with a specific question: a proposed sale, a conversion, a distribution or a current-year payment gap. Keep liability separate from how it is paid.

For a purchase record, preserve quantity, date, amount paid, fees as supported, account/wallet, transfers and remaining units. Unknown is unknown. Collect actual statements/exports and evidence before discussing a reconstruction with the tax professional; the course does not certify a guessed price window.

Verify actual unit identification and holding period before execution. A software HIFO setting alone is not filing evidence.
