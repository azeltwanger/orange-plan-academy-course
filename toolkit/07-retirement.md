The example $96,000 living + $12,000 healthcare − $40,000 gross income gives a rough $68,000 gap before tax/debt/event adjustments. It is not the final net portfolio draw.

Choose account withdrawal order separately from the investments sold. Compare sequential and blended approaches with the same spending and assumptions. A low-income window may support intentional Traditional distributions or conversions; verify special access before relying on it.
