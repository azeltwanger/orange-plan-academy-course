## Timeframe and wrapper

Reserve: near-term dependable funds. Bridge: approaching spending with its own flexibility. Forever: later money with time to remain invested. The original 0–3 / 4–10 / 10+ year picture is an illustration. Now / before59½ / after59½ concerns access and must be evaluated separately.

For each receiving dollar, record job/date, account/owner, permitted access, available investments, tax treatment, costs and intended holding. HSA / IRA / additional workplace saving is a comparison after the match; taxable early-access needs, menu or costs can change the order.

Traditional/Roth comparison uses equal pretax resources. At an illustrative 20% tax rate, $1,000 pretax in Traditional doubles to $2,000 and leaves $1,600 after that tax. $800 after-tax in Roth doubles to $1,600 on a qualified withdrawal. Future tax rates, eligibility, limits, access and the use of today's deduction change the comparison.
