# 9.2 · Stress tests, choices, and Current versus Preview

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
Scenarios answer one focused question, and a winning choice becomes a Preview beside the saved Current plan.

## The visual
Start with two columns:

- **Stress test — would the plan survive this?** Bear market; 50% drawdown after retirement.
- **Choice — should the household do this?** Retire earlier; spend less; move states.

Then transition to an aligned comparison:

    CURRENT                  PREVIEW
    790 of 1,000             824 of 1,000
    Planned: May 2032        Planned: May 2032
    Earliest: May 2030       Earliest: January 2030
    Spending: $120,000       Spending: $115,000

Highlight only the values that changed. One quiet action below reads **Save to plan**.

## Motion
Type the two scenario questions and examples first. Select one choice, then transform it into Current versus Preview. Reveal the Save-to-plan action without triggering it.
