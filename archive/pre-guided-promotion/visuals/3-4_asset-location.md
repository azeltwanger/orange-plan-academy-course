# 3.4 · Put the right holdings inside the right accounts

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
Asset location combines the job of the account, the tax wrapper, and the investments the account can actually hold.

## The visual
Three account columns:

- **Taxable / flexible** — accessible Bridge money
- **Tax-deferred** — long-term money with tax paid later
- **Tax-free** — long-run growth with qualified withdrawals tax-free

Across the bottom, add the filter that comes before placement:

**Can this account hold the Bitcoin exposure or other asset the plan calls for?**

Show three available forms without ranking them:

- Direct Bitcoin
- Bitcoin ETF
- No Bitcoin option

## Labels and data
Use a few illustrative holdings—Bitcoin, broad stock fund, cash, and a tax-inefficient income asset—and place each according to its job and the account menu. Avoid universal “best” labels.

## Motion
The account job appears first, the tax treatment second, and the available investment menu third. The holdings move only after all three are visible.
