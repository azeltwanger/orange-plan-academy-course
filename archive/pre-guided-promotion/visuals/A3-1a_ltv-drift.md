# A3.1 · Why LTV rises faster than price falls

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That the price is in the denominator, so the ratio accelerates.

## The visual
Two stacked tracks sharing an x-axis. Top: Bitcoin price falling. Bottom: LTV bar climbing, with fixed horizontal margin-call and liquidation lines. Same starting loan.

## Labels and data
Start 20% LTV. Show 70k → 29%, 50k → 40%, 30k → 67% margin call, 25k → 80% liquidation.

## Motion
**Key animation.** Price falls smoothly; LTV climbs faster and visibly accelerates. Each threshold line flashes once as it is crossed.
