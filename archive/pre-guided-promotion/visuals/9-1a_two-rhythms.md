# 9.1 · The two rhythms

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That maintenance is two small habits, not one big project.

## The visual
Two side-by-side loops. Monthly: small, four inputs, timed at under five minutes. Annual: larger, six areas, once a year. A shared rule beneath both: update the plan first.

## Labels and data
Four inputs and six areas named. Under 5 minutes on the monthly loop.

## Motion
Monthly loop spins fast twelve times, then the annual loop turns once — the cadence difference is the point.
