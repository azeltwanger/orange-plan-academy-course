## 0.2 How to use Ask in Orange Plan

*`TEACH` · ~5 min · PRE-DICTATION FILMING DRAFT*

In this lesson, I want to show you how I would use Ask while building and maintaining a financial plan.

Ask is a second set of eyes on the plan. Orange Plan does the calculations. Ask explains the result, points out information that may be missing or stale, compares choices, and takes you to the workspace where the decision belongs.

I would open it when a number does not make sense, when I want to understand what changed, or before I make a planning decision.

Useful questions include:

- What changed after I updated these numbers?
- Which inputs are affecting this result the most?
- Which information is missing, stale, or inconsistent?
- What should I review before I make this decision?
- Compare these two choices and explain the trade-offs in plain language.
- What should I model before I take this to my CPA, attorney, or insurance professional?

🎬 VISUAL — Ask opens from the global header, reads the current screen, and deep-links to the canonical workspace.

The current screen gives Ask useful context. On Cash Flow, it can help you check whether the income, spending, reserve, and usable surplus make sense. On Debt, it can surface the loans creating the most pressure or forced-sale risk. On Allocation, it can review the current mix, target, timeframes, and contribution routing. On Tax, it can help you frame a conversion or harvesting question. On Retirement Income, it can explain the simulation result, the spending gap, and the trade-offs between two strategies.

Ask can also guide you through Build & improve. It can tell you which information would make the plan more accurate, which planning area still needs a decision, and where to go next.

Any proposed planning change belongs in Preview. Current remains the saved plan. Preview shows the proposed result beside Current, including the simulation count, retirement dates, spending, taxes, or another outcome that materially changed. You decide whether to save the change.

That boundary matters because the AI should help you understand and compare a decision. The authoritative calculation still comes from the Orange Plan model, and the saved plan changes only after you approve it.

Ask is also useful before a professional conversation. It can organize the relevant facts and help you turn a broad concern into a specific question. The CPA still applies current tax law. The attorney still drafts and reviews the legal documents. The insurance professional still reads the actual policy and underwriting terms.

A daily Bitcoin market report can provide context without turning the plan into a trading system. Read it to understand price, liquidity, leverage, institutional activity, and important industry developments. Then ask whether any of that changed a planning fact or crossed a rule you already set. Most market days require no change to the plan.

Orange Plan also provides a privacy-scrubbed plan summary for use with an outside AI. Review the summary before sharing it. Use the encrypted backup only to restore Orange Plan data; keep that file out of every AI tool.

The security rule is simple: never enter a seed phrase, private key, wallet backup, passphrase, PIN, password, Social Security number, full account number, exact recovery location, or backup-file password into an AI chat. The plan can be reviewed without any of those secrets.

Use Ask when you need help understanding the decision in front of you. Keep the financial facts and calculations in Orange Plan, compare any proposed change in Preview, and save it only when the decision is yours.
