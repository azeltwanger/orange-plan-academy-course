## 9.1 Keep the plan current without rebuilding it every month

*`TEACH` · ~5 min · PRE-DICTATION FILMING DRAFT*

A financial plan stays useful when the facts stay current and the strategy changes only for a reason.

I use two rhythms: a short monthly pass and a complete annual review.

The monthly pass should take around five minutes in a quiet month.

Start on Home and check the current financial picture.

Review the accounts, holdings, recent activity, debts, and Needs Attention. A linked source can record exact posted purchases, sales, and uniquely matched internal transfers when Orange Plan can prove the event. Anything ambiguous becomes one focused question in Needs Attention.

That keeps the review small. Clean facts flow into the plan with provenance and a receipt. You spend your time on the exceptions that require judgment.

Three account truths remain separate:

- current balance and holdings;
- recorded activity;
- tax-detail coverage.

A fresh balance does not prove that every transaction or tax lot is complete. Read the status of each area before assuming the account is finished.

🎬 VISUAL — Home account row with three separate states: Balance and holdings · Activity · Tax details.

Then move through the four permanent destinations.

### Home

Confirm what is true now. Review accounts, activity, debt monitoring, and the one next item that needs attention.

### Cash Flow

Check income, living spending, taxes, debt payments, reserve funding, and saving. One unusual month can remain unusual. A repeated or permanent change belongs in the plan.

### Plan

Read the saved simulation result and its freshness state. Review any stale facts, open Build & improve work, and a strategy decision that genuinely needs attention. Keep proposed changes in Preview until you decide to save them.

### Protect

Confirm that custody, beneficiaries, trusted people, heir instructions, and the family process still reflect reality.

🎬 VISUAL — Monthly loop: Home → Cash Flow → Plan → Protect → 1–3 actions.

Finish the monthly pass with no more than one to three actions. A short list that gets completed keeps the plan moving. A quiet month is a successful review.

The annual review uses the same four destinations and goes deeper.

On Home, reconcile the major accounts, holdings, debt balances, and unresolved activity. Confirm that a balance checkpoint, recorded activity, and tax history have not been confused with one another.

On Cash Flow, review the spending baseline, reserve target, contribution routing, debt payments, and any future life event that changed.

On Plan, recalculate the simulation result, compare the planned retirement date with the earliest modeled date, review allocation, tax, debt strategy, retirement income, borrowing, and one focused scenario. Any proposed change stays in Preview until it earns a place in Current.

On Protect, prove one recovery, review provider and custody concentration, update beneficiaries and trusted people, confirm the heir letter, and review the legal and insurance work.

Then regenerate Your Plan and save the yearly PDF.

Market prices can change balances, LTV, taxes, and the simulation result. The strategy changes when a planning fact, threshold, or rule changes. A large drawdown may trigger a loan action, a spending review, or a Reserve decision because those rules were set in advance.

Choose the day of the month for the short review and the month for the annual review. Put both on the calendar. Orange Plan holds the plan; the calendar protects the habit.
