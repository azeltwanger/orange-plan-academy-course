## 1.4 WALKTHROUGH — Replace the quick estimate with real Foundation data

*`WALKTHROUGH` · narrate from the DO / SEE / ⚠ sheet*

> **V1 FILMING HOLD:** Record after the Slice 2 shell and Slice 3 Plan experience are visually stable. The durable sequence is locked; exact paths and labels require final verification.

## Before recording

- Fresh demo household with the quick setup complete
- Realistic household details, accounts, and current holdings ready
- No historical transaction reconstruction in this walkthrough
- No debt strategy, contribution routing, tax strategy, or retirement-income implementation yet

## 1 · Open Build & improve

**DO** Plan → Build & improve.

**SEE** the current state-dependent step: Build your plan or Make it more accurate.

**SAY** The course teaches the decision. Build & improve takes us to the source field that completes it.

## 2 · Verify household facts

**DO** Open Plan details → Household.

**CHECK** birth dates, spouse, filing status, state, dependents, planning age, and planned retirement date.

**SEE** planned retirement date and earliest modeled retirement date remain separate.

**⚠** Enter facts here. Strategy assumptions and what-if changes belong elsewhere.

## 3 · Verify accounts

**DO** Home → open each account.

**CHECK** account name, owner, account type, custody type where relevant, and tax treatment.

**SEE** account continuity is shown in separate dimensions: Balance and holdings · Activity · Tax details.

**SAY** A fresh balance does not prove that activity and tax history are complete.

## 4 · Verify current holdings

**DO** Account → Holdings.

**CHECK** current quantity or balance, asset identity, and any known current metadata.

**⚠** A current balance can be a checkpoint. Do not turn it into a purchase or invent cost basis.

## 5 · Leave historical activity for the owned workflow

**DO** Point to Account → Activity and Needs Attention without completing historical adoption.

**SAY** New exact activity and old history are different jobs. Module 5 owns cost basis and historical tax evidence. Later activity workflows preserve transfers as transfers and keep unknown basis unknown.

## 6 · Review the first result

**DO** Plan → Overview → Recalculate when available.

**SEE**:

- simulation count out of 1,000;
- through-age;
- planned retirement date;
- earliest modeled retirement date;
- result state: Preliminary, Current, Stale, or Unavailable;
- concise provenance such as accounts used and details that could change the result.

**SAY** This is the first useful Plan result. It remains Preliminary while later modules complete important facts and strategy decisions.

**⚠** Normal users use the Orange Plan standard of 800 successful simulations out of 1,000. Do not show a normal confidence-target control.

## 7 · Confirm the next course handoff

**DO** Return to Build & improve.

**SEE** the next accuracy task.

**SAY** Foundation is complete when the household, accounts, and current holdings are honest. Cash Flow is next because income, spending, reserve, and expected life events determine how much the plan can use.

## Foundation checkpoint

- Household facts are current.
- Planned and earliest modeled retirement dates are understood.
- Accounts have the correct owner, type, and tax treatment.
- Current holdings are honest checkpoints.
- Activity and tax-detail completeness are read separately.
- The first Plan result is read with its truthful state.
- No later-module strategy was entered early.
