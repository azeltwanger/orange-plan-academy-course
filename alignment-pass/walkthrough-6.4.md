## 6.4 WALKTHROUGH — Build the retirement paycheck

*`WALKTHROUGH` · narrate from the DO / SEE / ⚠ sheet*

> **V1 FILMING HOLD:** Record after the Retirement Income and spending-guardrail slices are validated. The final portfolio guardrail levels require the approved inverse-threshold model and receipt contract.

## Before recording

- Completed current facts for income, spending, accounts, debts, and life events
- Retirement benefits and planned retirement date entered
- Withdrawal and borrowing examples prepared without using live customer data
- Current Plan result available, even if it will be recalculated again in Module 9

## 1 · Open Retirement Income

**DO** Plan → Build & improve → Improve your plan → Retirement income.

**SEE** Current as the saved strategy and Preview as the proposed strategy.

## 2 · Build the spending number

**DO** Review planned annual retirement spending.

**SEE** monthly spending and today's-dollar view.

**CHECK** that debt payments and taxes are handled in their owned model fields rather than counted twice inside living spending.

## 3 · Build the income floor

**DO** Review Social Security, pensions, annuities, rental income, work income, and other reliable retirement income.

**SEE** total spending, reliable income, and the amount the portfolio must provide.

## 4 · Read the gap and Bridge

**DO** Review the years before Social Security, Medicare, retirement-account access, or another income source begins.

**SEE** the annual gap and the accessible assets assigned to fund it.

**SAY** The Bridge keeps a known retirement gap from forcing a Bitcoin sale at the wrong time.

## 5 · Set the withdrawal order

**DO** Review the order used across taxable, tax-deferred, Roth, cash, and any approved borrowing strategy.

**SEE** the year-by-year source of retirement cash flow.

**⚠** A modeled borrowing strategy remains a planning choice. The user still establishes the real loan outside Orange Plan.

## 6 · Set the draw-and-refill rule

**DO** Review how the Reserve is spent and refilled.

**SEE** the rule for strong, normal, and weak market conditions.

**CHECK** that the Reserve floor and refill decision do not create an avoidable forced sale.

## 7 · Read Current versus Preview

**DO** Change one strategy input in Preview.

**SEE** the exact before/after simulation count, planned and earliest modeled retirement dates, taxes, spending, or other materially changed output.

**SAY** Orange Plan calculates the effect. Preview keeps the saved plan intact while we compare the choice.

## 8 · Read the Orange Plan standard

**DO** Review the current simulation result.

**SEE** how many of the 1,000 simulations worked, the through-age, and the result freshness state.

**SAY** The normal Orange Plan standard is 800 successful simulations out of 1,000. A custom standard belongs in Advanced model settings.

## 9 · Read spending guardrails

**DO** Open Spending guardrails.

**SEE**:

- lower portfolio guardrail;
- current retirement-portfolio value;
- upper portfolio guardrail;
- current state: within guardrails, below lower, or above upper.

**⚠** The portfolio levels must come from the validated year-specific inverse calculation. Never relabel 60 / 80 / 95 probability percentages as dollar guardrails.

## 10 · Preview the annual spending review

**DO** Use a prepared example that crosses one guardrail.

**SEE** the separate annual review calculate a proposed spending adjustment and show it in Preview.

**CHECK** the annual change cap and Reserve refill status.

**SAVE** only when the example is meant to become the current strategy.

## Retirement Income checkpoint

- Retirement spending and reliable income are current.
- The portfolio gap and Bridge are understood.
- Withdrawal order and Reserve rule are saved.
- Current and Preview are read side by side.
- The normal standard is 800 of 1,000.
- Guardrail levels are portfolio values backed by the validated model.
- Module 9 will recalculate the completed-plan baseline after all areas are finished.
