## 9.3 WALKTHROUGH — Finish, test, review, and save the plan

*`WALKTHROUGH` · narrate from the DO / SEE / ⚠ sheet*

> **V1 FILMING HOLD:** Record after the shell, Plan, Scenarios, Protect, strategy workspaces, and Your Plan are visually stable.

## Before recording

- Every required Build & improve area intentionally complete
- Current account facts and important activity reconciled
- No unresolved blocker that would make the result Unavailable
- One focused scenario prepared
- One Preview change prepared
- Prior-year Your Plan PDF available for the comparison example when possible

## 1 · Finish Build & improve

**DO** Plan → Build & improve → View all.

**SEE** the four groups:

- Build your plan
- Make it more accurate
- Improve your plan
- Advanced

**CHECK** every required row. Advanced rows remain optional unless the household uses a custom assumption or strategy.

## 2 · Recalculate the completed plan

**DO** Plan → Overview → Recalculate.

**SEE**:

- simulation count out of 1,000;
- through-age;
- planned retirement date;
- earliest modeled retirement date;
- result state;
- concise provenance and any details that could still change the result.

**SAY** This is the first result we treat as the completed-plan baseline. Earlier results helped build the plan and may have been Preliminary.

## 3 · Read the Orange Plan standard

**SEE** whether the plan reaches 800 successful simulations out of 1,000.

**SAY** Normal users use the Orange Plan standard. A custom standard belongs in Advanced model settings and changes the qualifying earliest modeled date.

**⚠** Do not show a normal confidence-target control or lead with a percentage.

## 4 · Read the next action

**SEE** one required action only when the result, data, or plan state calls for it.

Examples:

- Make account activity more complete
- Recalculate a Stale result
- Review one strategy that could bring the plan to the standard
- Resolve one Needs Attention item

**⚠** Do not fill the page with generic insight cards.

## 5 · Run one focused scenario

**DO** Plan → Scenarios → create or select one question.

**CHANGE** only the inputs required for that question.

**SEE** the scenario result remain separate from Current.

## 6 · Move a real decision into Preview

**DO** Open the canonical strategy workspace and preview the intended change.

**SEE** Current versus Preview, including:

- simulation count;
- planned retirement date;
- earliest modeled retirement date;
- spending;
- taxes;
- and any other materially changed output.

**SAY** The model provides the comparison. We save the change only when Preview reflects the plan we intend to use.

## 7 · Save the intended change

**DO** Save to plan.

**SEE** the prior Preview become Current and the result receipt update or become Stale until recalculated, according to the final product behavior.

**RECALCULATE** when required.

## 8 · Review the four permanent destinations

**DO** Complete one final lap:

- **Home** — current accounts, holdings, activity, debt monitoring, and Needs Attention
- **Cash Flow** — income, spending, taxes, debt payments, reserve, and saving
- **Plan** — simulation result, dates, strategy, Scenarios, and Build & improve
- **Protect** — custody, beneficiaries, trusted people, legal readiness, and family handoff

## 9 · Open Your Plan

**DO** Plan → View full plan or Profile → Your Plan.

**READ** in planner order:

1. Position
2. Trajectory
3. Risk
4. Actions

**CHECK** that the document identifies the result state, through-age, active assumptions, and saved plan inputs.

## 10 · Save the yearly artifact

**DO** Print / Save PDF.

**NAME** the file with the year.

**COMPARE** with the prior year:

- net worth and Bitcoin share;
- simulation count and result state;
- planned and earliest modeled retirement dates;
- spending and guardrail state;
- completion of last year's actions.

## 11 · Back up and schedule

**DO** Profile → Settings → Data and privacy → Backup and restore.

**CREATE** the encrypted Orange Plan backup.

**⚠** The encrypted backup restores the app data. It does not contain or replace a Bitcoin wallet backup and should never be uploaded to an AI tool.

**SCHEDULE** the monthly review day and annual review month.

## Final checkpoint

- Build & improve is complete at the level the household needs.
- The completed plan has a Current simulation result.
- The normal standard is read as 800 of 1,000.
- Planned and earliest modeled dates are distinct.
- One scenario was tested without changing Current.
- One proposed decision was compared in Preview and saved deliberately.
- Your Plan was read and saved as a yearly PDF.
- The encrypted app backup and review schedule exist.
