## 1.3 What your first retirement result actually means

*`TEACH` · ~5 min · PRE-DICTATION FILMING DRAFT*

Your first retirement result gives you a starting point for the rest of the plan.

Quick setup uses a limited set of information: your age, income, spending, rough account values, Bitcoin holdings, planning age, and the growth model you selected. Orange Plan can use those inputs to produce an early retirement estimate before every planning detail is complete.

The quick estimate uses one deterministic projection. It applies one set of assumptions at different retirement dates and finds the earliest date where that projected path lasts through the planning age.

Treat that age as a useful first estimate. The rest of the course replaces the rough inputs with the financial facts and strategy decisions that actually drive the plan.

Once Orange Plan runs the plan through 1,000 simulations, the result answers a different question. It shows how many simulated market paths funded the plan through the planning age.

For example:

> 790 of 1,000 simulations worked.

The normal Orange Plan standard is 800 successful simulations out of 1,000. The standard determines the earliest modeled retirement date for a normal user. Advanced model settings can hold a custom standard for a power user with a specific reason to change it.

🎬 VISUAL — Quick deterministic estimate on the left; preliminary 1,000-simulation result on the right.

Two retirement dates need separate labels.

Your **planned retirement date** is the date you want the plan to test.

Your **earliest modeled retirement date** is the earliest date that reaches the Orange Plan standard under the current facts and assumptions.

Those dates may match. They may also tell you that the date you want needs more savings, lower spending, more time, or another change.

The first simulation result may still be preliminary. Orange Plan uses four truthful states:

- **Preliminary** — important facts or accuracy details remain.
- **Current** — the relevant facts and saved calculation are current.
- **Stale** — a modeled fact changed after the last calculation.
- **Unavailable** — the calculation or required source data failed.

🎬 VISUAL — Preliminary → Current → Stale → Recalculate, with Unavailable shown as a separate failure state.

That status protects you from treating a precise-looking number as more complete than the information underneath it.

Each course module improves a different part of the result.

Foundation replaces the rough account values with real accounts, current holdings, household details, and planning assumptions.

Cash Flow adds the real income, spending, reserve, and expected life events.

Allocation decides what the investable surplus is for, where it goes, and what it buys.

Debt adds the real loan terms and the job assigned to every debt.

Tax and Retirement Income build the tax strategy, income floor, withdrawals, and spending rules.

Protect completes the custody, family, and estate work that keeps the financial plan usable in real life.

As those inputs improve, the retirement dates and simulation count may move. The updated result reflects better information.

The Foundation walkthrough below this lesson replaces the rough setup data with the real Foundation facts, then reads the first result with the correct status. Each later walkthrough completes the area it owns. Module 9 recalculates and saves the completed-plan baseline after the full course is built.
