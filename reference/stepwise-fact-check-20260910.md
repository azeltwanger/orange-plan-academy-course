# Financial and security spot checks for the filming revision

Checked September 10, 2026. These checks support the educational wording; they do not certify a household transaction, a lender contract, the app's calculations or every applicable tax rule.

- Roth IRA distribution ordering and the different five-year rules: [IRS Publication 590-B](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p590b). Regular contributions, conversions and earnings must not be treated as one withdrawal category. A workplace Roth account does not simply inherit the IRA ordering rule.
- Workplace-plan distributions and current Roth owner lifetime RMD treatment: [IRS Publication 575](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p575) and [IRS RMD guidance](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-topics-required-minimum-distributions-rmds). Beneficiary requirements remain a separate question. Use current publications over an older FAQ when their age/RMD language conflicts.
- Debt payoff comparison: [CFPB debt action plan](https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_your-money-your-goals_debt-action-plan_tool_2018-11.pdf). Keep required payments distinct from extra payments; compare the cost and behavioral consequences of payoff methods. The Reed example assigns its available dollars once.
- Login protection: [CISA MFA guidance](https://www.cisa.gov/audiences/small-and-medium-businesses/secure-your-business/require-multifactor-authentication) and [CISA mobile communications guidance](https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/2024-12/guidance-mobile-communications-best-practices.pdf). Security keys/passkeys resist phishing; authenticator codes can still be phished. Avoid an absolute protection claim.
