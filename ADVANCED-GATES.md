# Orange Plan Academy — Advanced Library gates

**Status:** pre-dictation Core-versus-Advanced contract  
**Purpose:** keep the core complete without making every learner study every edge case.

The Advanced Library is not “Module 10.” It is a set of conditional deep dives linked from the core planning area that makes them relevant.

Every advanced lesson begins with:

> **Watch this only if [condition visible in your plan or current decision]. Otherwise this part of your plan is complete without it.**

Advanced lessons do not count toward core progress unless the learner's own plan triggers the gate.

## Gate rules

A strong gate is:

- visible in the plan or a decision the learner is actively considering,
- binary enough that the learner knows whether it applies,
- placed after the core concept needed to understand it,
- and explicit that skipping it is correct when the condition is absent.

A weak gate is:

- “watch this if you want to learn more,”
- a personality label,
- a balance threshold stated as universal,
- or a fear-based prompt that makes every learner feel required to continue.

# Modeling and assumptions

## How the 1,000 test runs are built

**Core owner:** 1.3  
**Gate:**

> Watch this only if you want to audit the model methodology, compare the return distribution with another planner, or explain why the test-run result changed after a model-version update. You do not need this lesson to choose a confidence target or finish the baseline plan.

**Advanced owns:** path construction, volatility/skew assumptions, correlation, inflation modeling, model versions, seed/reproducibility, limitations.

## Custom return periods

**Core owner:** 1.2  
**Gate:**

> Watch this only if the broad saved return view does not represent the planning case you need to test and you can explain the custom periods you want to use.

**Advanced owns:** period design, sensitivity ranges, avoiding double overrides, versioning.

## Holding-level return and income overrides

**Core owner:** 1.2; short callback in 4.2  
**Gate:**

> Watch this only if a specific holding behaves differently from the broad asset class Orange Plan is using—for example, a spot Bitcoin ETF being modeled as ordinary stock, a rental with its own income, or a security whose yield is part of the plan. Most holdings should keep the Plan default.

**Advanced owns:** fixed return, Plan Bitcoin model, declining return, custom periods, separate yield/income field, property assumptions, override audit.

# Debt and leverage

## Existing Bitcoin-backed loan

**Core owners:** 3.1 and 6.2  
**Gate:**

> Watch this if a Bitcoin-backed loan already exists in your plan. The core debt step is not complete until the real balance, collateral, rate, margin-call/liquidation rules, top-up resources, and household response are documented.

**Advanced owns:** current LTV, lender thresholds, auto top-up, release rules, loan term, refinance, liquidation behavior, counterparty risk, cross-loan collateral.

## Considering a new Bitcoin-backed loan

**Gate:**

> Watch this only if you are actively comparing a specific new loan with a sale, cash-flow change, or another funding source. Do not watch it merely because borrowing could make the projection look better.

**Advanced owns:** purpose, proceeds, stress price, flash-crash capacity, repayment/refinance, interest, lender concentration, tax tracing, estate balance.

## Debt capacity and leverage strategy

**Gate:**

> Watch this only if you are considering adding debt, changing an amortizing loan to interest-only, consolidating, or using debt to invest.

**Advanced owns:** full scenario comparison, variable-rate risk, amortization, debt-funded investing, refinancing, break-even analysis.

# Allocation and account access

## Large one-time rebalance

**Core owners:** 4.1–4.4  
**Gate:**

> Watch this only if reaching the target would require a meaningful taxable sale, retirement-account exchange, rollover, custody change, or movement of pledged collateral. Contribution-first drift correction does not require this lesson.

**Advanced owns:** lot selection, gains, charitable options, wash-sale/security rules, transaction sequencing, custody transfer, rebalancing Scenario.

## Early retirement account access

**Core owners:** 4.3 and 6.1  
**Gate:**

> Watch this only if the plan retires before the money needed for the Bridge is accessible and the household expects to use a traditional retirement account, Roth contribution basis, or another special access method.

**Advanced owns:** Rule of 55, 72(t)/SEPP, Roth ordering/basis, plan-specific distribution rules, rollover timing, penalties and exceptions. Current rules require professional verification.

## Self-directed retirement Bitcoin

**Core owner:** 4.4  
**Gate:**

> Watch this only if you own or are actively evaluating direct or alternative Bitcoin exposure inside an IRA or retirement-plan structure beyond a standard brokerage Bitcoin ETF.

**Advanced owns:** custodian/LLC structure, custody roles, fees, prohibited transactions, valuation, distributions, recordkeeping, professional review.

# Tax optimization

## Roth conversion planning

**Core owner:** 5.2  
**Gate:**

> Watch this only if you have meaningful traditional tax-deferred money and the tax roadmap shows a year with lower ordinary income before later Social Security, pension, or required distributions.

**Advanced owns:** conversion amount, marginal brackets, pro-rata rule, five-year rules, state, IRMAA, withholding/estimated tax, cash to pay tax, multi-year comparison.

## Capital-gain harvesting

**Core owner:** 5.2  
**Gate:**

> Watch this only if you have taxable Bitcoin or securities with gains and the roadmap shows a year where intentionally realizing gain may improve basis or lifetime taxes.

**Advanced owns:** stacking, state, NIIT, lot identification, repurchase, current digital-asset/security rules, charitable alternative.

## Tax-loss harvesting

**Gate:**

> Watch this only if you have a current unrealized taxable loss and another gain, carryforward, or portfolio decision that may make realizing it useful.

**Advanced owns:** current wash-sale treatment, replacement exposure, lot execution, state, carryforwards, documentation.

## State relocation

**Gate:**

> Watch this only if the household is genuinely considering a move and has at least two realistic locations, dates, and lifestyle costs to compare.

**Advanced owns:** domicile, residency, move year, state tax, housing/insurance/healthcare, estate law, professional review. It does not reduce relocation to income-tax rate alone.

# Retirement income

## Healthcare before Medicare

**Core owner:** 6.1  
**Gate:**

> Watch this if the planned retirement begins before Medicare eligibility or the household expects a material change in employer health coverage.

**Advanced owns:** premium and out-of-pocket model, ACA income interaction, COBRA, spouse plan, HSA, Medicare transition, state/household specifics.

## Sell versus borrow

**Core owner:** 6.2  
**Gate:**

> Watch this only if a specific retirement year or purchase is being compared between a taxable Bitcoin sale and a real Bitcoin-backed borrowing option. The core plan should still work without borrowing unless the household deliberately chooses that risk.

**Advanced owns:** tax, basis, interest, LTV, liquidation, lender risk, Bitcoin retained, capacity exhaustion, collateral sale, loan at death, repayment.

## Custom retirement withdrawal phases

**Core owner:** 6.2  
**Gate:**

> Watch this only if the tax roadmap or account-access timeline contains a real stage change that the default strategy does not represent—for example, before Social Security, after Social Security, or after required distributions begin.

**Advanced owns:** phase boundaries, bracket ceiling, account blend, asset priority, scenario comparison, CPA execution.

## Custom spending guardrails

**Core owner:** 6.3  
**Gate:**

> Watch this only if the household cannot follow the default 60/80/95 policy or has a documented reason to change the lower trigger, target, upper trigger, or correction cap.

**Advanced owns:** custom policy validation, flexibility budget, policy history, reset/remove decision, professional comparison.

# Custody

## Passphrase-protected wallet

**Core owners:** 7.1–7.2  
**Gate:**

> Watch this only if the wallet already uses a passphrase or the household is deliberately considering one after completing a tested ordinary hardware-wallet recovery.

**Advanced owns:** separate-wallet behavior, backups, duress/decoy limitations, family process, device support, recovery tests, permanent-loss risk.

## Collaborative multisig

**Core owners:** 7.1 and 8.2  
**Gate:**

> Watch this only if the long-term balance has outgrown the family's tested single-signature process or the household needs a provider-assisted multi-key design it can maintain.

**Advanced owns:** threshold, key roles, provider key, recovery without provider, inheritance, privacy, fees, key replacement, software/configuration records.

## DIY multisig

**Gate:**

> Watch this only if you can already operate and recover a single-signature hardware wallet, understand the additional configuration and privacy requirements, and deliberately choose to carry the maintenance without provider support.

**Advanced owns:** coordinator, descriptor/configuration, key fingerprints/paths, geographic distribution, replacement, compatibility, testing, estate handoff.

## UTXO and coin-control management

**Gate:**

> Watch this only if the wallet has many small deposits, privacy concerns, high future fee exposure, or a planned move/restructure where UTXO selection matters.

**Advanced owns:** UTXOs, consolidation timing, fee risk, address reuse, labeling, privacy trade-offs, tax-lot coordination.

## Provider diversification

**Gate:**

> Watch this only if a life-changing amount sits with one custodian, exchange, or collaborative provider and temporary loss of access would materially affect the plan.

**Advanced owns:** operating limits, provider concentration, recovery, transfer capacity, cost and complexity of multiple providers.

# Estate and family handoff

## Trust planning

**Core owner:** 8.1  
**Gate:**

> Watch this only if an attorney identifies a real probate, incapacity, privacy, management, tax, special-needs, minor-beneficiary, business, or family reason for a trust. Owning Bitcoin alone does not trigger the lesson.

**Advanced owns:** revocable/irrevocable distinctions, funding/titling, trustee powers, custody coordination, beneficiary designations, state law, professional drafting.

## Estate-tax and advanced gifting

**Gate:**

> Watch this only if current or projected wealth, state, prior gifts, business interests, or family goals create an actual federal or state transfer-tax question.

**Advanced owns:** current exemptions, portability, state estate/inheritance tax, lifetime gifts, basis, trusts, appraisal, attorney/CPA review.

## Special family circumstances

**Gate:**

> Watch this only if the plan involves a minor, special-needs beneficiary, blended family, estranged relative, creditor concern, non-US heir, business succession, or another situation where a simple spouse/children transfer is not adequate.

**Advanced owns:** professional coordination and process design, not do-it-yourself legal instructions.

**Advanced owns:** guaranteed/non-guaranteed values, fees, surrender, lapse, loans, financing, ownership, tax, alternatives.

**Core owner:** 8.4

**Advanced owns:** policy triggers, benefit period, inflation protection, elimination period, home care, partnership, hybrid products, public benefits, licensed review.

# Placement in the course

Each core module ends with a quiet **Optional next levels** section containing only the gates relevant to that module.

Do not show empty advanced modules in the learner's required progress path. The Advanced Library can also be searchable by topic.

A learner who does not trigger any gates can complete the full Orange Plan through the 28 core lessons and future Build Your Plan walkthroughs.
