## A · Gap-first method

Proposed life-protection method:

1. Estimate survivor spending and transition needs.
2. Add debts and specific commitments the family intends to fund.
3. Subtract surviving income, liquid assets deliberately available, verified survivor benefits, and existing coverage.
4. Review timing, inflation, tax, investment, and liquidity with a licensed professional.
