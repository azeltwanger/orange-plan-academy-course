# Set the Bitcoin allocation you can hold through a full drawdown

This is a fictional educational example. A review band identifies a decision point; it does not create an automatic buy or sell instruction.

## Name the denominator

At the illustrative starting price:

- Bitcoin: **$175,000**
- Other holdings included in household target allocation: **$95,000**
- 529 committed to the children: **$25,000**
- Home: **$450,000**

Orange Plan excludes the primary residence and beneficiary-restricted 529 from the target-allocation denominator.

| Scope | Amount | Bitcoin percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| App allocatable portfolio | $270,000 | 64.8% |
| Financial balances including 529 | $295,000 | 59.3% |
| Gross assets including home | $745,000 | 23.5% |

Name the denominator every time the percentage is discussed.

## Three requirements

The position must fit:

- Education
- Conviction
- Financial and behavioral capacity to hold

## Educational tiers

- Foundation: 1–5%
- Integration: 10–20%
- Optimization: 25–50%
- Sovereign: 50%+

The tier describes the position; it is not an identity.

## Drawdown math

**Bitcoin allocation × drawdown = approximate portfolio hit**

A 75% decline removes **$131,250** from the demo's $175,000 Bitcoin position.

- Allocatable portfolio falls from $270,000 to about **$138,750** if other holdings stay flat.
- Financial balances including the 529 fall from $295,000 to about **$163,750**.

The dollar loss is the same; the percentage depends on scope.

## Target, band, and action

- Target: intended long-term mix
- Review band: when drift becomes large enough to review
- Action: a separate household decision after understanding tax, basis, access, reserve, and custody effects

Approved demo target: **50% Bitcoin with a 40–60% review band**.

The current 64.8% app allocation is above the band. Possible responses include routing new money toward underweight holdings, revisiting the target after a genuine planning change, or separately reviewing a rebalance. The app state does not create an automatic trade.

The $1,500 taxable Bridge / investment route follows the saved target and current drift rather than automatically adding to the overweight holding.

## Where the numbers come from

### Current allocation

- **What it means:** Bitcoin share of the allocatable portfolio
- **Calculated from:** Bitcoin holdings ÷ holdings included in household target allocation
- **Edit source:** holdings and target allocation
- **This affects:** drift, routing, drawdown exposure, confidence, and earliest date

### Drawdown hit

- **What it means:** dollar loss from the Bitcoin decline before other holdings move
- **Calculated from:** Bitcoin value × drawdown
- **Edit source:** holdings or Scenario drawdown
- **This affects:** holdability, reserve adequacy, and target realism

## Done when

The household can state the app denominator, current allocation, target, band, and dollar loss in a 75% drawdown and explain the trade-off it accepts.
