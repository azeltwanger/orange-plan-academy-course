# Calculate the family protection gaps

Use the actual contract and licensed professionals before changing coverage.

## Life gap

Add survivor living support, debts, childcare, education, healthcare, final and transition costs, and specific commitments.

Subtract surviving income, liquid assets deliberately available, verified survivor benefits, and existing coverage.

Demo first-pass annual gap:

**$80,000 living + $22,000 debt − $40,000 surviving income = $62,000**

This is not a final coverage amount. Timing, changed expenses, benefits, assets, and policies still need review.

## Term and permanent

Term is often the first comparison for a temporary need. Permanent coverage can address a lifelong need but includes more cost, assumptions, surrender terms, management, and lapse risk.

## Disability

Review benefit percentage, cap, tax treatment, waiting period, benefit period, disability definition, and excluded income. Compare the expected after-tax amount with household spending.

## Umbrella

Confirm underlying limits, exclusions, household drivers, property, rentals, business, and other liability exposure.

## Long-term care

Compare family support, portfolio self-insurance, insurance, hybrid products, and public benefits where eligible.

## Beneficiaries

Orange Plan tracks the checklist. The carrier holds the binding contract and beneficiary record.

## Where the gaps come from

- **Life gap:** survivor needs minus available resources
- **Disability gap:** required spending minus after-tax disability income

## Done when

Each major risk has a starting gap or deliberate self-insurance decision and the real policy records are verified.
