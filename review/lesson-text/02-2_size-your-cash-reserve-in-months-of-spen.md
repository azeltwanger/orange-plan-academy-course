# Size your cash reserve in months of spending

## Formula

**Selected monthly spending basis × target months = reserve target**

Orange Plan performs the multiplication. The household chooses the basis and months.

## Choose target months

Austin's planning range:

- 3 months: possible starting minimum for stable dual-income households with few dependents
- 6 months: reasonable baseline for many households
- 12+ months: may fit single income, self-employment, variable pay, dependents, high fixed costs, self-funded healthcare, or long job-search risk

Use two parent questions:

1. How reliable is income?
2. How difficult is it to reduce expenses?

## Demo household

- Bare-bones spending: $5,000 per month
- Target: 6 months
- Reserve target: $30,000
- Current reserve: $30,000
- Status: fully funded

## Retirement

The reserve changes from paycheck protection to a retirement cash buffer. Austin uses 18 months as an initial planning default, while the actual basis, recurring income, risk tolerance, and refill policy determine the saved amount.

The retirement strategy is taught later. This lesson owns the basis and target months.

## Where the target comes from

- **What it means:** chosen cash protection amount
- **Calculated from:** monthly basis × target months
- **Edit source:** Cash Flow Reserve settings
- **This affects:** reserve gap, remaining surplus, and later retirement cash-buffer planning

## Done when

The target and current funding are visible, and the household can explain why its number of months fits its own risks.
