# Austin review — learner-question map

**Use this before reading each script.** The lesson title can stay concise and outcome-focused. It does not have to be rewritten as a question.

The question is the teaching spine:

> **Real learner question → worked example → Austin's judgment → one decision → checkable finish line**

This pass is deliberately **not adding another introduction to every video**. The opening should answer why the learner cares within roughly the first 30 seconds, then move into the example.

During review, mark `QUESTION` only when the script answers a different question, takes too long to reach it, or leaves the real answer for the walkthrough.

## Austin's question test

Before approving a lesson, ask:

1. Is this a question a learner would genuinely ask?
2. Does the opening make the question clear without adding a long setup?
3. Does the example actually answer it?
4. Is the answer my real planning judgment rather than generic financial education?
5. Does the learner leave with one decision they can make in their own plan?

When any answer is no, dictate the replacement **now**, before the lesson is approved. This is the final structural lens, not a second pass after filming.
