# Orange Plan AI — planning question guide

Use these as starting questions, not magic prompts. The useful part is the structure: ask about a real decision, name the constraint, and require the answer to use the current plan numbers.

## The question structure

> **Decision + constraint + what you want the answer to show**

Example:

> I want to retire at 55 without making a Bitcoin-backed loan necessary. Use the current plan numbers. What is the biggest constraint, which input is driving it, and what is the next move?

## The answer standard

A useful answer should contain:

1. **Verdict** — the direct planning read
2. **Evidence** — the number and source supporting it
3. **Trade-off** — what the choice gains and gives up
4. **Next move** — one action, one comparison, or one cleanup item

When the answer could apply to anybody, say:

> Use my plan numbers. Show the evidence, the trade-off, and the specific next action.

## Full-plan review

> Review my full plan. Rank the top three things that deserve attention by impact. Tell me what looks strong, what is holding the plan back, and what can wait.

> What is the one bottleneck that changes the most other parts of this plan?

> Give me one action now, one thing to model next, and one thing that can wait.

## Baseline and assumptions

> Which input in my current plan is the least reliable and material enough to change the retirement result?

> Which assumption is doing the most work in the earliest retirement date?

> Separate the current facts, expected life events, and questions that belong in Scenarios.

## Cash Flow and Reserve

> Why does Cash Flow show $3,761 when the household is putting $4,000 to work every month? Reconcile the numbers and tell me which amount is already included in Debt.

> Is the reserve target solving a real forced-sale risk, or are we holding more cash than the household needs?

> Which spending number looks inconsistent with the source data, and where should I correct it?

## Debt

> Review every debt and rank the one next move by interest cost, payment pressure, liquidity, and forced-sale risk.

> What is the strongest case for keeping this debt, and the strongest case for paying it down?

> What changes in Cash Flow when this debt is paid off?

## Allocation and the next dollar

> What would force us to sell investments during a 75% Bitcoin drawdown?

> We are above the Bitcoin allocation band, but we do not want to create a large taxable sale. Compare correcting the drift through new contributions with selling today.

> Which is underfunded first: Reserve, Bridge, Healthcare Bridge, or Legacy?

> Route the current available dollars in order. Show the amount, reason, account, and what can wait.

## Tax

> Which basis records are complete, which are unresolved, and what planning decision is blocked by the missing information?

> What is the most useful tax window in the current projection, and which assumptions create it?

> Prepare the exact question and source records I should bring to my CPA before a Bitcoin sale or Roth conversion.

## Retirement Income

> Break the first retirement-year total need into living spending, life events, debt, tax, and refill. Then show recurring income and total draw.

> Why is the total draw different from the $100,000 retirement spending input?

> Compare keeping the $100,000 paycheck with the Balanced spending amount. Show the monthly difference, confidence, timing, and flexibility trade-off.

> What would make borrowing better than selling in this plan, and which LTV, interest, tax, collateral, and estate risks could reverse that answer?

## Scenarios

> Explain this Scenario compared with the Baseline. Name the one or two actual changes driving the result.

> The 4% inflation Scenario lowers confidence from 94.6% to 91.6%. Which future costs changed most, and does this difference change the retirement decision?

> Is this difference large enough to change what the household would do, or is it model noise?

## Custody, estate, and protection

> What is the most material family-access gap in the current Protect plan?

> Separate what the app checklist says from what still needs real-world proof.

> Which single physical, human, or provider failure could stop recovery?

> Turn the current Protect gaps into one household action, one professional question, and one item that can wait.

## Maintenance and annual review

> What changed since the last review, and which change is large enough to affect the saved strategy?

> Is this data drift or decision drift?

> Summarize the current plan in six sentences and identify the one to three owned actions for the next review period.

## Follow-ups that improve almost any answer

- Where did that number come from?
- Which source input would I change?
- What else should move if I change it?
- What is the strongest argument for the opposite choice?
- Which assumption is carrying the result?
- What would force a bad Bitcoin sale?
- What can wait?
- Does the current plan already answer this, or is a Scenario needed?
- What requires professional or provider verification before execution?

## Three rules

1. The app calculation is the source of plan math.
2. Never provide Bitcoin secrets, credentials, full account numbers, Social Security numbers, or authentication codes.
3. Verify current tax, legal, insurance, lending, and custody execution with the appropriate professional or provider.
