## A · Role distinctions

Review the separation among:

- executor / personal representative after death,
- financial agent under a power of attorney during life,
- healthcare decision-maker,
- trustee,
- technical Bitcoin helper or custody provider.

Proposed standing language:

> A will nominates an executor; the applicable court or legal process generally establishes authority for probate assets. A financial power of attorney generally ends at death. Holding a key does not itself create legal authority, and a legal title does not itself provide a key.

The course describes the heir letter as a nonbinding plain-language starting guide, not the will and not a grant of authority.

- who to call first,
