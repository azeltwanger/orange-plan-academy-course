8.1 Choose the people, legal documents, and beneficiary records
~8 min at 155 wpm · VOICE-MATCHED DRAFT — Austin review + estate attorney review pending
============================================================

The estate plan has to answer two separate questions:

- Who has legal authority to act?
- How does that person connect with the custody process without exposing the Bitcoin today?

Custody alone does not answer the first question. A will alone does not answer the second.

The exact documents and procedures are state-specific. This lesson builds the planning structure; an attorney licensed in the household's state drafts and reviews the legal plan.

== SEPARATE THE ROLES ==

**Executor or personal representative** handles the probate estate after death when the legal process requires it. A will nominates the person; the applicable court or process generally establishes authority for probate assets.

**Financial agent under a power of attorney** acts during the owner's life within the authority granted by the document. A power of attorney generally ends at death.

**Healthcare decision-maker** handles medical decisions under the documents used in the state.

**Trustee** manages property held in or passing to a trust when the plan uses one.

A technical Bitcoin helper or custody provider can assist with the process. That role does not create legal authority and does not require receiving a seed phrase or private key.

The same person may serve in more than one legal role when the attorney and family decide that it fits. Do not assume one title gives every kind of authority.

== CHOOSE FOR THE WORK ==

An executor is not an honorary name on a page.

Choose someone who is trustworthy, organized, willing, available, and able to work with attorneys, tax professionals, providers, and family members while grieving.

The person does not have to be the most technical Bitcoiner in the family. Judgment is harder to replace than technical help.

Ask before naming them. Explain the general workload, where the process documents will be, who the professional contacts are, and who serves if the first choice cannot.

A spouse or heir may also serve as executor in many plans. Whether that fits depends on state law, family dynamics, and the custody design. There is no universal course rule that the executor and heir must be different people.

== REVIEW THE CORE DOCUMENT AREAS ==

The names vary by jurisdiction, but the core areas are:

- will,
- durable financial power of attorney,
- healthcare directive or medical authority documents,
- beneficiary, payable-on-death, and transfer-on-death records,
- and a trust only when it solves a specific problem identified with counsel.

The legal document should not contain a seed phrase, private key, passphrase, PIN, password, or complete recovery path.

The attorney needs to understand the asset and draft authority that works with the custody process without turning a public or discoverable document into a spending key.

== THE PROVIDER RECORD MAY CONTROL THE ACCOUNT ==

The legal effect cannot be reduced to a one-line slogan about a beneficiary form and a will.

For many retirement plans, IRAs, insurance policies, and accounts with a valid death-transfer designation, the provider record controls under the account terms and applicable law rather than the will's probate instructions.

Plan terms, state law, marital rights, spousal consent, community-property rules, and the validity of the designation can change the result.

The practical rule is to review the legal plan and every provider designation together, then confirm the provider has the current record.

Orange Plan is the checklist. The plan administrator, custodian, bank, or insurer holds the binding account record.

== RUN THE DEMO REVIEW ==

The demo household intends the spouse to be the primary beneficiary and the children to be contingent beneficiaries under an attorney-approved design for minors.

One old workplace retirement account still names a parent from before the marriage.

The current will may say the spouse receives the estate. That does not safely resolve the account mismatch.

The action is to verify the plan administrator's record, update it under the provider process, and coordinate the contingent design with counsel.

The household still needs to choose and ask:

- a primary executor or personal representative,
- a practical backup,
- the financial agent during life,
- and the healthcare decision-maker.

The canonical demo remains intentionally incomplete until those people agree.

== ASK THE ATTORNEY BITCOIN-SPECIFIC QUESTIONS ==

Ask:

- Have you planned for self-custodied or collaborative digital assets?
- How should the will, power of attorney, trust if any, and provider designations coordinate in this state?
- Does the financial power of attorney include the authority needed for digital assets and current providers?
- How will an executor, agent, or trustee prove authority to an exchange, IRA custodian, or collaborative provider?
- How should the legal documents point to the process without exposing keys or recovery material?
- Will you help the family understand execution, not only deliver documents for signature?

The professional-review packet will refine this language before filming.

== BUILD THE EXECUTOR PACKET ==

The packet connects the legal plan with the financial and custody processes.

It identifies the attorney, tax professional, custody contact, insurers, and providers.

It states where the will, death-claim information, custody map, policy records, and other process documents are stored.

It includes stop signs:

- do not share wallet backups or credentials,
- do not accept unsolicited recovery help,
- do not move Bitcoin before authority, custody procedure, and tax consequences are understood,
- and do not assume every account follows the will.

It contains no secrets.

Review the packet with the people while you are alive. The first time they hear the role should not be when the job begins.

== WHERE READINESS COMES FROM ==

Protect can track people, document status, provider confirmation dates, and professional contacts.

It cannot validate a state-law document or prove a provider accepted a beneficiary change.

The app owns the checklist. The attorney, court, and provider records supply the legal proof.

== YOUR DECISION ==

Choose the primary and backup people, identify the documents needing attorney work, and list every provider designation that must be confirmed or changed.

== PUT IT IN ORANGE PLAN ==

Record the roles, beneficiaries, documents, dates, and contacts in Protect. Make the real legal and beneficiary changes with the attorney or provider that owns the record.

== YOU ARE DONE WHEN ==

Every role has a primary and backup, the people have agreed, attorney work is scheduled or complete, and every provider designation has been checked against the household's intent.

# 08-2_split-access-dual-control-and-redundancy.md

## SPOKEN SCRIPT

TELEPROMPTER SCRIPT — segment 8.2
8.2 Design dual control and redundancy without creating a new loss path
~8 min at 155 wpm · VOICE-MATCHED DRAFT — Austin review + custody and estate review pending
============================================================

A family access plan needs two separate tests:

1. Can one person or component move the Bitcoin alone when the design says they should not?
2. Can one lost component or unavailable person permanently stop recovery?

The first is dual control. The second is redundancy.

Passing one does not automatically pass the other.

== TEST DUAL CONTROL ==

A single-signature wallet does not have cryptographic dual control. One complete device or wallet backup can control the funds.

Legal oversight, storage policy, or family communication can reduce the surrounding risk. They do not change the fact that one complete key path can sign.

A multi-key wallet can require more than one key. A collaborative provider can participate in a threshold design under the actual provider agreement.

Do not call a setup dual control simply because two people know it exists while either person still holds everything required to spend.

== TEST REDUNDANCY ==

A design is redundant when one realistic loss does not permanently stop recovery.

A device with a verified backup can survive loss of the device.

A 2-of-3 multisig can survive loss of one key when the other two keys and the required wallet information are recoverable.

A passphrase wallet may require both the backup and exact passphrase. If either is lost without a valid backup, the funds can be lost.

More copies are not automatically safer. Every complete backup can also become another theft path.

The goal is enough redundancy to survive loss without putting an unauthorized complete spending path in one location or person's control.

== DO NOT IMPROVISE A THRESHOLD BY SPLITTING WORDS ==

Do not divide one ordinary seed phrase between two people and call it multisig or dual control.

The wallet still needs the complete original backup. The fragments create a fragile two-part puzzle where loss of either can prevent recovery.

Use a supported custody method designed for the control required: a tested single-signature process, supported passphrase design, standardized backup system, multi-key wallet, or collaborative service.

I would not invent a threshold with scissors and envelopes.

== APPLY THE TESTS TO THE CUSTODY TYPE ==

**Custodial account:** the provider's authentication, beneficiary, and death-claim process controls access. The family needs the current provider process and documents.

**Single-signature hardware wallet:** any complete backup can control the funds. The design can still have strong physical redundancy and family readiness; it is not a no-single-key system.

**Passphrase wallet:** the backup and exact passphrase may both be required. Each side needs a recovery plan and the household must understand that a wrong passphrase can open another wallet.

**Multi-key wallet:** the threshold determines how many keys sign.

A 2-of-3 design has three keys and any two sign. No fourth key belongs in that description.

The setup may also require a non-secret wallet descriptor, configuration, or provider record to reconstruct the wallet. That information is not a private key, but it can reveal addresses and balances and should have protected redundant copies.

== SEPARATE THE FAILURE DOMAINS ==

Do not store all required components in the same home, safe, bag, bank box, or person's control.

Map the physical event, the human event, and the provider event.

Then check that no realistic single event removes enough components to stop recovery or gives one unauthorized party enough to spend.

== ALIGN LEGAL AUTHORITY WITH TECHNICAL ACCESS ==

Holding a key does not create legal authority. Being executor does not provide a key.

The attorney and custody professional need to understand who holds each component, who is legally authorized, who is an heir, and what the provider requires.

There is no universal rule that executor and heir must be different people.

There is a design test: if one person serving multiple roles ends up with every component required to move funds before the intended legal process, reconsider the roles or distribution.

== TEST WITH A PRACTICE WALLET ==

Use a small wallet built with the same type of threshold or provider process.

Confirm:

- the intended authorized combination can recover and spend,
- the design survives the loss it claims to survive,
- and one person cannot spend when dual control is intended.

For provider-assisted recovery, review the actual inheritance process and ask what happens if the provider is unavailable.

Document the date and result, never the keys.

== APPLY THE TESTS TO THE DEMO ==

In our demo, the operating exchange balance follows the provider's claim process.

The long-term balance first completes the family-ready single-signature process from Module 7.

Then the household compares:

- keeping the simpler tested single-signature design with separated backups and trained spouse,
- or moving to a professionally supported 2-of-3 design with separated keys and protected configuration records.

The first is simpler and retains one-complete-backup risk.

The second reduces one-key risk and adds provider, configuration, key-coordination, and estate complexity.

The household does not choose the more advanced design until it can explain and operate the trade.

== YOUR DECISION ==

State which tests the current setup passes, which one it fails, and whether the household accepts the limitation or moves to a supported multi-component design.

== PUT IT IN ORANGE PLAN ==

In Orange Plan, record custody type, component roles at a process level, people, provider, configuration-record location, and test date in Protect and the Family Custody Map. Never enter spending secrets.

== YOU ARE DONE WHEN ==

The authorized combination works, the design survives the loss it claims to survive, and unauthorized one-person spending is prevented when intended.


## MATCHING LESSON TEXT

# Design dual control and redundancy without creating a new loss path

## Two tests

1. Dual control: can one unauthorized person or component spend alone?
2. Redundancy: can one loss permanently stop recovery?

## Custody types

- Custodial account: provider process controls
- Single signature: one complete backup can control
- Passphrase: backup and exact passphrase may both be required
- Multi-key: threshold defines how many keys sign

A 2-of-3 wallet has three keys and any two sign. Protected wallet configuration records may also be required for recovery.

## Do not split ordinary words by hand

Dividing a normal seed phrase is not a designed threshold system and can create a fragile two-part recovery.

## Separate failure domains

Review physical, human, and provider events. Do not put all required components in one place or person's control.

## Align legal and technical roles

A key does not create legal authority. An executor title does not provide a key. Coordinate roles with counsel and the custody design.

## Practice test

Confirm the authorized combination works, the design survives its claimed loss, and one person cannot spend when dual control is intended.

## Done when

The household can state which tests the design passes and has proven the process on a practice wallet.

8.3 Write the heir letter and make sure the process starts
~7 min at 155 wpm · VOICE-MATCHED DRAFT — Austin review + estate attorney review pending
============================================================

The heir letter is the plain-language starting guide the family reads when the plan has to operate without you.

It is not the will and it does not grant legal authority.

Its job is to make the first hour a sequence of safe actions rather than a set of decisions made while grieving.

== THE FIRST PAGE ANSWERS FOUR QUESTIONS ==

1. Who should I call first?
2. What should I not do?
3. What assets and accounts exist?
4. Where are the legal and process documents?

The first call may be the executor, attorney, custody provider, or another person depending on the design.

The stop signs should be direct:

- do not type or share wallet backups, seed phrases, private keys, passphrases, PINs, or passwords.
- do not accept unsolicited recovery help.
- do not move Bitcoin before legal authority, custody procedure, and tax consequences are clear.
- and do not assume the will controls every provider account.

== WHAT BELONGS IN THE LETTER ==

The letter can identify categories of assets and accounts, including direct Bitcoin, collaborative or custodial accounts, retirement accounts, banks, brokerage, real estate, insurance, and business interests.

It can name the attorney, CPA, executor, technical helper, insurer, and custody providers.

It can state where the will, power of attorney, healthcare directive, executor packet, Family Custody Map, and policy records are held.

It can explain what the household wants the money to do in plain language.

I would keep it short enough to follow. A long technical procedure belongs in the provider documentation or executor packet. The letter tells the family which person or document comes next.

== WHAT STAYS OUT ==

No seed phrase, private key, passphrase, PIN, password, safe combination, full account number, backup photograph, or complete spending path.

The letter can say that a protected custody record exists in a named process location. It should not reveal the recovery secret or try to replace the provider's current claim procedure.

The attorney should review anything that might conflict with the legal documents or provider agreement.

== WRITE TO THE REAL PEOPLE ==

A useful opening can sound like:

“Call the attorney and your brother before moving any Bitcoin. They know the plan exists and are expecting to work together.”

“Nobody legitimate needs the wallet backup by phone, email, website, or chat. If someone contacts you first, stop and use the provider information in this packet.”

Then explain why the plan was built this way and what the family should protect first.

Review the letter with the spouse, executor, and required helpers while you are alive. Ask them to explain the first three actions back rather than only asking whether it makes sense.

== USE MORE THAN ONE DISCOVERY OR DELIVERY PATH ==

Keep a current copy under the document-storage plan reviewed with the attorney.

Make sure the spouse and executor know the letter exists and how to access or request it when authorized.

A cloud-delivery or dead-man-switch feature can add another notification path. It should not be the only copy or the only way the family learns the plan exists.

Email changes, spam filtering, service outages, missed check-ins, and stale recipients can prevent delivery.

== WHAT THE DEAD-MAN SWITCH DOES ==

A dead-man switch is a scheduled check-in and notification process.

If the owner stops confirming and the warning process completes, the service sends the approved no-secrets message or document to the named recipients.

It starts the process. It does not move Bitcoin, replace the will, grant authority, or store a wallet backup.

A switch that sends after the owner is unavailable requires the supported cloud service. A local-only browser cannot send a future message on its own.

Choose the current interval in the app, verify recipients, and understand the warning and trigger process. Do not hardcode an old 90-day default into the evergreen lesson.

== KEEP ONE CURRENT VERSION ==

Review the letter when a person, provider, custody setup, document location, or account changes.

Date it, retire old versions clearly, and verify recipient contact information.

A stale letter that points to the wrong provider can send the family in the wrong direction.

== WHERE COMPLETION COMES FROM ==

Protect can track the letter, review date, recipients, storage location, and switch status.

It cannot know whether the family understood it, the contacts still work, or the wording conflicts with a legal document.

The app owns the status. Family review and attorney coordination supply the proof.

== YOUR DECISION ==

Choose the first call, stop signs, recipients, and at least two ways the family can discover the plan.

== PUT IT IN ORANGE PLAN ==

In Orange Plan, create the no-secrets letter in Protect, verify recipients, record the storage plan, and decide whether automated delivery is an additional path.

== YOU ARE DONE WHEN ==

The letter is current, no-secrets, aligned with the legal and custody plans, understood by the key people, and discoverable through more than one path.


## MATCHING LESSON TEXT

## Four first-page questions

2. What should I not do?
3. What assets and accounts exist?
4. Where are the legal and process documents?

8.4 Calculate the family protection gaps
~8 min at 155 wpm · VOICE-MATCHED DRAFT — Austin review + licensed insurance review pending
============================================================

Insurance starts with the risk the household cannot absorb yet, not with a product.

The reserve self-insures smaller short-term problems. The investment portfolio may absorb some larger losses. Insurance covers the remaining gap when the consequence would otherwise break the family plan.

Use a licensed professional and the actual policy contract before buying, changing, replacing, or cancelling coverage.

== CALCULATE THE LIFE GAP ==

Add what the survivors still need:

- living support,
- debts the family intends or needs to pay,
- childcare, education, healthcare, final, and transition costs,
- and any specific commitment the plan should protect.

Then subtract:

- surviving income,
- liquid assets deliberately available,
- verified survivor benefits,
- and existing insurance.

Do not automatically subtract every Bitcoin. Decide whether it is liquid, pledged, taxable on sale, assigned to another goal, or intended to remain family Legacy capital.

For the demo household, current living spending is $80,000 and required debt payments are about $22,000. If Alex's $150,000 income disappears and Jordan continues earning $40,000, the first rough annual survivor gap is:

> $80,000 living + $22,000 debt − $40,000 surviving income = $62,000

That is not the coverage recommendation.

The real calculation changes spending after death, adds transition and family goals, accounts for survivor benefits, decides how many years the need lasts, and subtracts usable assets and current policies.

The $62,000 is the starting cash-flow question the licensed professional turns into a timed capital need.

== COMPARE TERM FIRST FOR A TEMPORARY NEED ==

Term life generally provides a death benefit for a defined period.

For a temporary family need, I would usually compare term coverage first because it is designed to cover a period and is often the simpler, lower-premium way to buy a given death benefit.

Permanent life insurance can address a lifelong need and may include cash value. It also has more costs, assumptions, surrender terms, management requirements, and lapse risk.

Permanent coverage can have legitimate uses in estate liquidity, business planning, special-needs planning, or another lifelong need reviewed with qualified professionals.

Do not buy from one attractive illustration or a banking slogan. Compare guaranteed and nonguaranteed values, fees, surrender charges, financing, ownership, and what happens under weaker assumptions.

Under current US federal rules, death proceeds paid to a beneficiary are generally excluded from gross income, while interest, transfer-for-value, ownership, estate, and other exceptions can change the result. The CPA and attorney packets hold the final wording for review.

== CALCULATE THE DISABILITY GAP ==

A disability can reduce income while healthcare or assistance costs rise and contributions stop.

Review the actual employer and individual policies:

- benefit percentage and monthly cap,
- who paid the premium and possible tax treatment,
- elimination period,
- benefit period,
- definition of disability,
- and which bonus, commission, or business income is excluded.

Then compare the expected after-tax benefit with bare-bones and normal spending.

The reserve may cover the waiting period. It is not automatically a substitute for years of lost earnings.

== REVIEW UMBRELLA LIABILITY ==

A personal umbrella can add liability and defense coverage above or outside parts of the home, auto, or renters policies, subject to required underlying limits and exclusions.

It generally does not insure damage to the policyholder's own property.

Review household drivers, property, rentals, business activity, public profile, and other liability exposure. Confirm the underlying limits the umbrella requires and the actual carrier terms.

== NAME THE LONG-TERM-CARE DECISION ==

Long-term care can create years of costs that normal health insurance does not cover in the same way.

Possible sources include family support, portfolio self-insurance, insurance, a hybrid product, public benefits when eligible, or a combination.

The comparison depends on age, health, state, care preference, premium, inflation protection, benefit period, elimination period, and the amount the portfolio can self-insure without damaging the spouse's plan.

For a younger household, the current action may be a future review date rather than a product purchase today.

== COVERAGE CHANGES WITH THE PLAN ==

The life need can shrink when debts are paid, children become independent, assets grow, and the household depends less on earned income.

The disability need changes when work changes or ends. Umbrella need can increase with assets and exposure.

Review coverage after marriage, divorce, a child, job change, major debt, business change, estate change, or significant increase in net worth.

Do not cancel existing coverage until a replacement is in force and the professional confirms the change.

== VERIFY BENEFICIARIES AND CLAIM INFORMATION ==

The insurer's beneficiary and ownership records need to match the estate plan.

The family should know the policy exists, who the carrier is, and where the claim information is stored. The heir letter does not need an account password.

Orange Plan tracks the checklist. The carrier holds the contract and beneficiary record.

== WHERE THE GAPS COME FROM ==

### Life gap

**What it means:** family need not covered by surviving income, usable assets, verified benefits, and existing insurance.

**Calculated from:** survivor cash flow, debts, commitments, transition costs, and available resources.

**Edit source:** survivor assumptions and real policy records.

**This affects:** coverage, premium, survivor plan, estate liquidity, and possible Bitcoin sales.

### Disability gap

**What it means:** required spending minus expected after-tax disability income.

**Calculated from:** policy terms, tax treatment, other income, and spending.

**Edit source:** real policy and Plan inputs.

**This affects:** reserve, supplemental coverage, and contribution continuity.

== YOUR DECISION ==

Choose which life, disability, liability, or long-term-care gaps need a quote or professional review now and which risks the household is deliberately self-insuring.

== PUT IT IN ORANGE PLAN ==

Track policies, owners, beneficiaries, carriers, dates, and open gaps in Protect or the Coverage Audit. Make real changes with the licensed professional and carrier.

== YOU ARE DONE WHEN ==

Each major risk has a calculated starting gap or deliberate self-insurance decision, the real policy terms and beneficiaries are verified, and consequential changes receive licensed review.
