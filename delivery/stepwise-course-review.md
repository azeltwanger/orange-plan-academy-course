The previous manuscript did not reliably establish that. It explained many topics, but the action often arrived late, the app work lived elsewhere, and a beginner could finish a video without knowing whether the planning step was complete. The redesigned course now makes the task, decision criteria, implementation and finish visible. Actual beginner use with the finished app remains untested.

## The promise the course can support

Build a first working plan, improve the decisions that matter to your household, finish the applicable outside actions and know how to review the plan when life changes. Confidence comes from knowing what the plan assumes, how it pays the bills, what could fail and what action to take. A high simulation percentage alone does not establish that confidence or guarantee retirement income.

The main path keeps the existing 25 teaching recordings. Each contains short steps leading to a usable result. Eight situational lessons appear only when the member uses the relevant strategy. The ten app walkthroughs are recorded in chapter takes and played beside the teaching they implement; they are not an eleven-video homework assignment after the course.

## What needed fixing

- Actions were buried inside explanations. Each teaching script now opens with the work to do and gives the judgment needed to do it.
- App navigation risked dating the teaching. Complete walkthrough speech, exact overlays and capture dependencies are now separate from teaching teleprompters.
- The example could silently change states. Original cash flow and a proposed spending reduction must be labeled; a proposed transfer is separate from an executed one.
- Missing facts could become invented precision. The revised flow identifies incomplete holdings, missing tax history and stale calculations and explains which work can continue.
- Technical branches could overwhelm everyone. College, loan management, unusual financing, conversions, tax-sensitive sales, early account access, more involved custody and trusts retain explicit entry/return routes.
- Completion could mean merely watching. Checkpoints name a saved decision, a verified source or an outside action that remains open. No new member worksheet or submission is required.

## Example: build the Reserve

The [full teaching script](../scripts/02-3_size-the-reserve-for-the-job-it-has-to-do.md) begins: “Work out your Reserve in five steps: essential monthly spending, months of cover, existing money available, the gap, and the pace for closing it.” It then explains how income stability, dependents, access and competing debt payments affect the choice.

| Teaching step | Exact useful overlay | Separate implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Identify essential costs and select months | Fictional: $7,200 × 6 = $43,200 target | W02 chapter 4: enter the target and verify the spending definition |
| Assign eligible existing money | $43,200 target − $32,000 assigned = $11,200 gap | W02 chapter 4: show existing sources without creating a second asset |
| Compare the funding pace with other commitments | $11,200 ÷ $500 = 22.4 contribution-months | W02 chapter 5, then W03: show the same monthly money going to Reserve and extra debt once |
| Implement the settled choice | Save the choice · Arrange the transfer with your bank | Record the chosen pace separately from the actual bank transfer |

The member finishes with a reasoned target and an affordable funding plan. The course does not pretend that watching the lesson funded the missing $11,200.

These are comprehension corrections, not a claim that a learner pilot occurred.

## What will establish beginner usability

After the matching redesigned flows are available, observe a few people unfamiliar with the app using the paired lessons with their own private plan. This is a production usability check, not another member assignment. Do not coach them through the task before seeing where they get stuck.

Observe whether they can start with imperfect records, identify the next missing item, enter balances without duplication, understand the tested spending/date, respond to a shortfall with a realistic change, save the intended choice and distinguish pending outside work from completed work. Then observe a maintenance return: a changed income or expense, the resulting plan review and the next action.

Record each place they need an unexplained term, an undisclosed app step or an instructor rescue. Repair that lesson or screen, then repeat the affected task. Their explanation of how bills are funded and what would trigger a change is better evidence than asking whether the course felt reassuring. No confusion-free claim is made before this observation.
