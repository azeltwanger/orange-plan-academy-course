## What was cut, and what was deliberately retained

Repeated intros, recaps and announcements about the example were removed. Inventory and entering its facts are one working explanation. Cash-flow reconciliation and realistic spending changes now share the same original-versus-reduced Reed case. Debt decisions use the current payment before assigning extra money; the fuller lender-specific calculation moves into the operating choice. Allocation begins with the other-$500,000 problem and derives the cash amount before teaching the relevant investment choices. Account availability and Traditional/Roth share one lesson; current and future contributions share another.

Tax proceeds/basis/gain stays a complete basic comparison. Detailed reconstruction and transaction identification move to the situational sale lesson; the main video still explains gain and loss harvesting, not a vague referral. The tax-timing lesson contains the conversion opportunity cost and intentional-spending alternative. Retirement combines complete spending, income, access dates and source accounts; healthcare includes the previously separate income interaction; sequence, finite cash and annual spending form the operating decision. Simulation definitions are taught once with the first result, then used.

Custody retains control, responsibility and failure checks. Recovery and account hardening are one protection lesson, with exact-device capture separate. Family ownership, legal authority, the custody map and usable first instructions are connected. Monthly updates, annual decisions and urgent changes share the maintenance lesson. The final review is the real saved plan and outside actions, not a listener exam.

The three retained task references are not more launch recordings. Custom-control details, jurisdiction-specific move questions and UTXO work are needed only when those tasks occur. No student must read them as homework to continue an unrelated plan.
