## Support operations: setup kit, not service activation

The landing page names a private community, one monthly group Plan Clinic, structured submissions, periodic workshops, Annual Plan Refresh and updates during active membership. This file provides the setup content but confirms none of those services live. No new price, renewal entitlement, response-time promise or private-service scope is approved here.

**Question submission fields:** relevant lesson; the decision you are working on; what you tried; what is still unclear; general assumptions needed to understand the question; permission to discuss a de-identified version; optional preference not to be recorded. Show a warning before submission: no logins, recovery secrets, account identifiers, full backups or private medical/legal documents. Members need a way to decline publicity without losing ordinary support access.

Do not promise every question is answered live or a recording is available until that policy is approved and configured.
