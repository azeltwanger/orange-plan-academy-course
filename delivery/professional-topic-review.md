## Specific corrections

These are mainly **important qualifications the earlier wording left general**, not eleven claims that the course's framework was wrong. The change IDs provide a stable way to review them without adding material to the member's reading path.

### P01 — An IRA is not personal loan collateral

**Location:** 4.5; W04 chapter 5. The earlier self-directed-account paragraph described responsibilities without naming an especially consequential misuse. Add that personally pledging IRA Bitcoin or selling personally owned Bitcoin to one's own IRA is not ordinary account administration. [S3]

**Production qualification:** Do not teach that every pledge automatically destroys every IRA. The pledged-portion distribution rule and the broader prohibited-transaction loss-of-status rule have different scopes. Any proposed account structure needs its actual legal facts reviewed. [S1]

### P02 — Explain “qualified Roth” and distinguish a workplace deferral

**Location:** 4.6; W04 chapter 6. The example already assumes qualified Roth treatment. Add the ordinary age-and-five-tax-year condition so the learner knows what that word means, while keeping the separate contribution/conversion ordering lesson in A6.3. Change “deductible Traditional contribution” in the payroll example to “pretax workplace contribution.” [S1, S14]

### P03 — Avoid implying an HSA is permanently medical-only

**Location:** 4.5; W04 chapter 5. Clarify the documentation and timing for tax-free medical reimbursement and the later nonmedical-use distinction. An HSA can have a healthcare job without a false claim that every other use is forever prohibited. Current contribution eligibility remains a separate question. [S4]

### P04 — Show what conversion withholding and existing IRA basis change

**Location:** 5.4; W05 chapter 4. Money withheld is not also in Roth; a taxable amount not rolled over can have an additional early-distribution consequence. Existing after-tax basis generally needs the owner's combined Traditional/SEP/SIMPLE IRA calculation, not the balance of a conveniently chosen account. Preserve the existing example's separate tax funding and opportunity cost. [S2, S8]

### P05 — A securities wash sale involving an IRA can permanently lose the deduction

**Location:** A5.2; W05 chapter 5. The earlier text correctly required replacement review but did not explain this consequence. Add the own-IRA/Roth-IRA replacement warning. The usual replacement-basis adjustment is unavailable in that case. This does not classify every Bitcoin instrument identically or promise that a direct-Bitcoin repurchase is always permissible. [S5]

### P06 — Waiting for Social Security has a stopping point

**Location:** 6.2; W06 chapter 2. Add that delayed retirement credits for one's own retirement benefit stop at 70. Spousal/survivor comparisons are not replaced by that rule. [S11]

### P07 — Coverage after a job is not necessarily Medicare enrollment protection

**Location:** 6.3; W06 chapter 3. The earlier paragraph said to coordinate enrollment. Add the actionable distinction: COBRA/retiree coverage does not extend the usual active-employment-based Part B enrollment window. Add premium-free Part A backdating and the effect on permitted HSA contributions. Exact dates and available exceptions need the member's coverage facts. [S12, S4]

### P08 — Marketplace and Medicare do not use one interchangeable income number

**Location:** A6.1; W06 chapter 3. Explain the principal add-backs for Marketplace income, the standard-deduction distinction, and Medicare IRMAA's different measure and usual lookback. Include repayment of already-used premium assistance in the comparison's cash need. Put the dated 2026 rule in the recording checks below. [S13]

### P09 — Employer-plan SEPP requires separation

**Location:** A6.3; W06 chapter 2. Add the separation-from-that-employer condition for the employer-plan periodic-payment exception, without attaching that employment condition to IRA SEPP. Retain the existing duration, modification and account-specific calculation cautions. [S10]

### P10 — Wallet inputs are not automatically tax-lot identification

**Location:** A7.4; W05 chapter 2 verification. Coin control chooses outputs. A consolidation can combine histories; it does not establish a compliant tax identification merely by selecting an output. This is a cross-check between the wallet mechanism and the IRS record/identification requirements, not a new claim that all outputs correspond one-to-one with lots. [S7, S26]

### P11 — Incapacity authority needs durability and effective scope

**Location:** 8.1; W08 chapter 1. Replace the general POA explanation with a check of durability, effective date and granted powers. The document's state-law validity and its use with the actual institution remain outside what an educational script can establish. [S28]

## Dated recording and calculation checks

Use these when producing screenshots and examples for the relevant year. They are **not additional member lessons**, and their appearance here does not mean the app already implements them.

**Marketplace, 2026.** The temporary enhanced premium assistance ended after 2025. Under the current IRS FAQ, excess advance Premium Tax Credit payments for tax years after 2025 have no repayment cap: calculate the full excess. Do not reuse a 2025 subsidy assumption or an older cap when a conversion raises income. Determine actual household eligibility and credit on the applicable year's inputs. [S13]

**HSA, 2026.** Notice 2026-5 implements changed treatment for qualifying Bronze/Catastrophic individual coverage and specified direct-primary-care arrangements. Do not reject qualifying coverage using an old blanket rule, or assume every high-deductible/low-premium arrangement is eligible. Contribution eligibility, qualified spending and Medicare enrollment remain different tests. [S4]

**Digital-asset identification, 2026.** Notice 2026-20 extends specified alternative identification relief through December 31, 2026 for eligible broker-held transactions. It requires timely records; it is not an after-the-fact selection privilege or a universal unhosted-wallet rule. A broker statement, a planning-app method and legal identification are separate evidence. [S6, S7]

**Medicare dates.** The usual active-employment Part B special enrollment period generally ends eight months after employment or its group coverage ends, whichever comes first; COBRA does not restart it. Premium-free Part A can be retroactive up to six months after a delayed application, but not before eligibility. Calculate HSA eligibility by effective covered months; do not tell everyone to stop HSA contributions six months before their 65th birthday. [S12, S4]

**Required distributions.** Use birth year, account type, employment/ownership exceptions and beneficiary status. The 2024 final regulations provide age 75 for people born in 1960 or later; generic IRS pages centered on today's age-73 cohort are not a sufficient long-horizon rule table. Those final regulations reserved the 1959 paragraph: verify the applicable later authority for any 1959 example rather than silently infer it. No 1959 example is added here. [S9]

**Owner Roth versus inherited Roth.** Owner lifetime RMD treatment does not remove beneficiary distribution requirements. A universal ten-year rule for every heir, or unconditional tax-free earnings regardless of the owner's qualifying period, would be wrong. The current course does not make those blanket claims; retain that distinction in any filmed inheritance example. [S1, S21]

**Cash access.** A money market fund is not an FDIC-insured deposit. For a newly issued marketable security bought in TreasuryDirect, account for its 45-day transfer/sale holding rule and the broker/bank transfer needed for an early sale. A four-week bill bought there matures before it can be transferred; reinvestment exceptions require their own check. That custody route is not the same as holding a bill at a brokerage. [S15–S17]

**Wallet procedures.** The Trezor backup-check and passphrase documentation support the mechanism, not an executed test. Do not apply a model-specific interface or a vendor's simplified dust figure to every script type. No restore, transaction, secret disclosure or funded-wallet wipe was performed in this review. [S24–S26]
