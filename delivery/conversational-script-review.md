## Delivery reference

The original Austin dictation supplies direct examples of his phrasing and reasoning. Austin then supplied one client's private coaching journey: six paths containing five unique transcripts, because Call 3 and Call 4 are identical. The calls were read as reference material; no raw transcript, client name, personal balance or identifying anecdote is added to the course. The course continues to use the fictional Reed household and the existing labeled examples. Current factual corrections and the PR #227 future contract remain in force.

The useful delivery patterns are a short agenda tied to the person's question, an everyday explanation before the technical term, an example worked through aloud and a next step that carries the result forward. Repetition is retained when it helps the listener follow; it is removed when it merely restates the topic or adds an empty conclusion. The walkthrough voice explains what we are entering or comparing and what the result means.

## What the journey helped clarify

- Retirement spending needs an explicit connection to monthly living costs; it is easy to mistake the input for the salary being replaced.
- A chosen retirement date, a calculated earliest date and a success percentage answer different questions. The result is read beside the question actually tested.
- Checking money may already fund ordinary bills, taxes or irregular expenses. Those commitments matter before assigning a Reserve amount.
- Traditional retirement treatment, a taxable brokerage account, a new contribution and a Roth conversion need plain definitions before a tax comparison makes sense.
- A comparison becomes useful when the member can follow the money for a particular retirement year and explain which account funds it.
- Current information, a preview and an annual spending response need to remain identifiable while discussing the result.
- Beginning a family document is different from completing the applicable legal/provider process and rehearsal. A final lesson also needs a clear route back to monthly, annual and material-change reviews.

These observations inform the writing. The calls involved coaching and an earlier app; they are not evidence that a beginner can independently complete this revised course with the future app.
