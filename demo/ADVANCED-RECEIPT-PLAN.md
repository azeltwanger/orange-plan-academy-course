# Orange Plan Academy — Advanced evidence-receipt plan

**Manifest:** `demo/advanced-receipt-manifest.json`  
**Schema:** `demo/advanced-receipt.schema.json`  
**Current state:** all nine receipt groups are `hold`

The receipt proves which version of the app, agreement, quote, worksheet, device/provider process, or legal matrix was actually demonstrated. It does not convert that evidence into a broader professional approval.

## AD1 · Assumptions and holding overrides

Capture:

- app commit and recording date,
- current assumptions route and labels,
- Baseline versus Scenario state,
- 3%/4% comparison,
- holding-override field, save behavior, and downstream output,
- clear statement that a disappointing result is not a reason for an override.

Acceptance requires the visible app behavior to match A1.1–A1.2.

## AD2 · Bitcoin-loan model and drawdown

Use synthetic terms only.

Capture:

- $30,000 / 1.50 BTC / $100,000 reference inputs,
- starting LTV and threshold math,
- finite top-up/paydown resources,
- sell-versus-borrow against the same cash need,
- preview versus applied state,
- Bitcoin retained, loan balance, collateral, interest, and source reconciliation shown by the current app.

The receipt does not approve a lender or term.

## AD3 · Lender agreement and exit

Use a properly redacted or synthetic agreement reviewed for the lesson.

Capture:

- collateral ownership/control,
- permitted reuse/segregation,
- governing law and borrower claim,
- price source, notice, cure, liquidation, release, fees,
- incapacity/death process,
- and the written exit rule.

Acceptance requires lending/legal review of the claim map. The Academy does not endorse the provider.

## AD4 · Basis reconstruction and sale packet

Capture:

- 1.75 BTC quantity reconciliation,
- 1.25 complete, 0.40 reconstructed, 0.10 unresolved,
- original-source preservation,
- transfer matching,
- modeled lot and real identification evidence,
- unresolved state in the app,
- and CPA handoff packet.

Do not use real customer data.

## AD5 · Conversion, harvesting, and relocation

Capture three separate questions on one accepted Baseline:

- no / smaller / proposed conversion,
- no / smaller / proposed harvest,
- and full-life relocation Scenario.

Show current app state, source inputs, changed outputs, and professional hold. Do not combine all three changes into one opaque Scenario.

## AD6 · Account access and healthcare Bridge

Capture:

- year-by-year cash need,
- verified primary and backup sources,
- access/tax/additional-tax distinction,
- current or clearly synthetic healthcare quote,
- premium plus expected cost sharing,
- income/MAGI sensitivity,
- same-$100,000 funding comparison,
- and dated Medicare handoff.

Acceptance requires plan/account records, CPA review, health-insurance review, and current quote/source dates.

## AD7 · Passphrase practice wallet

Capture only a dedicated practice wallet.

Prove:

- backup-only wallet,
- exact passphrase wallet,
- receiving and sending,
- clean-device or supported recovery,
- and family process.

No secret may appear in video, screenshot, metadata, transcript, or repository.

## AD8 · 2-of-3 recovery and staged migration

Use a practice wallet and synthetic/limited-value migration.

Capture:

- exactly three keys and threshold two,
- configuration/descriptor process without exposing private material,
- intended and alternate authorized combinations,
- no one-key spending,
- provider-independent recovery where claimed,
- small live tranche and spend from destination,
- staged remaining transfer,
- and family practice.

The main household balance is never the first test.

## AD9 · Trust ownership and authority matrix

Use fictional documents and no-secrets worksheet.

Capture for each asset:

- legal owner,
- intended owner,
- life and post-death authority,
- beneficiary/provider record,
- technical access requirement,
- tax record,
- acceptance/proof,
- and unresolved mismatch.

The receipt may show the matrix. It may not show a wallet secret or imply a fictional document is legally valid.

## Receipt lifecycle

1. `hold` — required evidence not assembled
2. `candidate` — captured but not fully accepted
3. `accepted` — all named roles accepted their scope
4. `retired` — app, agreement, rule, quote, device/provider process, or course decision changed

A retired receipt cannot remain linked from a current walkthrough.
