2. **Cash Flow:** Normal living spending is $80,000 a year, and after the saved extra-debt payment the app leaves about $3,761 a month, of which $3,500 is routed to accounts.
3. **Retirement:** The plan reaches the 80% target in May 2032 at Alex age 51, while the saved household plan keeps retirement at 55 and $100,000 of annual lifestyle spending.
