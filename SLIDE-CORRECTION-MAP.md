# Orange Plan Academy — slide correction map

**Status:** pre-dictation visual reconciliation  
**Purpose:** preserve the strong live-client teaching visuals while identifying every slide sequence that would contradict the repaired scripts or current app.

The existing decks are source material, not the current course authority. Final slide edits happen after the corresponding script clears numeric, factual, and professional QA. This map is created now so Austin does not approve a script and later discover that the visual forces another conceptual rewrite.

## Classification

- **KEEP** — the concept and visual can remain with only design cleanup.
- **COPY / TERM** — visual remains; wording or app term changes.
- **NUMBER** — structure remains; replace with `DEMO-HOUSEHOLD.md` values or an actual demo-app output.
- **REBUILD** — the slide teaches a retired or materially changed model.
- **MOVE TO ADVANCED** — useful, but it interrupts the core path.
- **REMOVE** — duplicate, filler, live-coaching residue, or misleading claim.

## Global slide rules

1. One slide makes one thing obvious.
2. Do not place paragraphs from the lesson text on the slide.
3. Use the continuous demo household unless the slide says **separate illustrative example**.
4. Calculated app outputs remain placeholders until the demo checkpoint produces them.
5. Use current app language: **Plan confidence target**, **test runs**, **earliest date reaching your target**, **Reserve / Bridge / Legacy**, **Plan → Income**, and **Protect**.
6. Do not show exact tax thresholds, contribution limits, RMD ages, manufacturer steps, or provider terms in evergreen slides unless the deck is versioned and maintained.
7. The slide teaches the concept. The future walkthrough shows the current screen.
8. Remove old live-client “YOU / ME,” “homework,” and “next week” assignments from the self-paced core. Replace them with one **Your decision** and one **Done when**.

- every current slide that would force a conceptual or factual change is identified here,
