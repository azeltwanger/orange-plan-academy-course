## What Austin is reviewing

The structure and fictional household are already decided. During the final read, review only:

1. **Voice** — would I naturally say this on camera?
2. **Judgment** — do I agree with the recommendation and trade-off?
3. **Example** — does the continuous demo make the decision easier to understand?
4. **Terminology** — does this match the accepted app surface?
5. **Finish line** — would the learner know what was decided and when the lesson is complete?

Do not rewrite a section merely because another sentence is possible. Change it when it sounds unlike Austin, teaches the wrong judgment, or makes the decision harder to follow.

## Review marks

- `APPROVE` — keep it.
- `TIGHTEN` — right point, too long.
- `SAY IT THIS WAY` — dictate or type the replacement.
- `JUDGMENT` — recommendation or example needs Austin's call.
- `APP` — product behavior or wording needs another check.
- `REMOVE` — learner does not need it for the decision.

Change the script header to `AUSTIN APPROVED` only after corrections are applied to the script and lesson text and one clean final read is complete.
