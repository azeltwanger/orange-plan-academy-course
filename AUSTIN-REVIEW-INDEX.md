# Orange Plan Academy — Austin review index

**Purpose:** give Austin one place to perform the final voice-and-judgment pass after the preventable structural, numeric, app, and professional changes are complete.

**Do not begin the final read until `PRE-DICTATION-QA.md` marks the applicable lesson ready.** A script appearing here means the lesson has a current draft. It does not mean the demo output, professional claim, slide, or walkthrough has cleared its hold.

## What Austin is reviewing

The structure is already decided. During the final read, review only:

1. **Voice** — would I naturally say this on camera?
2. **Judgment** — do I agree with the recommendation and trade-off?
3. **Example** — does the demo make the decision easier to understand?
4. **Terminology** — does this match how the current app presents the concept?
5. **Finish line** — would a learner know what they decided and when the lesson is done?

Do not rewrite a section merely because another sentence is possible. Change it when it sounds unlike you, teaches the wrong judgment, or makes the decision harder to follow.

## Review marks

Use these marks while reading:

- `APPROVE` — keep the section.
- `TIGHTEN` — the point is right but takes too long.
- `SAY IT THIS WAY` — dictate or type the replacement wording.
- `JUDGMENT` — the recommendation or example needs your decision.
- `APP` — the current product behavior or term needs another check.
- `REMOVE` — the learner does not need it to understand or make the decision.

After corrections are applied to both the script and lesson text, change the script header to `AUSTIN APPROVED` only after one clean final read.
