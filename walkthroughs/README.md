## Current status

The concept and checkpoint work is complete. The exact recording path remains held until Austin uses the deployed seven-mission Build Your Plan flow end to end.

A preview source branch is not the final customer-facing authority. The deployed acceptance pass must confirm:

- mission labels and order,
- task labels and routes,
- focus/return behavior,
- automatic versus manual completion,
- skip wording,
- saved/preview/Scenario state,
- and safe screenshot evidence.

## Production rule

Every walkthrough begins with the planning decision, continues the same synthetic household, verifies one important result, returns to Build Your Plan, and states both:

- the app completion rule,
- the human planning finish line.

Manual checkmarks never prove wallet recovery, legal validity, provider acceptance, insurance coverage, or learner comprehension by themselves.

Do not move historical walkthrough files into this directory until they have been rebuilt and verified against the deployed flow.
