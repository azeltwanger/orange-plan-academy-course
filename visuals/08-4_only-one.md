# 8.4 · The nine 'only one' questions

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That single points of failure accumulate quietly.

## The visual
Nine short question chips in a grid, each beginning 'only one…'. Six are marked as hits on an illustrative household. A counter reads 6 of 9.

## Labels and data
Device, seed backup, location, person who knows, exchange login, uninformed heir, contradicting document, unrecoverable passphrase, multisig keys in one place.

## Motion
Chips stamp in; the six hits turn risk red one by one and the counter climbs to 6.
