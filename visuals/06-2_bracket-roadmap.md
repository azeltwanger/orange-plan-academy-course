# 6.2 · The three stages of retirement income

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That there's a window where your income is the lowest it will ever be.

## The visual
A horizontal timeline in three shaded stages: early retirement, pre-Social-Security, then Social Security plus forced withdrawals. An income line runs across, dipping low in the middle and stepping up at the end. The dip is shaded and labelled as the window.

## Labels and data
Stages only. Do not print bracket thresholds — those are law-set and go on screen from the app.

## Motion
The income line draws; the window shading fills in the dip and holds.
