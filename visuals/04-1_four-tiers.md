# 4.1 · The four allocation tiers

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That your tier falls out of three answers, not a number you pick.

## The visual
Left: three dials labelled education, conviction, custody. Right: a four-step ladder of tiers with a percentage range on each. An arrow from the dials to a highlighted step.

## Labels and data
Tiers with ranges as written in the lesson. Do not imply a recommended tier.

## Motion
Dials turn, then the matching ladder step lights.
