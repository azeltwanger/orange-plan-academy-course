# A6.2 · One year, priced three ways

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That the three options cost different things, not different amounts.

## The visual
Three columns for the same year of spending. Sell: tax cost. Borrow: interest plus an LTV risk marker. Hold: reserve drawdown. Each column shows what it costs AND what it risks, in two separate rows.

## Labels and data
Same spending need across all three. Use loan purple for borrowed cash, Bitcoin tint for pledged collateral.

## Motion
Columns build simultaneously so they're compared, not sequenced. Risk row lands last on all three.
