# A3.2 · The four debt plays and how each fails

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That every play has a named failure mode, and you decide before you borrow.

## The visual
Four cards in a row. Each: the play on top, the upside beneath in neutral ink, and the failure mode at the bottom in risk red on a soft red field. Equal visual weight, so no play looks recommended.

## Labels and data
The four plays as taught. Failure mode gets equal or greater space than the upside.

## Motion
Cards flip in one at a time; the failure-mode band slides up from the bottom of each after the upside is read.
