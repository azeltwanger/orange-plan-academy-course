# 2.4 · College is a funding stack

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That the number you have to fund is what is LEFT after everything else pays, and that it is divided among six sources rather than saved up front by one person.

## The visual
Two stacked halves joined by a single funnel.

Top half — subtraction, built as a vertical ladder: a tall bar labelled PROJECTED SCHOOL COST, with two slices visibly cut away from it (grants and scholarships, then student work). What remains is a shorter, brighter bar labelled REMAINING NET COST. This is the hero number; it must read as noticeably smaller than the bar it came from.

Bottom half — division: the REMAINING NET COST bar fans out into six labelled channels of unequal width, showing that families use different mixes and not every channel is used.

## Labels and data
Top: PROJECTED SCHOOL COST − grants and scholarships − student work = REMAINING NET COST.
Bottom, the six channels: existing 529 · new 529 contributions · cash flow while enrolled · planned Bitcoin or taxable sales · limited student borrowing · still unfunded.

Emphasis: "cash flow while enrolled" is the channel people forget — give it slightly more visual weight than its neighbours. "Still unfunded" is drawn as a legitimate channel, not an error state.

⚠ No dollar figures anywhere in this frame. Sticker price, net price and loan limits all move annually and are deliberately unspoken.

## Motion
The top bar builds first, then the two slices cut away one at a time. Beat. The remaining bar drops through the funnel and fans into six channels left to right.
