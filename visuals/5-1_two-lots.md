# 5.1 · Same sale, two different gains

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That basis is tracked by lot and a valid, timely specific-identification record changes which gain is reported.

## The visual
Two lot cards for the same asset with different acquisition dates and basis. One identical sale amount below. A timestamped record card appears before the sale and points to one lot. Show the gain from that identified lot, then repeat with the other lot as a separate valid instruction.

## Labels and data
Lot 1 and Lot 2 · same proceeds · different basis · identification recorded no later than the transaction. No tax rate quoted as fact.

## Motion
The identification record locks onto a lot before the sale executes; the gain then computes. Reset and repeat with the other lot. Never depict a tax lot being chosen after the sale.
