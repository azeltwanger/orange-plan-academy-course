# 5.1 · Same sale, two different tax bills

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That basis is tracked per lot, and picking the lot changes the bill.

## The visual
Two lot cards for the same asset with different purchase prices. One identical sale amount below. Two resulting gains, side by side, visibly different sizes.

## Labels and data
Lot 1 and lot 2 with different basis. Same proceeds. Show both gains. No tax rate quoted as fact.

## Motion
The sale amount drops onto lot 1, gain computes; then it slides to lot 2 and the gain visibly shrinks.
