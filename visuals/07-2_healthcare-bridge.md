# 7.2 · The healthcare bridge

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That there's a known-length, known-cost gap before Medicare.

## The visual
A bar spanning retirement age to 65, labelled with its length in years. Underneath, three cost paths compared as parallel bars: marketplace, health-sharing, COBRA. Each with what it costs and what it gives up.

## Labels and data
Illustrative monthly premiums only. Do not state subsidy thresholds as fact.

## Motion
The bridge bar draws first with its length, then the three cost paths slide in beneath it.
