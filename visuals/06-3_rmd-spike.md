# 6.3 · What conversions do to the spike

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That converting early shrinks the balance that later forces income.

## The visual
Two bars for the same household at the RMD age. Left: no conversions, tall tax-deferred bar with a spike marker. Right: with conversions, visibly shorter bar. A small stack of conversion years sits under the right bar.

## Labels and data
Illustrative balances. Do not print the RMD age as a fixed number.

## Motion
The right bar shrinks year by year as conversion blocks are added underneath it.
