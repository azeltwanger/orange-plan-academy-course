# 2.3 · Every future cost has a lane

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That the timeline picks the account, not your opinion of Bitcoin.

## The visual
Four horizontal lanes stacked by time horizon, left-aligned to a shared 'today'. Each lane labelled with its horizon and filled with its allowed holdings. A crossed-out chip on each lane shows what is NOT allowed there.

## Labels and data
0-1 yr: HYSA, T-bills · 1-3 yr: short Treasuries · 3-10 yr: balanced stocks/bonds · 10+ yr: Bitcoin sell schedule can start. Not-allowed chips: Bitcoin on the first three, cash on the last.

## Motion
Lanes build top to bottom; the not-allowed chips stamp in last.
