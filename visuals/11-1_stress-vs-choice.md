# 11.1 · Stress tests versus choices

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That the two kinds of scenario ask different questions.

## The visual
Two columns. Left, stress test: 'would I survive this?' with examples you don't plan for. Right, choice: 'should I do this?' with examples you might act on. Different question mark treatment on each.

## Labels and data
Stress: bear market, 50% drawdown after retirement. Choice: retire earlier, spend less, move states.

## Motion
The two questions type in, then examples drop under each column.
