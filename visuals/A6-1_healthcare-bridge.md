# A6.1 · The healthcare bridge

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That the pre-Medicare bridge has a known time window but only a current estimated cost, and that the options do not make the same legal promise.

## The visual
A bar spanning retirement age to 65, labelled with its length in years. Underneath, four parallel option cards:

- employer continuation / COBRA;
- Marketplace insurance;
- spouse or other eligible plan;
- health-sharing arrangement — visibly stamped **NOT INSURANCE / NO SAME LEGAL PAYMENT GUARANTEE**.

Each card has two rows: current premium estimate and high-cost-year exposure. A single MAGI arrow feeds the Marketplace card and also connects back to the tax plan.

## Labels and data
Use current illustrative estimates only. Never state subsidy thresholds, old enhanced-credit rules, or provider-specific sharing terms as durable facts. Show deductible / out-of-pocket maximum / network / duration / legal guarantee as the comparison fields.

## Motion
The bridge window draws first. The option cards slide in. The MAGI arrow moves once and changes the Marketplace estimate, making the tax/healthcare connection visible. The health-sharing stamp remains on screen throughout.
