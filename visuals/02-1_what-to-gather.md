# 2.1 · The 7 things to gather

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That gathering is finite and mostly a download, not a project.

## The visual
Seven document cards in a grid, each with a one-word label and a tiny state chip reading 'download' or 'look up' or 'think'. Cost basis card is visually heavier (thicker border, Bitcoin soft fill) and carries a 'start here' tag.

## Labels and data
1 Income · 2 Spending · 3 Assets · 4 Debts · 5 Benefits · 6 Life events · 7 Cost basis

## Motion
Cards flip from blank to filled as each is named. Cost basis card gets its 'start here' tag last and holds.
