# 4.4 · The six-rung waterfall

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That routing is an order, and the order is the whole decision.

## The visual
Six stacked rungs with a single stream of money falling through them, filling each before overflowing to the next. Each rung labelled and annotated with why it sits there.

## Labels and data
1 match · 2 high-interest debt · 3 HSA · 4 Roth IRA · 5 traditional · 6 taxable and Bitcoin

## Motion
The stream pours in and fills rung by rung; each rung's reason fades in as it fills.
