# 2.1 · Where the surplus actually comes from

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That surplus is what's left after three claims, not what's left over by accident.

## The visual
A single wide income bar at top. Three blocks subtract downward in order — taxes, living, debt payments — each pulling from the bar. What remains is a clearly labelled 'surplus' block in Bitcoin soft.

## Labels and data
Taxes #B85C50 · Living #8B93A1 · Debt payments #64748B · Surplus in Bitcoin soft with Bitcoin border. Illustrative monthly figures.

## Motion
Each claim slides out of the income bar in turn; the surplus block is what's left standing.
