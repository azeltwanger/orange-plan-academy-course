# 8.1 · The four documents, and which one generally controls

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That a valid beneficiary, POD or TOD form generally pays before anyone reads the will — and that the student's job is to VERIFY each one with the provider, not assume.

## The visual
Four document cards in a row. Beneath them, a separate beneficiary-form card sits on a higher level with an arrow that bypasses the will entirely and points straight to the account.

## Labels and data
Will, power of attorney, healthcare directive, trust if applicable. The form on its own tier.

## Motion
The will card tries to route to the account; the form's arrow fires first and the will's path dims.

## ⚠ What this graphic must not say
**Never render the flat claim "the form overrides the will."** It is right often
enough to be dangerous, and a picture asserts things more absolutely than prose:
an arrow that simply deletes the will is the visual form of the sentence lesson
8.1 exists to correct. State law, community-property rules, plan documents,
spousal-consent requirements and the validity of the designation all bear on it.

Draw **generally**, not **always**: the will's path dims, it does not vanish, and
the VERIFY-with-the-provider step is part of the frame rather than a footnote.
Registered as a load-bearing claim in `CLAIM-REGISTRY.md`, and `visuals/` is
scanned by `tools/check-layer-parity.py` — this graphic is a live layer, not a
decoration.
