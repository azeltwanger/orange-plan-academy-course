# 8.1 · The four documents, and which one generally controls

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That a valid beneficiary, POD or TOD form generally pays before anyone reads the will — and that the student's job is to VERIFY each one with the provider, not assume.

## The visual
Four document cards in a row. Beneath them, a separate beneficiary-form card sits on a higher level with an arrow that bypasses the will entirely and points straight to the account.

## Labels and data
Will, power of attorney, healthcare directive, trust if applicable. The form on its own tier.

## Motion
The will card tries to route to the account; the form's arrow fires first and the will's path dims.
