# 8.2 · Dual control, redundancy, and the delivery signal

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That dual control and redundancy are separate tests, and that a delivery signal starts a process but creates no legal authority or signing power.

## The visual
Left two-thirds: a 2×3 comparison matrix.

Columns:
- **Can one person spend alone?**
- **Can one loss block recovery?**

Rows:
- single-signature backup with redundant copies;
- mnemonic + exact passphrase;
- tested 2-of-3 multisig plus policy/descriptor.

Use checkmarks and accepted-failure labels rather than ranking the rows.

Right third: a check-in timeline. A missed check-in delivers the heir letter / document-location message to named contacts, followed by two separate arrows: **LEGAL AUTHORITY** to attorney/court/provider process and **TECHNICAL RECOVERY** to the intended recovery team.

## Labels and data
Descriptor/policy cannot sign · legal role ≠ key role · switch sends process, never secrets. Do not render real people, locations, recovery material, or a universal Level 2 holder arrangement.

## Motion
The two test columns evaluate each setup independently. The switch fires last, then stops at the process message; it never unlocks a wallet or appoints anyone.
