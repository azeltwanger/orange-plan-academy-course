# A3.1 · The three lines every loan has

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That a margin call is a sequence you can see coming, not a surprise.

## The visual
A single vertical LTV track with three labelled thresholds: release line (low), margin call (mid), liquidation (high). A marker sits at the starting LTV. Side notes give the action at each line.

## Labels and data
Release, margin call ~65-70%, liquidation ~80-85%, as ranges not promises. Note that every lender differs.

## Motion
The marker rises through the lines; at each one the matching action card slides in and the line flashes.
