# 8.1 · The five custody questions

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That custody is a diagnostic you can answer today, not a purity test.

## The visual
Five numbered question cards in a column, each with two answer slots: 'today' and 'should be'. A summary strip at the bottom counts how many match.

## Labels and data
Where held · what type · who knows what to do · what if you're unavailable · single points of failure.

## Motion
Cards appear one at a time; the 'today' slot fills, then the 'should be' slot, and mismatches mark themselves.
