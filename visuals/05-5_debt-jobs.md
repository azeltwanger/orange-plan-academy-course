# 5.5 · Every debt gets a job

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That a ledger with no job per row is a ledger you'll re-litigate forever.

## The visual
A ledger table. Each row: debt, rate, and a job chip (minimum only / accelerate / refinance / leave open). One row shows a mortgage at a low rate with 'minimum only' and a short reason.

## Labels and data
Illustrative debts and rates. Every row must have a job chip — that's the point.

## Motion
Job chips stamp onto each row in turn; the last empty row gets its chip and the table reads complete.
