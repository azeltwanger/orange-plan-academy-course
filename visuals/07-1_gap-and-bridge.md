# 7.1 · The gap, and the years it's biggest

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That the portfolio carries the most weight before the floor turns on.

## The visual
A time axis from retirement day to Social Security. Spending as a flat line on top. The income floor rises in steps underneath it. The area between them is the gap, shaded, and it is widest at the left. The whole widest stretch is bracketed as the bridge.

## Labels and data
Milestones: retirement day, 59½, Social Security start. Spending flat, floor stepping up.

## Motion
Floor steps rise one milestone at a time and the shaded gap visibly narrows as each step lands.
