# 7.3 · Draw and refill

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That you spend from the reserve and refill it on a rule, not a feeling.

## The visual
A two-tank diagram. Reserve tank feeds spending. Portfolio tank refills the reserve. Arrows labelled with the rule that governs each. A branch shows the good-year and cut-year refill paths.

## Labels and data
Good year: refill fully to 18 months. Cut year: refill to about 12 of 18. Target never moves.

## Motion
Spending drains the reserve, then the refill arrow fires along whichever branch the year took.
