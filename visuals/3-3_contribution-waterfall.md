# 3.3 · The five-step contribution waterfall

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
Every new dollar follows one order, and the destination changes when the job changes.

## The visual
Five stacked rungs with one stream of money flowing through them:

1. Reserve, while it is short
2. Employer match
3. Extra debt from the saved debt strategy
4. Bridge or Legacy based on when the money is needed
5. Target allocation inside the chosen account

Beside rung four, split the stream into two clearly labeled paths:

- **Bridge** — taxable or flexible money available before retirement-account access
- **Legacy** — long-term money routed through the best available tax wrapper

## Labels and data
Add a small state switch beneath the waterfall:

- **Now** — Reserve may still claim part of the surplus
- **After Reserve is full** — that amount flows to the next funded job

## Motion
The stream fills the Reserve first, captures the match, passes through the saved Extra Debt amount, splits into Bridge or Legacy, and finishes by buying the target inside the account.
