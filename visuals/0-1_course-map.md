# 0.1 · The course map

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
How 11 modules stack into one plan, and that every module is teach + do.

## The visual
A vertical or stepped path of 11 numbered stations, left to right. Each station a small card: module number, short name. Under each, two tiny chips: 'teach' and 'walkthrough'. The path starts at 'Your numbers' and ends at 'Your plan, reviewed yearly'. Ink on page background, stations in surface white with hairlines. No color coding by module.

## Labels and data
Stations: 0 Start here · 1 Foundation · 2 Cash flow + reserve · 3 Allocation · 4 Debt · 5 Tax · 6 Retirement income · 7 Custody · 8 Estate · 9 Maintenance · 10 Plan review

## Motion
Stations light up one at a time along the path; the two chips fade in under each.
