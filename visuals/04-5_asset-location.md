# 4.5 · The right account for each holding

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That the same holding is taxed differently depending on the wrapper.

## The visual
A grid: holdings down the left, wrappers across the top. Cells marked best / fine / avoid with a simple fill, no icons. The Bitcoin row is emphasised.

## Labels and data
Wrappers: taxable, tax-deferred, Roth, HSA. Holdings: Bitcoin, stocks, bonds, REITs.

## Motion
The Bitcoin row highlights and its best-fit cells fill last.
