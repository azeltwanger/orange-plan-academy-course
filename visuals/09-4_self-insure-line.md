# 9.4 · What you can and can't self-insure

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That the stack absorbs some risks and not others, and that line moves.

## The visual
A horizontal line. Below it, risks the stack can absorb. Above it, risks it cannot — with the products that cover them. The line is labelled 'as the stack grows, this line rises'.

## Labels and data
Term life, disability, liability, long-term care. No premium figures.

## Motion
Risks sort themselves above and below the line, then the stack grows and the line rises, moving one risk below it.
