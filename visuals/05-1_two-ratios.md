# 5.1 · The two debt ratios

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That one ratio is stable and one moves with Bitcoin.

## The visual
Two gauges side by side. Debt-to-income on the left, marked 'doesn't move with Bitcoin'. Debt-to-assets on the right, with a ghosted second needle showing where it swings in a drawdown. Band markings on both.

## Labels and data
DTI bands and DTA bands as taught. Show the DTA swing, not a single reading.

## Motion
Both needles settle, then Bitcoin drops and only the DTA needle jumps to its ghost position.
