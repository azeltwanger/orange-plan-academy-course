# 6.4 · The room you're leaving empty

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That unused low-bracket room does not carry forward.

## The visual
A glass or vessel representing this year's 0% capital-gains room, partly filled by existing income. The empty space above is labelled 'room this year'. Beside it, an identical empty glass labelled 'next year — does not carry over'.

## Labels and data
Illustrative room figure. Emphasise the expiry, not the amount.

## Motion
The glass fills partway, the remaining room highlights, then the year ticks over and the unused portion vanishes.
