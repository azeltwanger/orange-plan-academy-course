# 4.2 · Reserve, Bridge, Legacy

**Paste `00-STYLE.md` first, then this.**

## What it has to make obvious
That every dollar has a job tied to when you need it.

## The visual
Three containers side by side on a shared time axis. Reserve (0-3 yrs) filled with cash color. Bridge (3-10 yrs) mixed stocks/bonds. Legacy (10+ yrs) Bitcoin. Each container labelled with what it funds.

## Labels and data
Horizons 0-3, 3-10, 10+. Targets as a share of investable assets, not net worth.

## Motion
Containers fill in order, Reserve first, and the time axis extends as each appears.
