# Start here — course reading order

Work through each main lesson and its matching walkthrough. **For your situation** identifies extra teaching for a decision you may or may not face. It is not a skill level. When you use that strategy, complete the lesson before relying on it; otherwise carry on. The Reserve remains the accepted reference; other wording is for owner review.
