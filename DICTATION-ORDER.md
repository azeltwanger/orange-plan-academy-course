## Settle these five before you record anything

These are the only things that, if you change your mind halfway through, make
you go back and re-record earlier lessons. Everything else is local to its own
lesson.

**1 · The couple's numbers.** They appear in 13 teach lessons. Working years:
income $190,000 · spending $80,000 · 1.75 BTC · $280,000 mortgage at 3.25% ·
$18,000 car at 7% · 401(k) $12,000/yr · Roth $7,000/yr. Retirement era:
$80,000 spend · $120,000 reserve · $600,000 taxable BTC · $400,000 traditional ·
$200,000 Roth · $51,600/yr combined Social Security.

**2 · The illustrative Bitcoin price: $100,000 a coin.** The cost-basis lesson
and the harvesting lesson both do arithmetic off it. Change it and both sets of
numbers have to be rebuilt.

**3 · 18 months as the retirement reserve default.** It's in 6 lessons, and
it's framed as a sequence-risk planning default, not a prediction about how
long a Bitcoin recovery takes. Your 2.2 dictation is the canonical wording.

**4 · Reserve / Bridge / Legacy as the bucket names.** These are the app's own
labels, so renaming them on camera desyncs the course from the screen.

**5 · The monthly pass is about five minutes.** Just corrected everywhere,
including your 0.1. If you'd rather say a range, say it here and it gets
changed once, in both places, before you record.
