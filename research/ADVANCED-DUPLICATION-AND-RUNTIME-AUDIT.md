# Orange Plan Academy — Advanced duplication and runtime audit

**Status:** complete before Austin's review  
**Current set:** 18 scripts · 14,805 spoken words · 95.5 raw minutes at 155 wpm · 139 production minutes from headers

## Goal

Advanced should answer the conditional decision without becoming a second required financial-planning course or a provider manual.

A lesson earns spoken time when it gives the learner:

- the gate,
- the decision mechanism,
- one worked example,
- Austin's judgment,
- the consequential trade-off or failure mode,
- the app/professional boundary,
- and the finish line.

Changing tables, agreement clauses, device procedures, state rules, and quote details belong in lesson text, maintained reference, or the versioned demonstration.

## Runtime rule

Do not add material during Austin's review merely because it is accurate.

The preferred edit is **replacement, not accumulation**. When Austin supplies a better story or explanation, remove the weaker paragraph it replaces.

There is no forced word-count target after Austin's read. The current 14,805 words should not grow. A reasonable post-review outcome would be approximately 12,000–13,500 words if the four flagged synthesis/implementation lessons can be compressed without losing their decisions.

- which inputs remain fixed,
- which variables change across runs,
- why comparable paths isolate a Scenario,

## A2.1, A2.2, A2.3 and A2.4

- A2.1: **What changed and where are the price lines?**
- A2.2: **Can the household survive, repay and exit?**

- A2.4: **Is the specific provider/agreement acceptable?**

# Acceptance test

An Advanced script passes when:

- the gate can be stated in one sentence,
- the learner question is answered before implementation detail,
- one example does most of the teaching,
- no adjacent lesson owns the same explanation,
- Austin's recommendation is visible but conditional,
- the external owner is named,
- the finish line changes the plan or records a deliberate pass,
- and cutting any remaining paragraph would remove understanding rather than only information.
