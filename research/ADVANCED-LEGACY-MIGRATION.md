## Retired claims and structures

Do not carry forward:

- `SPOKEN-PROSE VERSION (calibrated)` as a provenance claim
- one universal RMD age in evergreen video
- a rigid contribution waterfall
- “interest-only beats amortizing” as a general rule
- 40–50% LTV described as a normal or acceptable default
- lender threshold ranges without the actual agreement
- the old separate deterministic-versus-Monte-Carlo framing
- mandatory passphrase as the family-ready custody level
- a four-key diagram described as 2-of-3
- universal seed/passphrase inheritance splits
- “Bitcoin in a trust” as one strategy with one tax result
- specific state-tax savings or current law thresholds in evergreen video
- device wipe as the universal first recovery test

## Migration acceptance test

A legacy file is fully accounted for when every useful example or warning is either:

- preserved in a current script,
- moved to maintained lesson text,
- placed in a professional-review packet,
- or explicitly retired with a reason.
