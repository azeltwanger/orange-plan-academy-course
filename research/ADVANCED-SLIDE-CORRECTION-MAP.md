# Orange Plan Academy — Advanced slide correction map

**Purpose:** preserve the strongest live-teaching visuals without carrying retired claims into the rebuilt Advanced Library.

## Decision codes

- **KEEP** — concept remains accurate; restyle and update language
- **REBUILD** — teaching device is useful but facts, labels or structure are wrong
- **MOVE** — belongs in Advanced or maintained reference rather than Core
- **REMOVE** — unsafe, inaccurate, duplicative or product-specific without evidence
- **PRO HOLD** — final wording/visual waits for outside review
