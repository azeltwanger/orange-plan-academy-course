# 0.2 visual override — Use Orange Plan AI to make better decisions

**This brief supersedes the older 0.2 visual direction in `VISUAL-PRODUCTION-BRIEFS.md`.**

## What the visual must make obvious

Orange Plan AI is useful for five planning jobs:

> Understand → Prioritize → Compare → Challenge → Act

The app calculation remains underneath all five jobs, but the main visual is about usefulness rather than limitations.

## Primary visual

Show one planning question moving through four answer blocks:

### Question

> We want to retire at 55 without making a Bitcoin-backed loan necessary. What is the biggest constraint?

### AI review

1. **Verdict** — direct planning read
2. **Evidence** — current plan number and source
3. **Trade-off** — what the choice gains and gives up
4. **Next move** — action now, model next, or cleanup

### Household decision

> Update the plan, test one specific Scenario, or take the real-world action.

## Secondary visual

A two-column question comparison:

| Vague | Useful |
|---|---|
| Is my plan good? | Rank the top three issues by impact. Tell me what is strong, what holds back age 55, and what can wait. |
| Should I sell Bitcoin? | Compare selling, using another holding, and borrowing. Show tax, interest/LTV risk, Bitcoin retained, and plan effect. |
| Why is this number wrong? | Reconcile the output from its source rows and show where each source is edited. |

## Small footer only

Keep boundaries visually secondary:

> App math wins · No secrets · Verify execution details

Do not use a large red warning strip as the main composition. Safety belongs in the footer and spoken close, not as the purpose of the lesson.
