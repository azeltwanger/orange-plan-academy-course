# A2 · Bitcoin-backed borrowing and leverage

## Verified durable mechanisms

- LTV is loan balance divided by the current value of pledged collateral.
- A threshold price can be derived from loan balance, pledged Bitcoin, and the applicable threshold LTV.
- Opening a bona fide loan generally adds cash and an offsetting liability rather than creating net worth on day one.
- A collateral liquidation can create a taxable disposition; the borrower does not avoid tax merely because the sale was initiated by the lender.
- Cancelled or forgiven debt can create a separate tax issue under current federal rules.
- Loan-proceeds treatment, interest deductibility, tracing, ownership, liquidation, cancellation, and estate repayment require fact-specific tax review.

## Agreement-specific facts that cannot be generalized

The signed agreement owns:

- collateral title and control,
- segregation and permitted reuse,
- governing law and insolvency claim,
- valuation source and frequency,
- action, margin, and liquidation thresholds,
- notice and cure period,
- partial versus full liquidation,
- top-up and collateral-release mechanics,
- interest, capitalization, renewal, fees, and prepayment,
- death and incapacity process,
- and dispute or complaint procedure.

The course therefore does **not** call one starting LTV, lender structure, top-up policy, or liquidation line normal or safe.

# A3 · Tax optimization and implementation

## Digital-asset basis and identification

The current federal framework supports the course's central distinction:

- Orange Plan can compare lots for planning.
- The real disposition must be identified and documented under the rules that apply to the broker account or non-broker wallet.
- Prior filed treatment determines which units and basis remain; the household cannot rewrite earlier dispositions because a different method now looks better.
- Unknown basis remains visibly unresolved.

Exact identification timing, broker support, wallet-level records, transitional relief, and reporting fields are changing reference material and belong with the CPA or tax attorney.

## Roth conversions and retirement-account access

The course correctly avoids treating “fill the bracket” as an automatic recommendation.

Current implementation can depend on:

- existing ordinary income,
- state tax,
- Social Security,
- Marketplace or Medicare-related income effects,
- estimated payments and withholding,
- pro-rata and basis records,
- Roth ordering and multiple five-year rules,
- and the cash used to pay tax.

RMD starting age depends on birth year and current law. Roth owner and inherited-account rules are not interchangeable.

## Harvesting and relocation

- A realized loss or gain matters only when the after-tax, exposure, basis, and future-plan result improves.
- Repurchase and identification treatment can differ by asset and current law.
- State relocation requires real residency or domicile facts, timing, sourcing, records, and full household costs—not merely a different tax-rate input.

# A4 · Early-retirement access and healthcare

## Account access

The current federal framework supports the course's distinction between:

1. whether a distribution is available under the plan,
2. whether it creates income tax,
3. and whether an additional early-distribution tax applies.

The qualified-plan separation-from-service exception associated with the year the employee reaches 55 is not an IRA rule and does not guarantee that the employer plan permits the desired withdrawal pattern.

Roth IRA contribution basis, conversion amounts, and earnings require ordering and basis records. A 72(t)/SEPP series is not a flexible withdrawal account and remains professional-only implementation.

An HSA is a qualified-healthcare source when the expense and records qualify; it is not unrestricted Bridge spending.

## Healthcare

The Advanced lessons correctly require household-specific current quotes rather than one national premium assumption.

The full planning cost can include:

- premium or membership amount,
- deductible or member responsibility,
- expected out-of-pocket spending,
- maximum contractual exposure when one exists,
- network and prescription restrictions,
- exclusions or non-guaranteed payment risk,
- and the reserve needed to carry the arrangement.

Marketplace savings use current household-income rules. Spendable cash and the applicable income measure are not the same number. Withdrawals, conversions, gains, interest, dividends, wages, qualified Roth distributions, HSA reimbursements, and basis return can affect cash and income differently.

COBRA, Marketplace enrollment, Medicare timing, and health-sharing arrangements require current plan and program review. A health-sharing membership is not presented as insurance.

# A5 · Advanced custody

## Verified durable mechanisms

- Under supported deterministic-wallet designs, backup words plus an exact passphrase can derive a different wallet from the same backup words.
- A passphrase can reduce the consequence of one discovered backup and can create a second permanent-loss path.
- A 2-of-3 wallet has three keys and any two sign.
- Key count alone does not prove recoverability; configuration or descriptor records, compatible software, physical distribution, provider terms, and human capability matter.
- A migration should prove the destination and recovery process with a small practice wallet or tranche before moving the main balance.
- UTXO consolidation is a fee and privacy decision rather than automatic housekeeping.
- Phishing-resistant authentication and independently verified support channels are preferred over relying on manually entered one-time codes or unsolicited support.

## Device- and provider-specific facts held outside evergreen video

- exact passphrase entry and backup procedure,
- supported recovery check,
- descriptor or configuration export,
- signing and PSBT workflow,
- address verification,
- key replacement,
- provider-independent recovery,
- and inheritance or succession process.

The manufacturer, coordinator, provider agreement, and real practice test own those claims.

# A6 · Trusts and complex estate planning

## Verified course position

- Bitcoin ownership alone does not establish a trust need.
- The trust must be selected for a named legal or family problem.
- A signed but unfunded trust does not automatically control property that was never transferred, assigned, titled, or coordinated.
- Revocable and irrevocable trusts are not interchangeable tax, probate, creditor, or control strategies.
- Legal ownership, provider records, tax records, fiduciary authority, and technical Bitcoin access must agree.
- Possession of a key does not itself prove legal authority; legal authority cannot sign without a valid technical path.
- Retirement accounts and trust-beneficiary designations require separate plan, tax, and legal review.
- Trust income-tax treatment, completed-gift status, gross-estate inclusion, and basis adjustment are separate questions.

## Colorado and federal evidence boundary

Colorado has enacted a fiduciary-access framework for digital assets and has separate probate, personal-representative, trust, power-of-attorney, and beneficiary rules. The exact document language, fiduciary powers, minor-beneficiary design, trust funding, title evidence, and provider acceptance remain Colorado-attorney work.

Federal gift, estate, income-tax, and basis treatment depends on the actual structure and retained powers. The Academy identifies the questions; the attorney and CPA determine the result.
