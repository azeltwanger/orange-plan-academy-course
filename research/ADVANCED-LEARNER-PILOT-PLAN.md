# Orange Plan Academy — Advanced learner pilot

**Purpose:** verify that a triggered learner can use the conditional lesson without treating the example, app output, or professional boundary as a universal recommendation.

Advanced is not piloted as one linear course. Each participant tests only the section matching a real or believable planning condition.

## Participant groups

Recruit at least:

- 2 learners considering or already carrying a Bitcoin-backed loan
- 2 learners with taxable Bitcoin basis or a genuine tax-window question
- 2 learners planning retirement before 59½ / Medicare
- 2 self-custody users considering passphrases, collaborative custody, or multisig
- 2 parents / business owners / complex households with a real estate-planning trigger

One participant may test more than one section when the gate truly applies. Do not ask a beginner with no trigger to pretend every Advanced topic is relevant.

## Test sequence

For one lesson:

1. State the gate before watching.
2. Ask the learner whether the lesson applies and why.
3. Ask for a prediction: which input, risk, or proof should matter?
4. Watch the concept lesson.
5. Have the learner work the example without copying the conclusion.
6. Ask the learner to decide what they would do next in their own plan.
7. Ask what Orange Plan can calculate or record.
8. Ask what still requires an agreement, quote, record, device test, provider, or professional.
9. Ask which Core area must be updated after the decision.

## Scorecard

Score each item 0, 1, or 2:

| Skill | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gate | Watches from curiosity or misstates trigger | Recognizes part of trigger | Correctly states when to watch and when to skip |
| Concept | Repeats a slogan | Partially explains mechanism | Explains the causal decision in plain language |
| Example | Copies answer / cannot work it | Works with prompting | Rebuilds numbers or process and explains why |
| Trade-off | Names only upside | Names one downside | Explains what improves and what becomes worse/more fragile |
| App boundary | Assumes app proves the decision | Knows some external proof is needed | Separates calculation, recorded status, preview, and proof |
| Action | Leaves with more research only | Names a vague next step | Names one decision/action, owner, evidence, and return to Core |

A lesson passes when:

- average score is at least 1.5,
- no participant scores 0 on safety/professional boundary,
- and at least 80% can explain the lesson's decision without using the course's exact phrasing.

## Section-specific comprehension tests

### A1 · Modeling

The learner should be able to:

- explain why one changed assumption makes the comparison easier to interpret,
- distinguish confidence from a forecast,
- and use a holding override only for a real classification mismatch.

**Blocking misunderstanding:** changing assumptions until the preferred retirement date appears.

### A2 · Bitcoin-backed borrowing

The learner should be able to:

- calculate starting LTV and a threshold price,
- explain why top-up and cash resources are finite,
- compare a sale and a loan against the same cash need,
- name collateral/control/counterparty risks beyond price,
- and state a repayment and exit source not dependent on Bitcoin rising.

**Blocking misunderstandings:**

- calling an illustrative LTV safe or normal,
- treating auto top-up as unlimited protection,
- counting the same reserve several times,
- or concluding that retaining more BTC automatically means the loan is better.

### A3 · Tax optimization

The learner should be able to:

- distinguish complete and unresolved basis,
- explain why app lot selection must match the real documented sale,
- compare no conversion / smaller / ceiling,
- and state the non-tax costs or interactions that can change the result.

**Blocking misunderstandings:**

- inventing basis,
- rewriting old filed treatment,
- “always fill the bracket,”
- or harvesting without a repurchase/exposure plan.

### A4 · Access and healthcare

The learner should be able to:

- separate access, income tax, and additional tax,
- identify primary and backup sources for one early year,
- calculate all-in healthcare cost,
- and explain why spendable cash and Marketplace income are not identical.

**Blocking misunderstandings:**

- everything is locked until 59½,
- the full Roth balance is contribution basis,
- a health-sharing arrangement is the same as insurance,
- or a lower conversion tax automatically means lower total cost.

### A5 · Advanced custody

The learner should be able to:

- name the threat a passphrase solves and the new failure it creates,
- correctly describe 2-of-3,
- compare collaborative and DIY through family recovery,
- and outline a staged migration without exposing secret material.

**Blocking misunderstandings:**

- passphrase is always safer,
- memory is a backup,
- 2-of-3 contains four keys,
- a provider is required for recovery when the design claims independence,
- or the main balance should be the first test.

### A6 · Trusts and complex estate

The learner should be able to:

- name the problem a trust is intended to solve,
- distinguish signed from funded/implemented,
- separate key possession from legal authority,
- and identify the provider, tax, legal, and technical evidence still required.

**Blocking misunderstandings:**

- every Bitcoiner needs a trust,
- a trust automatically reduces estate tax or protects creditors,
- signing the trust transfers the Bitcoin,
- or naming a trustee creates wallet access.

## Observation prompts

Do not ask, “Did you understand?” Ask:

- “Show me where that number came from.”
- “What would make you choose the other option?”
- “What part can Orange Plan not prove?”
- “What would you take to the professional?”
- “Which Core decision changes after this?”
- “What should a person with no trigger do?”

## Revision rule

A repeated misunderstanding is repaired in the concept lesson before adding more walkthrough detail.

Use this priority:

1. Fix the gate.
2. Replace or simplify the example.
3. State Austin's judgment more directly.
4. Strengthen the trade-off or stop rule.
5. Move implementation detail to the professional/provider layer.
6. Add screen demonstration only when seeing the workflow is the missing piece.

## Pilot hold

The plan is complete; the pilot has **not been run**. Pilot results are required before filming the highest-risk Advanced sections: borrowing, tax implementation, healthcare interaction, advanced custody, and trusts.
