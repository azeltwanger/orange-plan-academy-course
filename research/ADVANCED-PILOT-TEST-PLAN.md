# Orange Plan Academy — Advanced learner pilot

**Purpose:** catch dangerous or confusing interpretations before final filming.  
**Minimum useful pilot:** 5–8 Bitcoin holders with different planning and custody experience. Show only the lessons whose gates apply.

## Pass standard

After a lesson, the learner can:

1. state why the gate applies,
2. work a parallel example,
3. explain the decision and trade-off,
4. identify which result came from Orange Plan and which came from an agreement, quote, record, device or professional,
5. avoid the named dangerous shortcut,
6. and state what changes back in Core.

Finishing the video or marking a task complete is not a pass.

## Test process

1. Ask the real learner question before the lesson.
2. Record the learner's answer and confidence.
3. Show the concept lesson without the walkthrough.
4. Give the parallel task below.
5. Ask the learner to source and qualify the result.
6. Show the current demonstration when available.
7. Record the exact wording or visual that created confusion.

## Scoring

- **0 — unsafe/confused:** repeats a blocking misunderstanding or cannot identify the decision
- **1 — partial:** understands direction but cannot trace sources, trade-offs or return to Core
- **2 — usable:** can explain, apply, source and qualify the decision

A lesson clears the pilot when:

- every participant whose gate applies scores 2 after the lesson,
- no blocking misunderstanding appears twice,
- and at least 80% complete the parallel task without prompting.

These are production rules for this pilot, not research claims.

## Failure response

Fix the causal explanation or example where the misunderstanding starts. Do not solve it with a longer disclaimer at the end. Retest the revised task with at least two people who did not see the first version.
