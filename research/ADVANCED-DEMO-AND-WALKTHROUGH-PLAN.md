## Demo standard

Every Advanced demonstration records:

- the lesson gate,
- the decision being implemented,
- synthetic or redacted inputs,
- current version/date/provider/device/document,
- saved versus preview state,
- the proof required outside the app,
- and the return to Core.

Never demonstrate a seed phrase, private key, passphrase, PIN, xprv, password, authentication code, real account number, or client record.

## What remains text-only

A lesson stays concept/reference-only when the implementation is too provider-, device-, state-, or fact-specific for a durable video. The professional or provider owns the execution; the Academy prepares the decision and questions.
