## Austin review test

For each Advanced example, ask:

1. Is the gate narrow enough that the right learner watches it?
2. Is this the example I would use with a client facing this exact issue?
3. Does the example simplify the decision rather than reward technical curiosity?
4. Is my recommendation clear and appropriately conditional?
5. Does the learner know what changes back in Core?

When the answer is no, replace the example before approval. Do not add a second example merely to make the lesson feel comprehensive.
