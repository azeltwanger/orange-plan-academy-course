# Foundation slide correction map

**Status:** pre-dictation visual audit  
**Source:** the eight existing live-client Foundation decks  
**Goal:** preserve strong teaching visuals, remove retired concepts, and know every visual change before Austin approves spoken scripts

## Status key

- **KEEP** — concept and visual structure remain useful; small copy polish allowed
- **EDIT** — keep the visual but change terminology, numbers, or scope
- **REPLACE** — the slide teaches a retired or materially wrong model
- **MOVE** — useful, but belongs in another module or the Advanced Library
- **REMOVE** — live-client residue, duplication, unsupported claim, or unnecessary slide
- **CHECKPOINT** — rebuild only after the canonical demo app output exists

The decks are visual source material. They do not override current scripts, the app, or professional review.

# Cross-deck corrections

## Terminology

Replace across all decks:

- Freedom date → planned retirement age / earliest date reaching target, depending on meaning
- Forever → Legacy
- Monte Carlo probability of success → confidence / percentage of test runs, with the lesson's exact definition
- Deficit, when it only means spending not covered by recurring income → portfolio-funded gap
- Beneficiary forms override the will → provider designation often controls under account terms and applicable law

## Live-client residue

Remove or rewrite:

- YOU / ME assignments
- “before next week” and “before we meet again”
- coach promises to update the app
- session numbering that conflicts with self-paced modules
- homework lists that duplicate the module's `DONE WHEN`

## Changing facts

Move to maintained lesson text or app output:

- contribution limits,
- tax brackets and thresholds,
- RMD ages and transition rules,
- estate exemptions,
- state tax examples,
- Social Security benefit amounts,
- device- and provider-specific recovery instructions,
- exact app button labels.

## Visual economy

For each concept lesson, use one dominant visual:

- Module 1 — input-to-confidence dependency
- Module 2 — surplus equation and reserve target
- Module 3 — debt gauges and treatment table
- Module 4 — current/target mix and next-dollar route
- Module 5 — year-by-year tax window
- Module 6 — first-year funding equation
- Module 7 — custody score and mismatch
- Module 8 — legal/custody/family alignment
- Module 9 — baseline versus Scenario and annual review loop

## Slide-production order

1. Replace retired or unsafe visuals before Austin's script approval where the visual changes the teaching.
2. Reconcile every demo number with `DEMO-HOUSEHOLD.md`.
3. Wait for app checkpoint outputs before building confidence, Income, tax-roadmap, and report result slides.
4. Preserve good layout and visual hierarchy from the original decks rather than redesigning every page.
5. Record exact app screens later in versioned walkthroughs, not in evergreen concept decks.
