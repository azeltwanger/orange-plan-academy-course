# Source material policy

Canonical scripts are under `scripts/`; derived copies flow outward only. Original permitted dictation under `source-material/` is immutable. Prior active material is retained in `archive/pre-guided-promotion/` and hash-checked by the build.

Raw client calls, personal financial records, medical anecdotes, account identifiers, credentials, recovery material and exact secret locations stay out of the public repository. Examples in active scripts and worksheets use the fictional Reed household or explicitly generic arithmetic. Do not upload font files.

Preserve source terminology and reasoning unless a newer owner decision, accepted product rule or documented factual correction changes it. Keep the reason in the amendment or alignment record. Historical claims about returns, providers, taxes, account limits or app behavior require current verification before they become present-tense claims. Source coverage is not proof of legal correctness or implementation.
