## 7. COMPLIANCE NOTES FOR YOUR ATTORNEY

These are the areas where I would not want you relying on a template. Flag each one for counsel.

**7a. Auto-renewal is the highest-risk mechanic in the product.** The FTC's "click-to-cancel" Negative Option Rule was **vacated by the Eighth Circuit in July 2025** and is not currently in effect. But that is not a green light. The FTC issued an Advance Notice of Proposed Rulemaking in **March 2026** to revive it, it continues to bring enforcement under **Section 5** of the FTC Act, and **ROSCA** (which was never vacated) already requires clear and conspicuous disclosure of material terms before billing information is collected, express informed consent, and a simple cancellation mechanism. Separately, **California's Automatic Renewal Law**, amended effective **July 1, 2025**, requires affirmative consent, renewal notice **15 to 45 days** before renewal for terms of a year or longer, online cancellation for online signups, and retention of consent records. California's law reaches your California customers regardless of where you are. The draft copy in sections 4.3 and 6b is written to the stricter standard on purpose.

**7b. The group/live-call component is where the education framing is most likely to break.** Recorded lessons that are identical for every student sit comfortably on the education side of the Investment Advisers Act's publisher's exclusion, which generally requires content to be impersonal, bona fide, and of general and regular circulation. A live call where you answer "should *I* do a Roth conversion this year?" is a different thing. Ask counsel specifically: what format keeps live sessions general, what standing language should you use to redirect personal questions, does recording the calls change the analysis, and does the answer differ under your state's rules.

**7c. Your professional background.** You worked in finance. Whether you hold, held, or previously registered under any license materially changes this analysis, and it is not something I can assess. Tell counsel the full history up front.

**7d. Marketing claims and testimonials.** The FTC's Endorsement Guides and the Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule (effective late 2024) both apply. Testimonials must be genuine, must reflect typical results or carry a clear atypical-results disclosure, and any material connection (free access, payment, affiliate relationship) must be disclosed clearly and near the claim. Avoid income, return, and retirement-date promises in ads. "Retire at 55" as a lesson title is fine; as an advertised outcome it is a different exposure.

**7e. Entity, and who is contracting.** These drafts assume a business entity. If the course is sold personally rather than through an LLC or corporation, tell counsel, because the liability sections read very differently.

**7f. Privacy.** Circle and your payment processor both collect member data. Confirm your existing Privacy Policy actually covers course and community data, or extend it. Ask about state privacy laws that may apply based on where your members live.

**7g. Sales tax / VAT on digital products.** Digital course sales can create tax obligations in various states and countries. This is a real operational question, not a footnote.

## 8. WHERE EACH PIECE GOES IN CIRCLE

Circle has one built-in legal hook and several places you add pages yourself. Use both.

### 8a. The built-in setting (do this first)

**Settings → Legal** (admin left-hand menu, scroll down to "Legal"). Circle lets you link **custom terms of service and a custom privacy policy** here, so members must agree to *your* terms alongside Circle's when they create an account.

- Custom Terms of Service → link your **Course Terms** page URL
- Custom Privacy Policy → link your **Privacy Policy** URL

This is the only place Circle enforces agreement at signup. It is the single highest-value item in this list.

### 8b. Create the legal pages themselves

Make each of these its own page in Circle, in a space set to be **visible to everyone including logged-out visitors** so you can link them from your sales page and checkout:

| Page title | Content from this file |
|---|---|
| Course Terms | Section 4 |
| Disclaimer | Section 3 |
| Community Guidelines | Section 5 |
| Privacy Policy | Your existing policy, extended per 7f |

Put them in a space named something like **"Legal"** or **"Start Here"**, and set that space so posting is admin-only.

### 8c. Sidebar links

Circle supports **custom links in the community sidebar**. Add a small group at the bottom of the sidebar labeled "Legal" with links to all four pages. This is what makes them findable without cluttering navigation.

### 8d. The rest, mapped

| Copy | Where it goes in Circle |
|---|---|
| **Short disclaimer** (§1) | Course home page, top of the first module, and the footer of your email broadcasts |
| **One-liner** (§2) | Every video description; footer of each toolkit PDF |

| **Renewal reminder email** (§6c) | Your email tool, automated 15–45 days pre-renewal |
| **On-screen video disclaimer** (§6d) | Burned into the video files at edit time |

### 8e. Important: checkout is probably not Circle

If you sell through Circle's built-in paywall, the consent checkbox and auto-renewal disclosure need to appear in that flow. If you sell through an external checkout (Stripe, ThriveCart, Kajabi, a landing page), **that** is where sections 6a and 6b must live, because the legal requirement attaches at the point where billing information is collected, not afterward. Confirm which one you are using and make sure the disclosure appears **before** the payment fields, not on the confirmation page.

Also note that Circle's own **Creator Terms of Service** bind you as the community operator. Read them, because they set out what Circle requires of you toward your members.

### 8f. If the course lives on Honen instead

The same four documents apply. Put them wherever Honen exposes static pages or a course-description area, link them from the first lesson, and make sure the purchase flow shows the consent checkbox and any renewal disclosure. The Circle-specific instruction that does not transfer is 8a, since that setting is Circle's.
